# Opening the exact board

Open `project/04_kicad/crow_mic_pod_v3.kicad_pro` for the original relative custom model/library hierarchy. The root `.kicad_pcb` is byte-identical and included for conventional release tooling; its adjacent footprint table resolves local footprints under `project/03_src/lib`. Use a matching KiCad 10 installation with its standard footprint/3D libraries. Custom official models are shipped under `project/03_src/lib/3dmodels`.

Historical investigation/reviewer packet archives are retained in the repository at the manifest source commit; they are not repeated in this manufacturing package. The active native-terminal authority archive cited by twin_adjudications.yaml is included. Current source, primary dossiers, rules, exact review reports and manufacturing evidence are shipped.
