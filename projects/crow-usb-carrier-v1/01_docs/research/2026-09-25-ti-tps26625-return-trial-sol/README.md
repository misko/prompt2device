# TPS26625 channel-8 partial return-route trial

This is **one improved source-generated six-member placement**, followed by one bounded native F.Cu return-route attempt. It remains research only. The retained project engineering ceilings apply to the IN bypass supply contact and ILIM contact at **2.5 mm direct pad-copper gap**; TI does not specify a numeric placement gap for the other contacts.

Relative to the frozen TI integrated/coupled baseline, the six source poses are `U_SPOKE8` (190.00,47.50,0°), `C_SPOKE_IN8` (189.75,44.00,0°), `C_SPOKE_OUT8` (194.60,46.70,0°), `R_SPOKE_ILIM8` (191.00,51.00,180°), `C_SPOKE_DVDT8` (193.90,49.30,180°), and `R_SPOKE_UVLO8` (186.50,46.80,0°). All six stay in their `analog_ch8` source owner with no foreign-region or native full-envelope collision. The 569 footprint/pad identities and 27 fixed refs are preserved; every other footprint pose is unchanged. The source-generated unfilled board SHA-256 is `944806e1759e73f468076caf053a42ab0abb76a6736dcf3ef75c4fca27c7ebea`.

The existing project ceilings pass: `U_SPOKE8.1`→`C_SPOKE_IN8.1` is **1.655273 mm** and `U_SPOKE8.7`→`R_SPOKE_ILIM8.1` is **2.405089 mm**. Compared with the preceding group pose, moving/rotating `R_SPOKE_ILIM8` reduces its `U.5` RTN-side direct gap from 5.563658 to **2.185362 mm**; moving `C_SPOKE_DVDT8` reduces its signal-side gap from 5.619506 to **2.838134 mm** and puts its RTN pad **2.352905 mm** from PowerPAD. Native exact pad-shape gaps for all 12 named pairings are in `receipt.json`. The remaining direct gaps include `U.6`→C(IN).2 **3.661193 mm**, `U.6`→C(OUT).2 **3.563231 mm**, and `U.5`→C(dVdT).2 **4.277343 mm**. These are geometric gaps, not inferred routing failures or new numeric requirements.

The saved board routes both bypass **GND returns** to `U_SPOKE8.6`: C(IN).2 path 7.400000 mm and C(OUT).2 path 4.850000 mm, using 0.20 mm F.Cu tracks and a 0.60/0.30 mm GND stitch at (195.55,48.50). Their vertices, the GND pin, and the stitch lie in one filled In1.Cu GND polygon. Separate 0.20 mm F.Cu `SPOKE_RTN8` tracks join `R_SPOKE_ILIM8.2` (3.572487 mm), `C_SPOKE_DVDT8.2` (3.420000 mm), `U_SPOKE8.3` (1.400000 mm), and `U_SPOKE8.5` (1.814214 mm) to PowerPAD.11. Native connectivity confirms both support GND pads with U.6 and both control returns with U.3/.5/.11, with no GND/RTN cross-connection. This is a connected local RTN track network, **not a qualified copper/thermal island**.

The supply/output and ILIM/dVdT/UVLO signal legs are still unrouted, so none of the five TPS26625 loops is closed and loop area is **undefined**, not zero. High-current path width, local thermal copper/vias, loop area, and full P2 return evidence remain debts. The previously reviewed 0.20 mm ADC8N link remains connected with its continuous filled In1 reference, 0.200001 mm USB-region margin and 0.205001 mm nearest nonendpoint envelope margin. Full archived `.kicad_pro`/`.kicad_dru`/POFV/V-PROCESS replay yields **199 violations and 499 opens** for both baseline and trial, with +0/−0 DRC issue identities and no V-PROCESS failures. The unchanged open count is a branched-net report and does not close the missing TPS26625 legs or wider ADC8N net.

Reproduce with `python3 probe.py`; the pinned frozen TI packet must exist at the path in the script. The committed filled native board is the sole board artifact. Its byte SHA is observational because generated POFV UUIDs drift; the script checks native semantic identities and issue-set delta. No canonical source, formal P1, or P2 promotion follows.
