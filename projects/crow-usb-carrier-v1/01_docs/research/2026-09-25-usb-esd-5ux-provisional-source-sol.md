# Provisional 5-V-standoff USB data ESD source candidate, 2026-09-25

The current UV selection (PESD2USB3UV-TR/C3704436) left its 3.3-V reverse standoff below Crow's 3.394-V worst-case N3V3X DC setpoint and the conservative 3.60-V XU316 USB at-pin ceiling. The proposed one-part replacement is Nexperia PESD2USB5UX-TR/C3709087. Its 5.0-V reverse standoff exceeds that ceiling, so the specific steady-state standoff headroom concern is closed at the supply-bound screen. The cathodes protect USB_DP/USB_DN, not the supply directly; this comparison does not establish actual line waveforms or transient pin survival. This is a provisional source candidate, not accepted electrical protection or a release.

The official [Nexperia PESD2USB5UX-T datasheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/PESD2USB5UX-T.pdf) is retained at `02_parts/PESD2USB5UX-TR/PESD2USB5UX-T.pdf`, SHA-256 `6e86f26606d8dd9bd563f172b68f5db53f8ee1d9b7c1105a88598d9d7344b112`, accessed 2026-09-25. Table 2 gives pins 1/2 as protected cathodes and pin 3 as common-anode GND; Tables 1, 5 and 6 give 5.0-V VRWM, 0.47-pF typical/0.60-pF maximum capacitance, 7.2-11-V VBR, 8-kV IEC contact and 4-A 8/20-us surge. It explicitly lists USB2.0. Versus 3UV, the capacitance and standoff improve, but breakdown rises from 4.2-8 V, IEC contact falls from 18 kV and surge falls from 8 A. The 3.3-V typical TLP clamp at 8 A is a test-condition result, not a guaranteed XU316 powered/unpowered limit. XU transient coordination and system ESD remain open.

Figures 13/14 in the 5UX and 3UV manufacturer PDFs show the same SOT23/TO-236AB package maxima and 3.3 x 3.0-mm occupied/reflow pattern. The 5UX-specific native footprint is an exact geometry copy of the UV land source, with 5UX provenance: pins 1/2 on the paired side, pin 3 isolated, matching Table 2 and the rotated Figure 13 pin arrangement. The separate project-authored 5UX VRML is the same conservative 3.0 x 1.4 x 1.1-mm maximum body and lead envelope as UV, with the 5UX Figure 13/PDF hash in its header; it is not supplier CAD and omits measured mould draft and bent-lead detail.
