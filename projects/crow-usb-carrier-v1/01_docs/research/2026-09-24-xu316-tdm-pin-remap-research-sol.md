# Crow XU316 TDM edge-remap review (read-only, HEAD 4a9cb6b1)

**Finding:** the *current* frozen pin plan has two physical escapes. MCLK/BCLK/FSYNC leave U_XU on one TQ128 edge at pads 23/22/20, while DATA enters pad 107 on a different edge. An all-four-signals one-edge plan is **plausible at the silicon port level**, because currently unconnected pad 19 (X1D00/P1A0) is an independent tile-1 one-bit port adjacent to pads 20–23. It is not authorized by the present schematic, XN/firmware, or a native corridor measurement. It cannot be implemented by floorplan relocation alone or claimed to work without firmware authoring/review.

## Exact identity and constraint

The retained XMOS [XU316-1024-TQ128 datasheet v2.0.0](https://www.xmos.com/documentation/XM-014532-PC/html/rst/XU316-1024-TQ128.html), locally pinned as `02_parts/XU316-1024-TQ128-C24/XM-014532-PC-v2.0.0.pdf` (SHA-256 `a2ce2dc835df06793a4e1aa6c5d226c6a01c25c979a63b09a2b57059c17321cf`), Table 1/Pin Configuration, identifies pad 19 X1D00/P1A0 (IOL), pad 20 X1D01/P1B0 (IOL), pad 22 X1D10/P1C0 (IOL), pad 23 X1D11/P1D0 (IOL), and pad 107 X1D24/P1I0 (IOT). The datasheet application-clock section says the secondary-PLL/application-clock output is routed specifically to X1D11/P1D0; MCLK should not be casually reassigned to a different pin. The [XMOS clock-control note](https://www.xmos.com/documentation/XM-014200-AN/html/doc/rst/an02022.html) supports that fixed mux boundary.

Crow `03_tscircuit/src/crow_usb_digital.tsx` connects FSYNC to 20, BCLK to 22, MCLK to 23 and DATA to 107. It labels pin 19 `X1D00_NC`; the source netlist and Terra's exact isolated board `ce589f8a…` agree. On that board the centres are pad 19 (209.500,107.6625), 20 (209.900,107.6625), 21 (210.300,107.6625, NC), 22 (210.700,107.6625), 23 (211.100,107.6625), and 107 (200.8375,97.800) mm. Thus pad 19 is an actual same-edge hardware candidate, but the proposed four-pin group still has a pad-21 gap and needs a fresh breakout/courtyard/decoupler screen. The present four-endpoint `tdm_to_xmos` source contract explicitly names U_XU.107, not .19.

XMOS [lib_i2s v6.0.1](https://www.xmos.com/documentation/XM-007055-UG/html/doc/rst/lib_i2s.html), “Connecting I²S signals” and “TDM connection”, allows the TDM signals on any non-overlapping one-bit ports **on the same tile**, subject to port/resource and timing constraints. X1D00/P1A0 meets the package-level tile/one-bit test; whether the selected Crow USB-audio application (`lib_xua`, XN, clock blocks, threads, and full port ownership) can use it has not been compiled or demonstrated. The [XMOS USB Audio I²S/TDM guide](https://www.xmos.com/documentation/XM-012296-UG/html/doc/rst/opt_i2s.html) says required port definitions come from the application XN file. The retained Crow XU hardware research already states that no vendor precompiled image targets this pinout and a board-specific XN/firmware build is owed.

## Decision boundary

Keep the current two escapes unless a separate schematic/firmware change is admitted. For a same-edge alternative, first inventory every tile-1 port and clock-block user in the exact USB-audio application; change DATA from X1D24/P1I0 to X1D00/P1A0 in source, XN and firmware; confirm pin-19 IOL voltage/power state, TDM input timing and lib_xua resource legality with an actual build and later bench timing. Then regenerate netlist/native board, update the endpoint/corridor contract and IC semantic bindings, and measure the translator-to-XU lane, foreign decouplers (C_XU_VDD_104/105/106/109), filled return and native DRC. Without those changes, a layout-only one-edge claim would silently route DATA to an NC pin while firmware still reads pad 107.

No firmware support, complete route, SI result or P1 acceptance is claimed here; no source or board was edited.
