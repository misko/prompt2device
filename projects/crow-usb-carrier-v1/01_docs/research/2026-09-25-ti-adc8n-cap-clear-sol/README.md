# ADC8N own-cap clearance: bounded north-escape screen

**Result:** one north-of-cap F.Cu route clears both the moved `C_ADC_AC8N1` full native envelope **after its unavoidable named pad launch** and `usb_vbus_sense=[195,62,220,82]` mm. The [reproducer](north_probe.py), [filled-profile board](candidate_filled_profile.kicad_pcb), [native DRC JSON](candidate_drc.json), and [receipt](receipt.json) are research only. This is not P1/P2 or source placement acceptance: it is 4.837 mm longer than the first 15.180-mm local route and passes beneath another same-owner IC body, `U_SPOKE8`. The 36-member channel-8 owner/J8 obstruction and full four-terminal ADC8N tree remain.

The exact southwest-cap TI source board retains `C_ADC_AC8N1` at `(198.00,60.05)` mm with full checker envelope `[195.205,58.305,200.795,61.795]` mm. Its pad 2 at `(199.80,60.05)` is inside that envelope, so literal zero cap-envelope contact from the pad is impossible. The first segment from this pad to `(199.80,58.05)` is the sole named launch exemption. Every subsequent segment's **full 0.20-mm F.Cu copper shape** stays at least **0.055001 mm** clear of the cap envelope and **0.147488 mm** clear of the USB rectangle. The route then follows `(195.05,58.05)→(195.05,61.6)→(190.75,65.9)` toward `C_ADC_CM8N.1`; all vertices are in the receipt. The west passage at x=195.05 lies between the cap and `C_A8P` full envelopes with limited physical room; these clearances are measurement, not a manufacturing allowance.

The long `(195.05,61.6)→(190.75,65.9)` segment crosses `U_SPOKE8`'s full envelope `[187.805,60.805,192.195,65.195]` **and body** `[187.825,60.825,192.175,65.175]`. No U_SPOKE8 copper pad or DRC rule is violated, but passing an analog ADC8N signal under that switching support IC is not accepted for noise, field coupling, service, or layout intent by this geometry screen. The only other footprint-envelope intersections are the named launch cap and receiving cap. This is the main reason the north route remains a diagnostic, even though it solves the two requested cap/USB intersections.

That move would shrink the gap to `C_FILTER8N1` from 0.780 to 0.580 mm and change the cap's related-pad distances. It is **not implemented or admitted** here; full 569-ref pose/pad, fixed-ref, support-part envelope, locality, profile DRC, return, and source-owner checks are prerequisites.
