# TMUX4827 non-BGA substitute screen — 2026-09-24

**Disposition: no public-stock-admissible replacement found.** This is a public catalog and manufacturer electrical screen, not a schematic substitution, package land acceptance, PCBA allocation or board release.

Crow uses eight `TMUX4827YBHR` dual-SPDT switches (`U_ISO1`–`U_ISO8`) powered by held `N5V_LDO_HOLD`. A replacement must preserve two independently selected SPDT paths per channel, analog signals at least −12 to +12 V despite a 5 V supply, powered-off isolation across that signal range, and comparable low resistance/distortion. Ordinary sourcing policy requires stock for five boards plus 150 units on **each aggregated exact LCSC line**: 8/board requires **190**, while a two-single-SPDT or two-dual-SPST redesign at 16/board requires **230**. [TI's TMUX4827 primary product page](https://www.ti.com/product/TMUX4827) and the Crow exact-part dossier bind these electrical requirements; TI lists the exact orderable as [TMUX4827YBHR, 9-ball YBH DSBGA](https://www.ti.com/product/TMUX4827/part-details/TMUX4827YBHR). Its `.A` order suffix remains YBH, not a second package.

I queried the public [JLCsearch component endpoint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tscircuit/docs/main/docs/web-apis/jlcsearch-api.md) on 2026-09-24 at 15:29 UTC. Exact HTTP-200 response bodies and their byte counts/SHA-256 are retained in [2026-09-24-tmux4827-substitute-public](2026-09-24-tmux4827-substitute-public/manifest.json). JLCsearch is a public **derivative** database with unknown upstream inventory timestamp; these observations are not reserved stock. Its TMUX4827 row reports **C22428234, DSBGA-9, 234** (190 threshold), whereas the project's separately retained direct public-catalog observation at 00:45 UTC reported 5,437. Both clear the threshold but the discrepancy requires order-time verification; neither removes the BGA via-in-pad problem.
