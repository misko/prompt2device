# Superseding Crow ADC hardware/software interface, 2026-09-23

This candidate contract supersedes the **ADC-specific** portions of `2026-09-22-hardware-software-interface.md` if the two-TLV source candidate is adopted. The prior document remains dated history. USB host/device, XMOS boot flash, USB pins, VBUS sense, JTAG, oscillator input at XU physical pin 23, and all XU rail-bank assignments remain as in that document. This file defines hardware resources and future initialization behavior; it does not authorize or contain firmware implementation.

## Pins, source and frame

The external `Y_AUDIO` 24.576 MHz oscillator remains the XU audio reference. XU physical pin 22 supplies `TDM_BCLK_1V8` → translated/quiet-buffered `ADC_BCLK` at 12.288 MHz; pin 20 supplies `TDM_FSYNC_1V8` → `ADC_FSYNC` at 48 kHz; pin 107 receives the shared `ADC_DOUT1` through the existing 3.3 V→1.8 V TDM translator. Both TLV320ADC6140IRTWT devices use those same clock/frame nets and common `ADC_RESET_N` on SHDNZ. TI [SBAA383C](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/sbaa383c/sbaa383c.pdf) documents multiple TLV320ADCx140 devices on one I2C/TDM bus. The ADCs run their PLLs from the shared BCLK; separate die PLLs have no published absolute cross-die aperture-skew maximum, so common-frame operation is supported but exact phase equality is not claimed.

The former CS5308P ADC MCLK line, falling-BCLK/falling-MCLK phase rule, CONFIG straps and CS one-bit slot offset are **obsolete**. The oscillator remains at XU pin 23 and XU-derived BCLK/FSYNC remain shared. TLV GPIO1 pin 20 is unconnected: its reset default is an IRQ output, so a direct externally driven MCLK connection would risk startup contention. The existing two-flop/OR FSYNC stretcher remains provisionally in the source. Its leading edge tracks the raw FSYNC while its trailing edge is extended about two BCLK periods. This is supported only as a hardware candidate; confirm the actual ADC-pin pulse meets TI FSYNC setup/width and that the XU capture sees slot zero at the intended edge. The retained clock-enable supervisor qualifies quiet 3V3_ADC and 1V8/3V3X before driving the ADC bus.

A single frame is **8 × 32-bit slots = 256 BCLKs**, leaving 24 meaningful sample bits per slot at 48 kHz. Use TI TDM mode, 32-bit ASI word length, `TX_OFFSET=0` on both ADCs, default FSYNC/BCLK polarity and `TX_FILL=1` on both. At 12.288 MHz, the bus is below TI's 25 MHz maximum and its >18.5 MHz special opposite-edge capture condition. ADC A channels 1–4 transmit slots 0–3 from **Pods 4,3,2,1** through the inherited `POD_TO_ADC` permutation. ADC B channels 1–4 transmit slots 4–7 from Pods 5–8. Set both slot maps while output channels remain disabled; program both `TX_FILL=1` **before** enabling either ASI output, since reset default `TX_FILL=0` drives zeros in unused slots and would contend on shared SDOUT. Host sampling edge/slot alignment and shared-line turn-around remain first-article checks. TI's SBAA383C shared-bus example uses P0_R7=0x31, P0_R11–14 slot indices, P0_R115 channel enable, P0_R116 ASI output enable, and P0_R117 ADC/PLL enable; those registers are a configuration contract, not a board firmware image.

## I2C and analog mode

XU physical 93 (`X0D35`, tile 0 P1L0) is software-defined open-drain SCL; physical 94 (`X0D36`, tile 0 P1M0) is SDA. XMOS XM-014532-PC v2.0.0 confirms both are 1.8 V IOR bank pins and uncommitted by USB/TDM. Exact stocked TI TCA9406DCUR translates bidirectionally: VCCA=1V8, VCCB=3V3_ADC, OE=ADC_READY, which has an existing 100 kΩ pulldown. TI SCPS221G qualifies OE to 5.5 V and high-impedance buses when OE is low or either supply is off. Internal 10 kΩ pullups plus four external 4.7 kΩ pullups define the bus. TLV A address straps 0/0 → 7-bit 0x4C; B 0/1 → 0x4D. Do not enable I2C transactions before shared SHDNZ is released and TI's ≥2 ms post-release register-init delay has elapsed. Hold XU BCLK/FSYNC inactive through the preconfiguration interval so the published TLV sleep-current condition with external clocks stopped applies until wake; after P0_R2=0x99, wait ≥1 ms, write/read back fast SHDNZ_CFG=00 before enabling the clocks, channel power or ASI outputs. TI §8.4.2 permits only the sleep-exit P0_R2 transaction while asleep; P0_R5 cannot be prewritten in sleep. Confirm clock-independent I2C wake/configuration on the first article.

AVDD=IOVDD=quiet 3V3_ADC, internal AREG selected on wake, differential AC input, 2.5 kΩ typical **per input pin**, 2 Vrms differential full-scale, DRE/AGC off, 0 dB channel gain are the initial fixed-gain contract. These retain 4.44 dB full-scale margin for the 1.2 Vrms pod maximum. Use linear-phase decimation and set `DSP_CFG0 HPF_SEL=0` (all-pass): TLV reset default is a 12 Hz digital high-pass at 48 kHz, which would alter the low-frequency array response. Dynamic DRE mode is an alternative for later qualification, not the default fixed-gain source candidate. Both devices must receive identical processing/phase configuration and readback. The 32 AC coupling caps isolate the retained 1.65 V OPA common mode from TLV internal input bias. The 10 kΩ pulldowns sit **before** the caps at TMUX outputs; the ADC inputs must not be externally pulled to ground.

## Startup, shutdown and mute

Hardware retains the 3V3_ADC POR, qualified digital-rail supervisors, ADC_READY and delayed low reset pulse. The old report's numerical high/low reset intervals were calculated for CS5308P and are **not TLV timing proof**. For TLV, SHDNZ must be low while AVDD/IOVDD rise and release only after both are within 3.0–3.6 V and clocks/digital rail are valid. Existing `ADC_RESET_N` asserts low on POR or digital-bad and then emits a delayed low pulse; its assertion/release sequencing is structurally compatible but must be measured against TI timing at first article. TI requires rail ramp slower than 1 V/µs, ≥100 ms between true power cycles, and ≥2 ms after SHDNZ release or software reset before further I2C. Wake with **P0_R2=0x99** at 3.3 V: internal AREG_SELECT=1, VREF_QCHG=3 (100 ms planning window), and SLEEP_ENZ=1. This is the permitted sleep-exit transaction; setting VREF_QCHG only after an earlier 0x81 wake would miss the initial quick-charge window. Permit at least 1 ms and check device state before configuration and capture.

The new held-domain inverter `U_ADC_PWR_BAD` and NMOS `Q_ADC_PWR_RST` assert common SHDNZ low as soon as `PWR_EN` falls, ahead of the delayed `Q_DUMP` and `LDO_EN` transitions. Program P0_R5 `SHDNZ_CFG=00` for immediate hardware-shutdown mode **before unmuting capture**. TI otherwise specifies ≥10 ms t3/t4 power-down interval in its default clean shutdown; immediate mode reduces it to ≥100 µs. The source holds fourteen 470 µF capacitors on 5V_LDO_HOLD and delays the deliberate dump with eight 15 nF C0G capacitors and 150 kΩ equivalent resistance (100 kΩ in series with two parallel 100 kΩ). The 200 Ω 3V3_ADC bleed remains, preserving the inherited eventual discharge behavior. During a warm reset from active mode, TI gives a typical ~25 ms transition into hardware shutdown and then requires SHDNZ low ≥1 ms after the device has entered shutdown before re-release; a simple low pulse of the old CS length is not by itself a guaranteed state check. If the fast mode is lost at power cycle, the state returns to default until reprogrammed. The source-stage timing screen and its limits are below; actual AVDD/IOVDD/SHDNZ/dump waveforms remain first-article gates.

For **preconfiguration sleep** the default ≥10 ms is the conservative TI requirement because no sleep exception is published. The accepted upstream 4.91 V minimum less the existing 0.81 V diode/FET/feed allocation sets a **4.10 V held-bank start**, not 4.91 V. The shutdown terminal is a separate condition: TI ADC minimum AVDD/IOVDD is 3.0 V, and ADI LT3045 Rev D specifies ≤350 mV dropout even at 300 mA output, giving a conservative 3.35 V input floor. Fourteen Panasonic 470 µF parts, with an explicitly **engineering** 0.54 retained-capacitance factor for tolerance/temperature/age, give 3.553 mF. A 70 mA aggregate held-bank envelope gives 33.5 ms from 4.10 V to 3.33 V by CΔV/I, and 19.8 ms to the full-regulation 3.71 V floor for 3.38 V output. Both exceed the 10 ms default powered interval; the later 3.0 V terminal relies on the conservative 300 mA dropout row, not the normal 0.25 A rail allocation. The 70 mA envelope must include the OPA maximum quiescent 27.2 mA, 200 Ω bleed up to 16.9 mA, VMID dividers 3.38 mA, eight TMUX maximum 1.12 mA, LT3045 ground-current 7 mA reserve, and a remaining 14.4 mA for ADC sleep with clocks, supervisors/Schmitts, pullups and OPA dynamic output; it is a **candidate engineering cap**, not a manufacturer's global maximum. The delayed Schmitt timer has nominal 18.0 ms RC, with an reviewer-screened 14.8–29.4 ms trigger span at 70 mA held load; rerunning the same leakage/C/R/VT− model at 120 mA gives about 15.7–33.4 ms; its exact dynamic timing, Q_DUMP drive and LDO shutoff still require a first-article waveform screen. The enlarged bank also changes 22 Ω precharge time/energy and board placement; the source must be reviewed for startup, inrush, R_PRE pulse energy and thermal before adoption.

The 0x99 wake selects the 100 ms VREF quick-charge window instead of the default 3.5 ms for the source's 10 µF VREF caps; TI describes about 200 Ω charge resistance and the nominal RC is about 2 ms even before tolerance. `INCAP_QCHG` can be set to TI's 50 ms option for large input caps, but TI does not publish a charge-current maximum guaranteeing the 94 µF nameplate bank settles within 50 ms. Keep host capture muted and exclude ADC samples from the stream until both references, PLL/status and each analog input have actually settled; a measured startup allowance is required for the implemented board. On any reset, rail loss or USB recovery, remute, reapply the configuration to both dies, and resume only with an aligned FSYNC frame. No firmware has been authored or built here.

## Power and evidence

At 3.3 V, four channels, 48 kHz, PLL on, DRE off, BCLK=256fS, TI SBAS992A lists **21.3 mA AVDD + 0.1 mA IOVDD typical per TLV**, hence 42.8 mA typical for two. TI lists 23.6+0.1 mA per TLV with DRE on. Sixteen retained OPA2320 channels consume ~23.2 mA typical (1.45 mA/channel; TI SBOS513F), two 1 kΩ/1 kΩ VMID dividers draw about 3.3 mA, and the retained 200 Ω OPA bleed draws 16.5 mA at 3.3 V. Those identifiable loads total ~85.8 mA typical DRE off, before supervisors, buffers and transients, versus the 0.25 A LT3045 rail allocation. This leaves ~164.2 mA planning headroom; it is **not** a guaranteed worst-case current proof because TI publishes operating-mode ADC typical current, not a maximum in this table. The candidate retains the 0.25 A allocation until a full worst-case source/thermal screen closes it.

Primary files are `02_parts/TLV320ADC6140IRTWT/` (TI SBAS992A, SBAA383C, RTW0024A) and `02_parts/TCA9406DCUR/` (TI SCPS221G), with exact source pinmaps in TSX and native footprint dossiers. Public JLC snapshots pass five boards plus 150 extra for C1852023 (188 vs160), C840107 (16,905 vs155), and C84494 (56,473 vs360), but are not allocated assembly stock. The physical board, solder/placement, clock edge timing, cross-die phase and analog settle remain unqualified by this source contract.
