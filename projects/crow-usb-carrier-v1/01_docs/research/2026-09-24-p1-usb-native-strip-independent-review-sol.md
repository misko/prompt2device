# Independent review: isolated USB strip and P1 capacity boundary

Verdict: **diagnostic geometry only; P1 remains INCOMPLETE**. I inspected the isolated native board and `p1_corridor_capacity.py` without changing either. This review concerns the ESD-to-XU USB strip; it does not replace the J_USB-to-ESD entry, the other three P1 allocations, connector FULL, or route/DRC proof.

## Bound observation

The isolated board is `/tmp/crow-p1-sublane-native-20260924-terra-3/projects/crow-usb-carrier-v1/04_kicad/crow_carrier.kicad_pcb`, SHA-256 `ce589f8a4889854946ef9a1c234ed57de89bfbf220e60f72cd6d818734d8813e`. Its copied floorplan and netlist byte hashes match the current source (`1eb9fc598a40094716413ef6996bfcf2efe3dd021eff15cd452e670aa24fffdb` and `f3b4e039a695eeec34843174e9430d4bb5c65c4773ed3fa2a9998a18a0654d0e`). There is no canonical project board in `04_kicad` to bind this observation to.

Native pad identity/centres agree with the Terra measurement: U_USB_ESD.1 USB_DP (216.650, 36.425), .2 USB_DN (217.350, 36.425), U_XU.60 USB_DP (216.1625, 95.400), and .59 USB_DN (216.1625, 95.800), all millimetres. Terra's candidate x=216.10..217.40, y=36.75..91.30 is a 1.30-mm F.Cu *screening* strip, wider than the source's 0.97-mm pair demand; its reported absence of foreign F.Cu body/pad intersections is useful but omits edge clearance, courtyard and pad-access envelopes. The ESD native footprint bbox is [215.900,35.275,217.825,36.770], so the candidate strip overlaps its own body by 0.020 mm at the north end. U_XU's body bbox is [199.825,91.325,217.175,108.675], separated from the strip's south end by 0.025 mm. These are endpoint geometry facts, not passable pocket geometry. The rejected single TDM reservation is separate: the XU-side data/clock exits do not share that lane, with decouplers C_XU_VDD_104/105/106/109 blocking the proposed split rectangle; it cannot be credited by the clear USB strip.

The saved board contains one GND zone on In1.Cu, but `IsFilled()` is false and its filled polygon count is zero. A read-only in-memory `pcbnew.ZONE_FILLER(board).Fill(board.Zones())` succeeded and produced nine filled polygons. Twelve *point samples* at x=216.10/216.75/217.40 and y=36.75/50/70/91.30 fell inside the resulting In1 GND fill. This establishes that a native fill measurement is technically possible on an isolated copy. It does **not** establish continuous return under every possible pair path, stitching/current-return geometry, or a hash-bound saved result: the fill was never saved, and sparse samples can miss a void or neck.

## Why the current capacity checker cannot admit this strip

`p1_corridor_capacity.py` (SHA-256 `dac1e5497ccebbb07fef3fb67154a8bbdf768f252ae3eb5b87db87a8e62c3f4b`) accepts a separate JSON schema-1 contract, a bound board SHA, and an externally expected contract SHA. The existing P1 diagnostic is YAML schema 2 with null geometry, not that contract. Its `crow-p1` profile demands the four named signal allocations plus exact-once coverage of all 59 interface nets, including the nine power-boundary nets; a USB-only strip therefore cannot be P1-complete.

For each rectangular pocket the checker requires the *entire endpoint footprint bbox* and pad bbox inside it, pocket touching but not intersecting the lane. With the present ESD bbox crossing y=36.75, no ESD pocket containing the full footprint can touch the north end of this exact strip without intersecting it. A pocket containing the full XU body is very large and is not checked for foreign parts or available pad launches. Worse, `layer_obstacles()` omits every pad/body of an endpoint reference from the lane, including XU pads 61–64 and ESD pad 3; the screen cannot certify their clearance or pair access. It uses bbox obstacles without clearance expansion, courtyards, effective per-net width/gap, or native rule precedence. A raw slot count is explicitly optimistic and remains `INCOMPLETE` even when numerically sufficient.

The existing whole-board In1 GND zone bbox spans [20,20,240,140]. The checker treats *any* zone bbox overlap with a lane as unresolved pour overlap, and its `crow-p1` profile explicitly returns `reference-plane/pour continuity not measured`. Refilling a copied board therefore can supply **separate native return evidence**, but cannot make this checker grade the filled reference plane or turn its result into `PASS`. Its rule-area check likewise compares name/bbox/layers rather than the effective rule geometry. These are fail-closed limits, not reasons to claim capacity.

## Minimum next evidence

1. Freeze a fresh isolated candidate and all sidecars: source floorplan, netlist, board, effective `.kicad_pro`/`.kicad_dru` rules and footprint libraries, with hashes. Author actual native/source-declared USB lane and **separate, pad-specific** J_USB-to-ESD and ESD-to-XU entry/exit pocket geometry. Move the strip edge or endpoint boundary so the ESD pocket is disjoint from the through lane; check every foreign and same-footprint non-target pad with native clearance envelopes. Bind an independently expected JSON contract hash to that exact saved board. Keep the full 59-net P1 denominator visibly INCOMPLETE while testing only the USB sub-lane.
2. On an isolated copy, refill In1 GND, save the refilled board, hash it, and evaluate filled-polygon coverage continuously under the proposed path/envelope, including voids, necks and layer transitions. Tie that report to the post-save board SHA and zone/net/layer identity. Point samples alone are a screening result. Native DRC on the same saved board/rule sidecars must identify any local pad/track/zone/rule-area violations; clearance and differential-pair width/gap need the effective native per-net rules.
3. Only after all four named allocations, the nine power windows, exact pad/net endpoint pockets, native outline/rule areas, reference planes, source-to-native parity and P1-class DRC are measured on one frozen candidate should an independent P1 admission consider the board. This note grants no route, connector FULL or P1 acceptance credit.
