# Crow TMUX4827 package and public-stock alternative screen

**2026-09-24 15:29 UTC; public-only research.** No source or board change. The Crow design uses eight `U_ISO1`–`U_ISO8` TMUX4827YBHR instances on `N5V_LDO_HOLD`, with ±12 V analog-path and power-off protection requirements. The ordinary five-board +150 public-stock threshold for a single shared LCSC line at 8/board is **190** (40 needed + 150 reserve).

| TMUX6136 | Dual SPDT in larger packages, but requires high/dual supply rather than the held 5 V rail and has materially higher Ron | 0 JLCsearch rows | Not pin-compatible and does not preserve the power topology; no stock basis. |

**Decision:** No source-ready package or pin-compatible replacement was found that retains dual SPDT, held 5 V supply, ±12 V path, low distortion and powered-off protection while removing YBH BGA via-in-pad and meeting the public-stock rule. TMUX4819DSGR is the only credible package-level direction found; it is a *two-for-one redesign with zero JLCsearch catalog evidence*, not an admitted substitution. Continue the current exact TMUX4827 process qualification or initiate a separate sourced redesign.
