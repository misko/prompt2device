# ADC8N local route and filled In1 return: isolated TI research

**Result:** on the exact source-generated southwest-cap TI integrated board, a native F.Cu connection from `C_ADC_AC8N1.2` to its nearest same-net endpoint `C_ADC_CM8N.1` is DRC-clean under the complete TI project profile. A filled In1.Cu GND polygon continuously covers a 0.20-mm ribbon beneath the entire candidate trace, and two ordinary GND stitches join that polygon to named F.Cu GND pads near the launch and receiver. This establishes only a local research route/return geometry. The four-pad ADC8N tree, source owner regions, complete ADC analog boundary, connector qualification, and P1/P2 remain unresolved.

The [probe](route_probe.py) pins the input board SHA-256 `0b9d017706845ad4d77c2b579dd36f2e34c0ecf55a7b699ace4fca97465c5b93` and the frozen TI [project/rule profile](profile), assembly, and POFV producer hashes in [receipt.json](receipt.json). The board has 569 footprints. The replay compares filled baseline and candidate using the same complete profile: eight generated B2 POFV rule areas, `V-PROCESS`, `kicad-cli pcb drc --refill-zones --save-board`, and actual filled copper. It preserves all original footprint poses and pad number/net/layer/shape/position identities, including all 27 P1-fixed refs. Only six ADC8N track segments, two ordinary GND vias, and their F.Cu GND necks are added to the [candidate board](candidate_filled_profile.kicad_pcb). Its input-before-profile form is [also retained](candidate_unfilled.kicad_pcb). The route and receipt are on independent research branch `codex/crow-adc8n-local-route-sol`; no canonical source or board was edited.

The 499-open total needs careful interpretation. The baseline and candidate DRC unconnected lists contain **no ADC8N pair**; therefore that report's total cannot show the new ADC8N link. Its 499 entries have ten changed pair identities each way after adding GND copper, reflecting KiCad's recomputed missing-edge selections, not ten new/removed physical net obligations. Native connectivity independently confirms the two exact ADC8N pads are joined. The two remaining ADC8N pads, `C_ADC_AC8N2.2` and `U_ADC_B.13`, are not connected by this local trace; the complete four-terminal ADC8N tree is still owed. The cause of KiCad's omission of ADC8N from this DRC report is not established, so the unchanged count is not treated as complete-net evidence. Both full DRC JSONs are retained for audit.

The local route does **not** establish a source-owned corridor. Its diagonal centerline enters the present `usb_vbus_sense=[195,62,220,82]` planning region between `(197.8,62.0)` and `(195.0,64.8)` mm, about 3.96 mm of centerline. The source owner is `analog_ch8`, and the surrounding 36-member typed-cell problem remains obstructed by fixed J8's 0.045-mm native overhang and 11 other foreign-region-hit channel-8 refs. No J8 checker exception was used. The F.Cu route and In1 fill are only local; the full ADC8N/P and VMID2 analog demand, connected return over all future branches, channel-8 physical cell, ESD and label debt, and production via/assembly review remain.

The receive stitch is near `C_ADC_CM8N` and its adjacent analog parts. Native DRC and `V-PROCESS` pass, but this packet does not qualify solder wicking, mask/assembly access, channel-level SI, or the complete return-current loop on a routed board. The 0.190-mm gap has only 0.040-mm rule margin and deserves explicit fabrication review before any design adoption.

Run `python3 route_probe.py` from any directory in this research branch while the hash-pinned frozen TI source packet remains at the recorded path. The exact TI `.pro`, `.dru`, and assembly bytes are archived in `profile/`; the producer resolves the original assembly path to its related frozen nets, floorplan, and part data. Their hashes are checked before use. The script rejects input hash drift, pad/pose drift, new DRC or V-PROCESS findings, loss of native endpoint connectivity, a sub-rule copper gap, or a hole in the filled route ribbon. KiCad creates fresh UUIDs for new tracks, vias, and POFV areas, so the receipt's generated-board byte hashes describe **this committed observation**, not a promise of byte-identical replay; the pinned vertices, rule-area identities, issues, and geometry are the reproducible checks.
