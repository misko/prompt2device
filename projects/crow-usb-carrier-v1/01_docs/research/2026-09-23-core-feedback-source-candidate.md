# TPS62825 core feedback correction, isolated source candidate

This candidate changes only the existing 0V9 adjustable TPS62825 feedback network. [TI TPS62825 SLVSEF9I](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62825.pdf) §8.2.2.2 says the bottom feedback resistor must not exceed 100 kΩ and gives 120 pF feedforward capacitance for a 100 kΩ bottom resistor. The inherited core used 100 kΩ top / 200 kΩ bottom and omitted feedforward. The replacement is **49.9 kΩ top RT0402BRD0749K9L / 100 kΩ bottom RT0402BRD07100KL**, with **120 pF C0G CC0402JRNPO9BN121** across top. Source pin 1 of C_CORE_FF goes to 0V9 and pin 2 to U_CORE_FB. The bottom resistor and capacitor reuse existing stocked MPNs; only the 49.9 kΩ YAGEO thin-film resistor adds a new MPN. Its exact YAGEO primary PDF is retained in the dossier.

TI's 594–606 mV VFB range, ±50 nA FB leakage, opposing 0.1% initial resistor tolerance and independent ±25 ppm/°C × 100°C TCR screen produce nominal **0.8994 V** and computed DC **0.8859–0.9131 V** (outward rounding). E-TOPO's executable `feedback:` block computes 0.886–0.913 V and verifies the unchanged **0.88–0.92 V** declared rail envelope. [XMOS XU316 primary](../../02_parts/XU316-1024-TQ128-C24/XM-014532-PC-v2.0.0.pdf) gives 0.855–0.945 V VDD; the computed core DC screen leaves at least 30 mV to both operating limits. Its declared 1.2 A maximum output and 4.95–5.05 V input remain within TPS62825's 2 A and 2.4–5.5 V operating conditions. The worst declared core output power is unchanged at 0.92 V ×1.2 A =1.104 W. Divider power is below 0.01 mW per resistor, versus the YAGEO 62.5 mW rating.

The existing TPS3808G09 core monitor has nominal 0.84 V falling threshold, ±1.5% threshold range, and at most 2.5% fixed-part hysteresis according to [TI SBVS050N](../../02_parts/TPS3808G09DBVR/TPS3808-SBVS050N.pdf). Screening its highest release near 0.874 V leaves about 12 mV to the new core DC low. This is a startup steady-state screen, not a guarantee for ripple, ground shift, dynamic droop or collapse. First article must show the regulator's monotonic ramp, completed 0V9/1V8/3V3X rails before XMOS reset release, and combined output ripple/ground shift at release below 10 mV (the calculated DC low has about 12 mV to the worst screened reset-release threshold). Combined positive overshoot/ripple/ground shift must stay below 25 mV to retain at least 7 mV below XMOS 0.945 V maximum; combined negative dynamic droop/ground shift must stay below 25 mV to retain at least 5 mV above XMOS 0.855 V minimum. These are first-article acceptance allocations, not guaranteed TPS62825 waveform limits; the TI soft-start entry is typical only. No assertion that reset always precedes XMOS's absolute operating-floor crossing on a fast collapse is made. Existing 1V8_OK → CORE_EN → CORE_OK reset topology and 0V9 net are retained.

The nominal 0.47 µH / 47 µF core output LC remains the TI Table 8-3 marked combination. The Murata cap-bank engineering screen at its more-adverse 0.92 V model point and the current/thermal allocations do not weaken under the contained DC window; installed stability, EMI, heat and startup waveforms still need physical qualification. TI's feedforward recommendation changes the compensation path, so first-article startup/load-step proof is particularly relevant.
