This is a fail-closed schema/geometry result, not a P1/P2 placement or routing acceptance. No board, outline, connector, checker, or canonical source was changed.

The reproducible [probe](probe.py) pins the southwest-cap integrated board (`0b9d017706845ad4…`), current floorplan (`c2a6562c1a012e69…`), modular plan (`75c3a517cea50dd6…`), P1 source (`191e5580a6ddf56b…`), and checker (`e060924188e4e68d…`); full hashes and measurements are in [receipt.json](receipt.json). It reads 569 native footprints and exactly 36 unique `analog_ch8` refs. J8 belongs to that denominator and to the 27 fixed refs. The machine-readable [typed_cells.json](typed_cells.json) assigns every member to exactly one candidate cell, retaining `analog_ch8` as the primary cap cell. These are **minimum native-envelope/pad rectangles**, useful only as a necessary-condition screen: they have no route or return margin, and are not proposed production cell boundaries.

## Fixed connector contradiction

The native board outline has bounding box `[20.000,20.000,240.000,140.000]` mm. J8's full checker envelope is `[198.875,19.955,216.268081,34.495]` mm; its body is `[198.891919,19.975,216.268081,34.475]` mm. Thus the full envelope extends **0.045 mm** beyond the top edge, and even its body extends **0.025 mm**. Its 12 pads begin at y=23.260 mm. The probe's smallest J8 cell is the full-envelope rectangle. The current checker reports `analog_ch8_j8: physical cell off board outline`. Clipping its north edge to y=20.000 instead reports `J8: native footprint/pad leaves physical cell analog_ch8_j8`. This is a monotone contradiction: any cell containing J8's required full envelope includes y=19.955, while every allowed cell lies at or below the board's y=20.000 top edge. Subdividing the other 35 refs or recutting their source regions cannot remove it.

Current `usb_frontend=[200,35,238.5,40]` mm starts **0.505 mm** below the south edge of J8's full envelope. The prior y=29 USB-region overlap in an older research note is no longer present in the pinned source. J8's overhang is a **checker authority problem**, not by itself proof of a bad connector mounting. Any future tightly scoped edge-connector exception requires independent mechanical/FULL evidence for the actual mating orientation, body/courtyard overhang, edge registration, fixed pose, and pad accessibility. Such evidence must identify J8 and the permitted overhang explicitly; a generic out-of-outline footprint allowance would weaken unrelated ownership checks. Alternatively, a separately reviewed connector/outline redesign could remove the contradiction. Neither change is made or credited here.

## Complete source recut still owed

The diagnostic primary cell for reviewed `C_ADC_AC8N1` has full envelope `[195.205,58.305,200.795,61.795]` mm. Its gap to the current `analog_ch8` east edge x=201 is **0.205 mm**; the gap to the `usb_vbus_sense` north edge y=62 is **0.205 mm**. This cap has `ISO8N` and `ADC8N` signal pads, no GND pad. The exact-bound cell does not establish a signal entry, return mouth, or filled reference. The saved In1.Cu GND zone is unfilled; Terra's independent via screen found only a possible GND stitching site at `(199.80,58.00)` mm, 2.05 mm from the ADC8N pad, with no route or return credit.

An eventual source proposal must add exact typed `physical_cells` rows (`id`, `owner_block`, `refs`, `transit`) under `analog_ch8`, maintain one primary region with id `analog_ch8`, and replace the present broad 36-ref placement pattern with per-cell placements. Every one of the 36 refs must occur exactly once, including fixed J8. The minimum-bound manifest demonstrates complete accounting, not valid dimensions. Each occupied cell must contain its **full** native footprint envelope and every pad, exclude every unassigned native envelope/pad, lie in the outline, and avoid all foreign source regions. Larger useful cells require independent native-clearance checks; bare per-ref minima reserve no routing space.
