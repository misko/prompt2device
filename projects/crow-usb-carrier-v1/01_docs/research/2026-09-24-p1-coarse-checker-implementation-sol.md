# P1 coarse reservation checker prototype

`p1_corridor_capacity.py` now accepts a separate JSON `schema: 2`, `kind: p1-coarse-reservations` contract. Schema 1 and its historical tests remain intact. The new `crow-p1-coarse` profile binds the board, P1 requirements YAML, modular interfaces, USB4215 alias part file, floorplan YAML, and the contract itself to independently supplied SHA-256 digests. It requires the exact source-derived 59-net partition (USB 4, service 13, ADC timing 14, ADC analog 18, power/mechanical 10), while sampling one or more source-owned native `ref.pad/net/layer` witnesses per net at named block faces. It does not demand every terminal pocket or graph reachability.

Each allocation declares `coverage_nets`, `boundary_witnesses`, and `reservations`. A witness records `source`, `native`, `net`, `block`, `layer`, `face`, `boundary_bbox`, and `reservation_id`. Its pad must be on the declared native net and layer, belong to the specified modular block, lie in an on-board face bbox, and touch its named reservation on the stated side. A reservation records `id`, `kind`, `layer`, `bbox`, and `nets`; a signal reservation additionally records `axis`, positive `demand_slots`, and `slot_pitch_mm` at least the native minimum. Power/mechanical windows keep current, return, thermal, and separation proof open. All bboxes must be on board; same-layer native rule-area and cross-allocation overlaps fail.

The checker computes two raw signal counts: `current_slots` with all footprints, and `potential_slots` with P2-movable footprints omitted. **Only an explicit `p1_fixed_refs` list in the hash-bound P1 source makes a footprint immutable for this screen.** `placement.anchors` alone does not: it contains provisional local decouplers and support parts. Fixed refs must also have a matching native pose in the bound floorplan. Native NPTH holes and existing copper stay fixed. An insufficient fixed-only potential is `FAIL`; present movable obstructions are named `movable_relocation_debt`, not a P1 rejection. This is still optimistic bbox arithmetic: no effective width/clearance, pad escape, filled GND reference, DRC, connector fit, or independent P1 review is supplied. Output always has `routing_realized: false` and `p1_accepted: false`; otherwise viable reservations remain `INCOMPLETE`.

The current Crow source has **no** `p1_fixed_refs` authority, and no coarse reservation contract is instantiated. The Crow profile therefore fails closed as `INCOMPLETE` until the fixed set is reviewed and added in a separately authorized source change. This work does not edit the Crow YAML, board, task graph, or P1 dispatch. It also does not relax Connector FULL before P3 or routing, or P4/P5 proof gates.

The generator resolves starting poses from `anchors` before `seeds`, then applies `post_anchors` after legalization. The checker allows intentional role overlap and checks a fixed ref against the effective post-anchor pose. A mixed USB allocation may use a `power_or_mechanical` window for `VBUS_USB` only when the bound source retains the incomplete `usb_local_power` demand. `VBUS_PRESENT_N` and other signal nets still require positive scalar demand, so a signal allocation cannot relabel QSPI or ADC windows to evade the capacity screen.

A witness bbox must also be local to the source block region: neither side may exceed one quarter of the smaller region dimension. A P2-movable witness must remain within that region plus a one-tenth-region-span halo. Fixed edge connectors can sit outside their block region because their source/native anchor pose is bound separately, but their witness bbox still obeys the size limit. This rejects the diagnostic unified candidate's 94–122 mm pad-to-reservation bridge boxes; they cannot become P1 block-face evidence by merely containing a pad and touching a distant reservation.

Validation: sixteen synthetic native tests cover viable raw capacity with movable debt (including an auto-placed `anchors` member), a truly fixed closure, post-anchor precedence, source-authorized USB VBUS power windows, a long witness bridge, absent/wrong-net/alias witnesses, wrong block face, off-outline or overlapping reservations, native rule-area overlap, forbidden P2 pocket claim, missing fixed-set authority, and refusal to treat a small fixture as the Crow 59-net profile. The 22 schema-1 tests still pass.
