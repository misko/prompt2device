# TLV320ADC6140 fixed-gain 2.5 kΩ front end: bounded source proposal

2026-09-23 UTC. Research only, no main-source edits or firmware. This is for two TLV320ADC6140IRTWT, eight 48 kHz simultaneous analog inputs in 8×32-bit TDM, DRE **off**, 0 dB channel gain, default 2 Vrms differential full scale, 3.3 V AVDD. The 1.2 Vrms differential pod maximum retains 4.44 dB converter headroom. TI [SBAS992A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv320adc6140.pdf) §7.5 gives 112 dB typical SNR/113 dB typical dynamic range under the stated AC-coupled 2.5 kΩ conditions (typical input-referred planning noise 4.48 µVrms using DR); at the nominal 1 Pa pod reference the ADC-alone capsule SNR penalty is ~0.75 dB, before retained OPA/filter noise. TI [SBAA383C](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/sbaa383c/sbaa383c.pdf) and datasheet §8.3.1.3 support multi-device simultaneous sampling and common TDM, though XU edge/config and phase calibration still need source implementation/first-article verification.

## Concrete AC-coupling cell, repeated for all 16 legs

`OPA2320/filter → TMUX2821 → switched node → (C_AC_A || C_AC_B) → ADC_INxP/M`. Fit **two Murata GRM32ER71A476KE15L, C84494, 47 µF ±10%, 10 V X7R 1210 per leg**, so 32 new caps/board. Move each existing 10 kΩ `R_ADC_PD` from ADC input to the **switched node before the new capacitors**; it keeps the isolated/off switched node defined at ground without forcing the powered ADC's internally biased input to ground. Retain the ADC-side 1 nF `C_ADC_CM` provisionally, after checking the exact filter/settling and switch transient. The two caps share pads/routes symmetrically and sit close to the ADC pins; respect Murata's exact 1210 land geometry and TI's local analog-input routing. This is one exact stocked cell, not a demand to fit 16×22 µF on nameplate alone.

Fresh JLC public responses at 04:09:25–27 UTC: [C84494 raw](/tmp/crow-tlv-front-C84494-20260923.json) reports **56,473**. This proposal adds 32 C84494 per board to the **10 already adopted per board**, so the exact-part aggregated five-board build plus 150 extra is `(32+10)×5+150=360` units, comfortably in stock; [C1852023 raw](/tmp/crow-tlv-front-C1852023-20260923.json) reports **188** TLV320ADC6140IRTWT, versus `2×5+150=160` needed; existing 10 kΩ C60490 stock 8,035,855. This is public stock, not reserved JLC PCBA allocation or uploader/placement acceptance. The exact Murata manufacturer [reference sheet](https://search.murata.co.jp/Ceramy/image/img/A01X/G101/ENG/GRM32ER71A476KE15-04CA.pdf) and retained [SimSurfing model](https://ds.murata.com/simsurfing/mlcc.html?oripartnumbers=%5B%22GRM32ER71A476KE15L%22%5D&partnumbers=%5B%22GRM32ER71A476KE15%22%5D) already have a source-stage engineering screen in `01_docs/research/2026-09-23-grm32-bank-candidate.md`. The direct model gives **14.7 µF typical at −55 °C and 5.05 V**, whose ×0.90 tolerance ×0.875 lifecycle factors give **11.576 µF per device**. The adopted bank screen first rounds that model down to `47 µF×0.31=14.57 µF`, then applies the same factors for a more conservative **11.474 µF per device**. The calculations below use the rounded-down 11.474 µF. This is deliberately more adverse voltage than the coupling capacitor's normal ~0.15 V DC difference, but it is a *small-signal typical model plus engineering reserves*, **not** a manufacturer guaranteed minimum at the actual large AC swing. Do not silently call it a production worst case.

At TI's **typical** 2.5 kΩ per ADC input leg and negligible OPA source resistance, the two-cap lower planning value is **22.948 µF/leg**. Its first-order pole is 2.774 Hz, with −0.083 dB additional gain and +7.90° high-pass phase at 20 Hz. An optimistic two-cap nominal+tolerance upper value `2×47×1.10=103.4 µF` gives 0.616 Hz, −0.004 dB and +1.76° at 20 Hz. Thus an intentionally broad capacitor-only planning range gives up to **0.079 dB / 6.13° channel mismatch at 20 Hz** if channels see opposite corners. The phase spread corresponds to about **0.85 ms of equivalent phase delay at 20 Hz**, a material array-function risk requiring quantified review against the localization/beamforming use case; it is **not** a constant time skew or a proven board behavior. The range is not a guarantee because TI specifies 2.5 kΩ as *typical*, without input-resistance extrema, and the actual biased/large-signal capacitance and correlated lot distribution are unspecified. At 100 Hz the lower-C pole contributes only ~1.59°; the mismatch effect falls with frequency. The existing 1 µF pod film into 100 kΩ bias has a ~1.59 Hz pole, −0.027 dB and +4.55° at 20 Hz; that stage remains, so the new lower-C planning case gives ~−0.11 dB / +12.4° cumulative at 20 Hz. The old CS path does not have this second AC-coupling pole. No user-locked 20 Hz gain or phase tolerance exists, but the array-phase risk must be assessed rather than waved away for lack of a numeric threshold.

## Analog loading and distortion

TI [SLYT796A](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/slyt796a/slyt796a.pdf), an application study using TLV320ADC5140/6140 EVMs, states the AC-coupled ADC pins bias near **1.5 V**. The retained OPA/filter common mode is ~**1.65 V** on quiet 3.3 V, so these nonpolar caps see about 0.15 V DC in normal powered operation; at 1.2 Vrms *differential* maximum, ideal balanced legs each swing ±0.848 V peak. OPA outputs stay ~0.80–2.50 V; ADC input pins, if the 1.5 V bias is held, ~0.65–2.35 V, both within 0–3.3 V operating rails. The small high-pass loss leaves the converter below full scale. OPA supply/ramp/mux conditions can shift these values and need power-state checks.

At audio frequencies, each OPA leg sees 10 kΩ to ground at the switched node and approximately 2.5 kΩ through the cap to the ADC's bias, or ~2.0 kΩ combined when the mux is on. The retained CS5308P in mid-impedance mode has 3 kΩ *differential* input resistance (Cirrus DS1314F2 §3), equivalent to ~1.5 kΩ per balanced leg before its own 10 kΩ pulldown: the proposed TLV audio-band resistive load is **not heavier** than the existing CS topology. Near DC the new cap removes the 2.5 kΩ load, leaving only the existing 10 kΩ; the OPA's ~1.65 V DC is blocked from TI's ADC input bias. With 0.848 Vpeak leg signal, the 2.5 kΩ AC path draws ~0.34 mA peak and the retained 30 nF filter shunts draw ~1.60 mA peak at 20 kHz; these are plausible for OPA2320AIDR but its *closed-loop* stability/THD into this capacitive filter plus mux and actual 2.5 kΩ ADC load must be checked. Source R_OUT is 10 Ω and TMUX RON ≤0.3 Ω, small relative to 2.5 kΩ. The 1 nF ADC pin shunt and all op-amp feedback poles must be simulated together rather than treated as an isolated one-pole low-pass.

TI §8.3.3 specifically prohibits 2.5 kΩ in DC-coupled mode and requests low-voltage-coefficient AC capacitors for best distortion. SLYT796A Figures 1–3 **measure** high-K ceramic coupling distortion versus frequency/amplitude and show 47 µF greatly reduces low-frequency distortion compared with 4.7 µF at 2.5 kΩ, but its tested 47 µF X5R is **not** the exact Murata X7R part or a pair. The application study warns ceramic distortion rises toward bass and with amplitude; it does not give an exact 20 Hz THD guarantee for this network. Exact Murata large-signal C/THD and full-path OPA+ADC THD should be validated before claiming the 113 dB ADC and retained audio-band distortion in the assembled path. SMD film 4.7/10/22 µF exact JLC rows found in this bounded search had stock ≤2/zero for the needed 230-unit single-per-leg threshold, so film was not silently substituted; high-K ceramic is an evidence-backed source candidate, not necessarily final best performance.

## Power-up, isolation and control contract

The 10 kΩ pulldown stays at the TMUX output **before** the AC caps, maintaining the existing off-state discharge of the switched node; the ADC input **after** the caps must instead rely on its internal bias and local 1 nF, with its off/AVDD-off injection state analyzed. TI §8.3.3 says caps >1 µF require configured `INCAP_QCHG`; register P0_R5 permits 2.5, 12.5, 25 or 50 ms typical quick-charge windows. The two parallel 47 µF pieces are large, so select the 50 ms setting initially, but **do not assume it fully settles 94 µF nominal**; the actual charging current, common-mode ramp, turn-on mute time and repeated power cycles require measurement or TI confirmation. During `TMUX off`, ensure the ADC internal bias is legal and no capacitor transient back-powers the unpowered mux/OPA; during `TMUX on`, suppress output until settled. Preserve the existing quiet shutdown, 3.3 V rail and XU clock/reset ordering. The x140 requires I²C configuration for DRE-off/0 dB/2.5 kΩ/AC mode/TDM/quick-charge; this is a hardware/software contract only, not firmware authoring.

## Disposition

**A source proposal exists:** two stocked TLV320ADC6140IRTWTs with 2×C84494 per input leg, pulldowns moved to the mux side, and the existing OPA/filter/TMUX retained. The nominal electrical and aggregated stock arithmetic support moving to an isolated candidate source branch with explicit unclosed THD, startup/settling, input-impedance corner and **array-phase** checks; the possible 0.85 ms equivalent 20 Hz phase-delay spread is a real risk to review before adopting the analog network. The proposal does not justify a sealed release or a claim of guaranteed 20 Hz phase matching. Relative to CS5308, it adds 32 1210 caps, a second low-frequency pole and I²C configuration, while offering eight manufacturer-documented synchronized channels at a 3.3 V analog supply with publicly stocked ADCs. If the measured/characterized bass THD, array phase or settling is unacceptable, reconsider a better stocked nonpolar coupling technology or a different ADC mode rather than overriding the user’s 150-unit reserve.
