# Independent review — TPS26625 ILIM first-via headroom screen

**Disposition: retain as a local native-geometry improvement only; no P2 or manufacturing acceptance.** I reran the finite scan and the selected native-profile replay from SOL commit `0fa2455269995e12fe4ea796d2cdc76e112c4c5f`, then restored generated receipts.

The finite screen is correctly bounded: 90 sampled centers at 0.05-mm pitch, with 9 meeting its analytic 0.20-mm via and launch criteria. It does not prove a continuous optimum, a full route search, or a tolerance distribution. The selected first ILIM via `(192.20,47.80)` independently reproduces native effective F.Cu gaps of **0.260253 mm** to the GND return and **0.249267 mm** to U.8. Against the packet's 0.200-mm screen these are 0.060253 and 0.049267 mm of exact-geometry headroom, respectively.

The selected replay preserves the one C(dVdT) pose move, 569 footprints, 27 fixed refs, group envelope/owner containment, pad identities, and ADC8N witness. Named input, output, ILIM, C(IN)/C(OUT) GND, and RTN connections pass. Both off-pad GND stitches remain on filled In1 outline 8 and RTN remains separate from GND. V-PROCESS is clean; full profile remains 199 violations and 499 opens before and after, with +0/−0 issue identities. The new ILIM centerline is 4.053741 mm, 0.230252 mm longer than the prior local witness; the 5.204687-mm² ILIM/RTN projection remains a 2-D diagnostic, not a measured loop quantity.

The improved clearances are **research margin, not manufacturing tolerance**. The screen omits continuous-position optimization, finished copper/etch and registration variation, drill positional variation, solder-mask/paste/fillet behavior, stackup and return-current variation, and current/thermal loading. Future changes to the six-member placement, return topology, stack, or process must re-run the native and V-PROCESS checks. dVdT/UVLO signal routing, PowerPAD/RTN thermal realization, actual loop impedance/current/temperature, incomplete pod distribution, source-cell authority, and ADC8/J8/P1 debts remain open.
