**Proposal only; no canonical source, board, route, graph, or active P1 attempt changed.** The smallest existing placement seam for the [isolated PLL pose](2026-09-24-xu-pll-east-isolated-sol.md) is three entries in `placement.post_anchors` of `03_src/floorplan.yaml`: `C_PLL_100N: [220.0, 102.8, 0]`, `C_PLL_1U: [220.0, 105.0, 0]`, and `FB_PLL: [223.0, 103.9, 180]`. The generic generator applies post anchors after legalization and then checks collisions. Ordinary `placement.anchors` alter the legalizer's obstacle sequence, so they are not equivalent for this scope.

I tested both forms in `/tmp/crow-xu-replay-seam-sol`, an isolated copy of `/tmp/crow-xu-southwest-sol`. From the same copied floorplan/netlist, I freshly generated a 569-footprint baseline, then one board with those three ordinary anchors and one with those three post anchors. All three target poses are exact in both variants. Relative to the **fresh generated baseline**, ordinary anchors change **five** poses: the three targets plus `C_XU_USB18` (`[210.4,110.8,0] -> [198.6,103.6,0]`) and `C_XU_USB33` (`[206.6,110.8,0] -> [210.4,110.8,0]`).
