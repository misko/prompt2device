# Full-outline connector qualification coupon — feasibility audit

**Status: NOT FABRICATION-READY / NO FULL CREDIT.** This is a read-only
engineering feasibility record. It creates no PCB, Gerber, drill, fabrication
authorization, connector qualification result, ESD test article, release, or
order decision.

## Conclusion

A full-outline **research representation** can copy the current private TI
diagnostic's eleven connector footprints, 220.0 x 120.0 mm outline, four
copper layers, and 1.63 mm KiCad stack target.  It cannot yet be made into a
credible governed mechanical qualification coupon, because the current source
contains no mounting-hole or restraint geometry and no accepted board/coupon
authority.  Inventing those features would make a different mechanical
subject, so it could not close the reaction, service, registration, cable, or
interface predicates in connector FULL.

The current three-pose board is private diagnostic evidence only.  D14 says it
may not be used as fabrication input, and D13 permits neither fabrication nor
order from the `prototype_only` USB ESD selection.  Producing a source-only
research sketch would not itself be fabrication, but it would not change either
authority boundary.  No coupon PCB or manufacturing payload is generated by
this audit.

## Missing values that prevent a governed coupon

| Needed value or decision | Why it is essential | Owner/source authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted exact candidate board hash and its final outline/connector-neighbor geometry | ADR 0011 requires the relevant board or governed coupon; the current diagnostic is expressly unaccepted. | P1/coupled-placement engineering and its independent review |
| Mounting-hole positions, diameters, hardware, support plane and allowable restraint locations | Every reaction test requires load to pass through the PCB to adjacent board restraints. The current board has no mounting-hole footprint or `mounting_holes` declaration. | Mechanical/board integration owner |
| Fixture restraint design and operating load cases | A clamp or standoff must not use connector bodies, cables, solder joints, components, or traces as a counter-hold. | Mechanical test owner |
| Intended enclosure geometry, cable exits, straight runs, bends, strain relief and far-end supports | The three cable targets and service/access observations are installed-state predicates. A bare outline cannot establish them. | Enclosure/cable integration owner |
| Fabricator's controlled four-layer stackup and finished-thickness/edge tolerance, plus assembly method | The 1.63 mm value is a KiCad target, not a bound finished article/process. Registration and shell-stake observations require the actual process. | Fabricator/manufacturing owner |
| Numeric acceptance limits, cycle counts and measurement uncertainty | The existing plan deliberately does not invent force, deflection, setback, or cycle limits. Measurements without limits cannot grade a PASS. | Engineering authority cited by the qualification owner |
| Explicit narrow fabrication authorization for an unpowered mechanical coupon | D13 and D14 supply no fabrication authority. The decision must exclude ESD exposure, powered operation, release, and production/order credit. | Electrical decision owner with project release authority |

The existing USB-only four-layer research coupon does not fill these gaps.  It
targets 1.60 mm rather than 1.63 mm, has no controlled finished stackup,
leaves limits unset, and explicitly has no FULL or fabrication credit.  The
complete-field fit prototype likewise has no mounting features and calls its
220 x 120 mm rectangle exploratory.

## Smallest viable future article

The smallest article capable of closing all 19 connector targets is not a
small single-port coupon.  It is an **unpowered, full-field mechanical
qualification coupon** that reproduces the accepted board's full connector
field, exact connector footprints and poses, outline/edge process, thickness,
mounting features, and populated-neighbor geometry.  It also needs an attached
or faithfully represented intended enclosure and cable-support arrangement.
That permits all eleven connectors to be populated for both declared service
groups, while the fixture routes reaction through the declared PCB mounting
features.

Before fabricating that article, bind one packet to: the native board and
coupon hashes; connector, mate, cable, board, enclosure, and assembly-lot
identities; stackup and finished dimensions; restraint drawing; calibrated
measurement equipment; approved acceptance limits; and the existing
per-target records in `2026-09-22-connector-physical-qualification-plan.md`.
The resulting observations can be considered by the connector FULL gate only
after the separate authorization and review required by ADR 0011.  It still
does not close D13's USB ESD transient finding.
