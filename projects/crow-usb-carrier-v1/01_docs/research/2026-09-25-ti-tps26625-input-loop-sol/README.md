# One native TPS26625 input-bypass loop trial

This packet adds **one** `N12V_PROTECTED` F.Cu segment between `U_SPOKE8.1` and `C_SPOKE_IN8.1` to the reviewed partial-return board `80d193b3` (board SHA pinned in `loop_probe.py`). The script reconstructs that source-generated board from frozen TI inputs, verifies every footprint/pad pose and existing track against the immutable prior artifact, then replays both versions under the complete archived `.kicad_pro`/`.kicad_dru`/POFV/V-PROCESS profile. The only new copper is a **1.20 mm** wide, **1.90 mm** long track on x=188.30 from y=45.90 to 44.00 mm. Its rounded ends contact both named F.Cu pads; native connectivity confirms the supply side, while the previously routed 7.40 mm GND return still joins `C_SPOKE_IN8.2` to `U_SPOKE8.6` over one filled In1.Cu GND polygon. GND and `SPOKE_RTN8` remain separate.

The projected pad-center closure polygon is `(188.6,46.5) → (188.3,45.9) → (188.3,44.0) → (188.8,44.0) → (190.7,44.0) → (192.5,44.0) → (192.5,48.5) → (191.4,48.5) → start`, with **15.410 mm²** shoelace area. The polygon closes across the capacitor and IC pin-center chords; these two chords are internal component/device spans, not PCB tracks. The 1.90 mm new track plus its within-pad center chords gives a 3.070820 mm pad-center supply-path surrogate. These are geometric loop measurements, not measured current distribution or an EMI result.

The source `INPUT_TRUNK` class assigns `N12V_PROTECTED` a **1.20 mm minimum width** and 0.20 mm clearance. The first centered 1.20 mm attempt at x=188.60 connected the pads but failed native clearance to PowerPAD.11 (`SPOKE_RTN8`): 0.1004 mm actual versus 0.2000 mm required. One lateral correction to x=188.30 passes the full profile: **199 violations / 499 opens** both before and after, with +0/−0 issue identities and zero V-PROCESS failures. Effective-shape gaps on the final route are 0.362121 mm to PowerPAD.11 and 0.381774 mm to adjacent UVLO pin U.2; the other nearby foreign-pad gaps are in `receipt.json`.

The width is compliant along the new track, but U.1's native land is only 0.60 × 0.24 mm and the rounded track end reaches its upper edge by just 0.12 mm in y. Native contact and DRC do **not** establish current-sharing, voltage drop, hot-corner ampacity, or a robust production neck at that pad. The RTN PowerPAD has connected local tracks but no qualified RTN copper/thermal island. OUT, ILIM, dVdT, and UVLO signal legs remain unrouted; those four loops have undefined areas. The ADC8N witness, 27 fixed refs, source ownership/full envelopes, and 569 pad identities are unchanged. No canonical source, P1, or P2 promotion follows.

Reproduce with `python3 loop_probe.py` from this directory. The filled board is the sole native artifact. Its byte hash is observational because generated POFV UUIDs drift; the script checks semantic source and native issue identities.
