# Crow XMOS schematic page 30 — read-only presentation investigation

## Observed failure and cause

- Current source: `projects/crow-usb-carrier-v1/03_tscircuit/src/z_schematic_presentation.tsx` declares one `U_XU` 24 × 24 mm schematic body and round-robin pin arrangement across four sides; `crow_usb_digital.tsx` declares one physical XU316 chip. Current canonical `03_tscircuit/build/circuit.json` has one U_XU source component, one schematic component, and exactly 129 source ports and 129 schematic ports (pins 1–128 and EP129). It has no native multi-unit split.
- Current PDF page 30 is landscape, 900 × 607.5 pt, with one schematic component. Poppler XML reports pin number/label fonts of 4–5 px on a 1350 px representation, approximately 2.7–3.3 pt physical. The current circuit-to-svg transform is 23.984 px/mm in its 1200 × 720 page SVG. Its pinned code fixes pin number and label font to 0.15 mm (`get-sch-font-size.ts`, `create-svg-objects-for-sch-port-pin-label`), producing 3.598 px, or 2.70 pt after PDF conversion. This is independent of the spacious 24 mm body and is the direct reason normal-page pin labels are unreadable.
- The pin banks have 32–33 pins per side. The side-bank pitch is roughly 18 px at the current SVG scale, so larger text can fit vertically. A read-only `/tmp` SVG probe multiplying only pin-number/label fonts by 2.6 made left/right labels legible but caused top/bottom rotated labels to overlap. `/tmp/xmos-page-original.svg` and `/tmp/xmos-page-font2p6.svg` retain that diagnostic; neither is source or a candidate PDF.
- The pinned `tscircuit@0.0.2351` ChipProps supports `schPinArrangement`, `schPinStyle`, `schWidth`, and `schHeight`; it exposes no `schUnit` or equivalent multi-unit chip field. `schPinStyle` provides margins, not font size. A multi-unit projection is therefore unnecessary as a first experiment and would be materially broader.

## Minimal bounded experiment after authorization

1. In isolated source, change only the U_XU presentation body width from 24 mm to about 40 mm in `z_schematic_presentation.tsx`, leaving its height, all 129 pin labels, physical chip, pin numbers, connections, and footprint untouched. Width around 40 mm should give top/bottom pins about 30 px horizontal pitch at the measured transform and still fit the 1200 px landscape content width; verify rather than assume the transform remains near 24 px/mm.
2. Add a narrow renderer option for this declared sheet's pin-number and pin-label font scale, invoked by Crow's driver for `xmos_core` only, initially 2.4–2.6 times the pinned 0.15 mm font. Apply it to SVG text classes after `circuit-to-svg` and before baseline normalization. Do not scale text positions, traces, or the whole page. Preserve the renderer's exact canonical circuit SHA header and its zero-component fail gate. If text placement is still cramped, regroup only the source-authored `schPinArrangement` order into explicit functional banks (power, USB/reset/clock, audio/I2S/control, QSPI/debug, NC); do not omit a numbered pin or change connectivity.
3. Produce one isolated fresh Circuit JSON/PDF. Compare old/new source ref, MPN, footprint, and every `(U_XU pin number, source-port name, connectivity key/net)` tuple. Require all 129 source and schematic ports once each, and no change to all other component pin-to-net populations. Run source/electrical identity, schematic render, ERC, pin-map parity and M-FRESH; then obtain independent normal-page visual review of page 30. Any generated-circuit hash change is reviewed before a new hash-bound schematic witness, not silently repinned.
4. Check label collisions on the actual PDF: extract page-30 text bounding boxes (Poppler XML or SVG) for each pin number/label and net label, then inspect every top/bottom bank and the corners visually at 100% print scale. A nominal 8 pt or larger pin text target is useful, but no numeric threshold alone replaces the visual S6 review. Reject a result that grows text but overlaps, truncates, or disconnects any label.

## Cost and boundary

Expected one TSX producer (~72–120 s observed), renderer (<1 min), focused semantic checks, and independent PDF review. Do not rerun producers merely for the read-only investigation. The existing 39-page PDF is unchanged. This proposal does not touch electrical TSX, physical pin allocation, PCB layout, vendor ordering, or D11 public sourcing authority.
