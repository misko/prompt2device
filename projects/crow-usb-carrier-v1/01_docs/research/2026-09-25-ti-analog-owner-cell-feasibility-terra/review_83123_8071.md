# Independent review: power pocket and coupled owner findings

Reviewed: SOL commits `83123d09` and `8071ec74`. Scope is the pinned
Q_PRE-repair diagnostic board only; this is not P1/P2/P3, routing, return, or
release evidence.

## Findings

`83123d09` is geometrically sound as a negative sparse-pocket finding. Its
reviewed-board hash is `e07ed8bc663fdfd4ce39477165b656b0dcf2bfbae54d84ec501bfccf326d22ef`
and it retains the exact 42/27/68/34 (171) terminal denominators. The reported
full envelopes agree with native extraction: U_AFE2 is
`[62.055,73.540,69.545,79.145]`, U_AFE3 is
`[84.055,73.540,91.545,79.145]`, and U_BUCK is
`[44.750,94.005,49.995,100.995]` mm. Full-footprint pockets are blocked by
adjacent owner regions for the AFE sites, and U_BUCK's same-owner rectangular
closure reaches foreign quiet-power `C_PWR_CT2`. A pad-only exception would
violate the checker’s explicit native-body/pad rule.

`8071ec74` correctly shows a bounded local repair, not an acceptance: moving
C_PWR_CT2 by +1.13 mm in Y gives a full-envelope lower edge of 101.655 mm,
just clear of the stated input-buck core hull ending at 101.645 mm; the 13
quiet-power plus six established cells pass the private physical-cell check.
The 12-reference `input_buck_core` then fails because all owner references are
required when an owner uses physical cells. This is an exact source grammar
constraint, not a copper or route result.

I also tested the narrower alternative of expanding the **primary** input-buck
region only to y=101.645 after that same in-memory C_PWR_CT2 move, without an
input-buck cell. It fails strict cells earlier at
`quiet_audio_control: physical cell overlaps foreign region input_buck`.
Therefore, on the current reviewed geometry, neither a narrow primary-region
expansion nor a partial core cell clears N5V_BUCK. Keeping native parts in
place requires a complete input-buck physical-cell partition (or a separately
reviewed placement/region redesign that removes all quiet-cell intersections).

The current packet's complete analog-owner-cell probe supplies a separate
limitation. Before any rectangular ch2/ch3/ch4 recut, a complete one-cell
owner hull fails off board because J2/J3/J4 full envelopes begin at y=19.955
mm. Thus a complete analog physical-cell route needs resolved edge-connector
physical authority and a multi-cell design; it is not available today. A
native placement move is the alternative to that complete partition, but it
must be independently checked for every displaced channel part and endpoint.

## Decision

Do not add another sparse pocket or pad-face exception. The next bounded
source decision should choose one of two explicit architectures: a full,
edge-authorized analog ch2/ch3/ch4 and input-buck cell partition, or a
placement redesign that restores full-footprint separation before celling.
The first is necessary if current native positions are retained; neither commit
provides P1 credit.
