The split does **not** yield a complete single-rectangle USB owner. The assigned USB footprint set is `J_USB`, `C_USB_VBUS`, `R_USB_CC1/2`, `R_USB_VBUS_BLEED`, `U_USB_CC_ESD`, `U_USB_ESD`, and `U_USB_VBUS_ESD`. Only `R_USB_CC2`, `R_USB_VBUS_BLEED`, and `U_USB_ESD` have full F.CrtYd bboxes inside the candidate USB rectangle; the other five lie partly or wholly north of y=35. Even excluding the exceptional edge connector, the support-courtyard union is `[216.155,29.205,238.445,36.915]`. Any single axis-aligned USB rectangle containing that union also intersects fixed `J8`'s F.CrtYd `[198.875,19.955,216.265,34.495]` by **0.110 mm in x and 5.290 mm in y**. Including `J_USB` additionally requires y≤19.45, outside the board. This is a rectangle-ownership conflict, not an observed physical courtyard collision: the actual USB support and J8 courtyards remain separated on the saved board.

Thus the candidate gives a potential named JTAG integration corridor, but no admissible source-owned contract yet. A future source model needs a split/nonrectangular USB owner or an explicitly governed connector/edge exception, while retaining all eight assigned USB footprints and fixed connector envelopes. It must then allocate both JTAG faces, source/native pad-to-face P2 obligations, reset's digital-power branch, return/reference, and native clearance. The current full 13-net `xmos_service_escape` contract remains **FAIL**; this split is incomplete research, with no P1 dispatch or acceptance.
