# Three-rail TPS62822 source candidate — 2026-09-23

This source candidate changes U_3V3X, U_1V8 and U_CORE from TPS62825DMQR/DMQ0006A to exact TPS62822DLCR/C473385 and TI DLC0008B 8-pin lands. It also changes their six input capacitors to Samsung CL21A106KOQNNNE/C1713 and the 3V3X 453-kΩ top resistor to Viking ARG03BTC4533/C2686428. It retains the three 100-kΩ bottom resistors, other two top resistors, 0.47-µH XFL4015-471MEC inductors, 47-µF output MLCCs, 120-pF feedforward capacitors and rail sequencing. Historical accepted source reviews, ADRs, raw circuit JSON and native CAD outputs remain historical artifacts; they do not attest to this unbuilt candidate.

The new TI Rev C dossier binds pins 1 EN, 2 FB, 3 AGND, 4 NC, 5 PGND, 6 SW, 7 VIN, 8 PG. NC4 and unused PG8 stay open. The project footprint follows the manufacturer's 0.60×0.25-mm example lands at 0.5-mm pitch and has a 2.4×2.6-mm courtyard enclosing its pin-1 silk marker. Its WRL is a drawing-derived **maximum body envelope** (1.6×2.1×1.0 mm), not vendor CAD, for collision review; JLC C473385 orientation, centroid and population acceptance remain to be checked before PCBA output.

The retained divider ratios give computed PWM-mode DC source estimates of 3.243321–3.393193 V (3V3X), 1.822162–1.898076 V (1V8), and 0.885835–0.913022 V (CORE). The rounded `power_tree.yaml` screens remain derived estimates. Hard *device-pin* limits are 3.00–3.60 V for XU316 USB 3V3, 1.70–1.95 V for the shared Winbond flash 1V8, and 0.855–0.945 V for XU316 PLL CORE. Proposed combined dynamic/return allocations are ≤100, ≤40 and ≤20 mV respectively. The 1V8 low estimate stays about 32.9 mV above the retained supervisor's worst rising release; changing the ratio to 200k/100k would lose that guaranteed release overlap, so it is retained. None of the listed allocations proves an assembled waveform. PSM/light-load behavior, supervisor timing, hot loss and monotonic starts still need review and first-article measurement.

TI Rev C requires ≥3 µF effective input and ≥5 µF effective output. For the exact Samsung input part, its separate typical curves give 47.21% DC retention at 5.13 V and 70.55% low-amplitude AC retention; their 33.31% product is not a joint PVT bound. Selecting a more adverse 30% combined engineering retention, then 10% initial, 15% temperature and 10% lifecycle adverse factors gives two × 10 µF × .30 × .90 × .85 × .90 = **4.131 µF estimated** per input bank. Local capacitor temperature must remain within X5R's +85°C rating at the 70°C ambient design case. The retained output bank source estimates 11.48/12.95/12.95 µF, above TI's 5-µF floor, while nominal 0.47 µH/47 µF is listed in TI's table. Hot current-biased inductance, peak current relative to TPS62822's 2.7-A minimum high-side limit, converter stability and capacitor lot behavior remain unqualified. The 85% parent-current assumption is a planning budget, not a thermal guarantee; hot package junction must remain ≤125°C in the 70°C ambient/full simultaneous load case.
