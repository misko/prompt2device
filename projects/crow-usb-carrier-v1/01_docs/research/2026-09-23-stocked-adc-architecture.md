# Crow ADC with five boards plus 150 spare units: bounded architecture proof

2026-09-23 UTC. Research only; no source edit, part freeze, firmware, purchase, or JLC placement acceptance. The user explicitly retains **150 extra units per part**. Required public-catalog stock is **155 for one ADC/board** or **160 for two identical ADCs/board**. AK5578EN (36), AK5558VN (32), CS5308P-DNR (0) fail this hard requirement regardless of their engineering promise. Every figure below is from manufacturer primary material or the timestamped JLC public-catalog JSON, not allocated PCBA inventory.

## Actual signal requirement and noise reference

Retained Crow pod ADR0003 and `2026-09-01-design-math.md` specify a **1.2 Vrms differential** maximum at the carrier connector, nominal 0.653 Vrms at 110 dB SPL, and ~0.952 Vrms at the +3 dB/resistor corner. The AOM-5024L-HD-R capsule is −24 dBV/Pa typical and **80 dB SNR at 1 Pa/94 dB SPL**, so its nominal output at the carrier for 1 Pa is `0.0631×18/11 = 0.1033 Vrms`; capsule self-noise referred to this connector is about `0.1033/10^(80/20)=10.33 µVrms`, subject to actual pod bias/load, bandwidth, weighting and tolerance. The 1.2 Vrms peak *interface* level is not the 80 dB SNR reference. A hypothetical ADC with 99 dB SNR referred to 1.2 Vrms could still have 13.3 µVrms noise, causing ~4.2 dB reduction of the capsule-only 80 dB SNR at 94 dB SPL. System noise must be assessed in volts at the same input plane.

There is **no user-locked 123 dB ADC or exact allowed SNR-loss minimum**. Preserve eight simultaneous channels, 48 kHz, 24 valid bits in eight 32-bit TDM slots, the 1.2 Vrms unclipped interface, existing microphone and spoke functionality, and stable cross-channel timing for recording/source association. The prior CS5308P is a reference: at 2 Vrms/123 dB its typical ADC input noise is 1.42 µVrms and capsule-only SNR penalty ~0.08 dB; the OPA/filter/cable noise adds to that. A quantitative acceptance limit for added noise/phase distortion has not been agreed, so calculations below rank options; they do not silently create one.

## Two PCM1865DBTR: feasible data format, unclosed analog noise and phase

TI SLAS831D §7.5, §9.3.5, §9.3.9, §9.3.16.4 and §10.1.4 give four differential analog inputs/IC, 4.2 Vrms differential full scale at 0 dB PGA, selectable −12..+12 dB **analog** PGA in 1 dB steps, and 256-BCK TDM frames with 24 data bits + 8 zeros per 32-bit word. Fig. 59 explicitly shows two devices, first slots 1–4, second slots 5–8, on one shared TDM data bus with two I²C addresses. At 48 kHz `BCK=48k×8×32=12.288MHz`. For Crow's 1.2 Vrms max, **+9 dB analog PGA** maps to `1.2×10^(9/20)=3.383Vrms` or 80.5% of 4.2 Vrms FS; using TI's stated ±0.5 dB PGA+ADC gain accuracy, +9.5 dB corner maps to 3.583 Vrms, leaving **1.38 dB** to nominal FS. +10 dB leaves only 0.38 dB under the same accuracy bound; +9 is the safer initial configuration. Per-device PGA and routing must be configured over I²C and latched with clocks running; this is a hardware/software contract, not firmware authoring here.

**Do not apply 110 dB SNR to +9 dB gain.** TI's 110 dB typical/97 dB minimum differential SNR is at **0 dB PGA**; its only high-gain point is 90 dB typical/85 dB minimum at **+32 dB** (Table §7.5). Typical input-referred noise inferred from these endpoints is ~13.28 µVrms at 0 dB (`4.2/10^(110/20)`) and ~3.34 µVrms at +32 dB (`[4.2/10^(32/20)]/10^(90/20)`). The data do **not** specify SNR, THD+N or input-referred noise at +9 dB, and interpolating is not guaranteed. At 0 dB, ADC noise alone would degrade the nominal 80 dB capsule SNR at 94 dB SPL by `10log10(1+(13.28/10.33)^2)=4.22dB` before buffer noise; if +9 dB actually achieved ≤5.27 µVrms input-referred ADC noise, ADC-only degradation would be ≤1 dB. The exact +9 dB number needs TI characterization or measured first article and full filter/OPA noise. TI's −93 dB typical differential THD+N is also at 0 dB and −1 dBFS, not the +9 condition. A 24-bit output format is real but does not establish 24-bit effective noise performance.

**Better clock topology than TI's illustrated Figure 59:** supply the existing common 24.576 MHz (`512fs`) low-jitter MCLK to **both** PCM1865 SCKI pins, and XU316's common 12.288 MHz BCK plus 48 kHz LRCK to both in **ADC slave mode**. TI §9.3.9.4 Table 5 expressly identifies slave mode with external MCLK and input BCK/LRCK as *PLL not required*; Table 6 lists 24.576 MHz for 48 kHz. §9.3.9 calls LRCK the exact sampling-point clock. Identical filter modes, common hardware reset/rail sequencing and the same clock tree are necessary. This avoids independently BCK-PLL-derived master-clock phase. It supports a single sampling **frequency and nominal LRCK-defined instant** across devices, but the datasheet does not state a maximum cross-chip aperture/group-delay mismatch or a deterministic start-of-decimator phase across independent chips. The two-chip TDM figure proves multiplexing, not those analog phase bounds. Source proof remains open until TI provides a synchronization guarantee/procedure or a design-specific independent timing argument; first article must then measure repeated power/reset simultaneous impulses and continuous drift. Do not claim phase coherence solely from shared frames.

PCM1865 input pins are designed to bias near AVDD/2 and TI §9.3.1 recommends DC-blocking capacitors even with external amplifiers to avoid gain/DC shift. The retained OPA output sits near 1.65 V on 3.3 V, which is promising but not proof; adding ADC-local coupling/filter and modifying the retained 10 kΩ pulldowns may be needed. The TI 10 kΩ typical per-pin input impedance and +9 analog gain change the loading/filter. Two TSSOP-30 cells, two address straps, shared clocks/reset, I²C port, pin map, analog and power networks replace the CS QFN cell. The existing XU hardware contract has not yet assigned an I²C master/control path; reserving legal pins/access is required without writing firmware.

## Stronger stocked architecture candidate: two TLV320ADC6140IRTWT

TI SBAS992A specifies four simultaneous differential analog channels per chip, 48 kHz support, **2 Vrms differential full scale** at 3–3.6 V AVDD with default 2.75 V VREF, TDM output with selectable 32-bit words, per-channel programmable slots and tri-state unused slots, I²C addresses, shared SHDNZ/BCLK/FSYNC. At 48 kHz eight 32-bit slots again use 12.288 MHz, below TI's 25 MHz shared-bus ceiling. TI app note **SBAA383C** provides an actual two-chip shared-data configuration: chip A slots 0–3, chip B slots 4–7, common SDOUT, BCLK, FSYNC, I²C, AVDD/IOVDD and SHDNZ; its pp. 17–19 scripts show the exact slot-register pattern and supply/reset order. The datasheet explicitly lists ability to **synchronize multiple devices for simultaneous sampling**, with I²C broadcast and per-channel phase calibration at modulator-clock resolution. This is materially stronger manufacturer-primary multi-chip timing support than the PCM1865 note, though the actual XU FSYNC polarity/edge and phase-calibration settings still require hardware/software contract and first-article confirmation. At 12.288 MHz, the app note's >18.5 MHz special latching caution does not apply.

**Both DRE-off and DRE-on are valid candidate modes; neither is a Crow-locked requirement.** TI §7.5 specifies, at 2.5 kΩ and AC-coupled differential input, DRE-off **112 dB typical SNR** with a shorted input and **113 dB typical dynamic range** with a −60 dBFS sine. The latter corresponds to ~`2/10^(113/20)=4.48 µVrms` as a planning noise estimate, yielding ~0.75 dB capsule-only SNR penalty at 94 dB SPL; with a provisional 4 µVrms retained buffer contribution, total system penalty is ~1.25 dB versus capsule alone, compared with ~0.67 dB for the prior CS path. DRE-on at the same 2.5 kΩ setting is **122 dB typical SNR** with a shorted input (115 dB minimum), and **123 dB typical dynamic range** with a −60 dBFS signal, at TI's specified −36 dB threshold and 24 dB maximum DRE gain. At 10 kΩ it is 117 dB SNR and 118 dB dynamic range. These are condition-specific A-weighted 20 Hz–20 kHz results; 123 dB is not a fixed, signal-independent noise floor. The retained 1.2 Vrms maximum is 4.44 dB below 2 Vrms full scale at 0 dB static gain.

At 1 Pa/94 dB SPL, the nominal pod output 0.1033 Vrms is **−25.7 dBFS** relative to 2 Vrms, above TI's recommended DRE trigger below −30 dBFS (−36 dB in the performance test; −54 dB default). DRE therefore should **not be credited with the 123 dB quiet-signal result at the 1 Pa capsule-SNR reference**. It may improve weak-signal recordings below its threshold. TI §8.3.7 says DRE monitors signal amplitude and modulates its low-noise PGA, with digital assistance and low latency intended to minimize audible artifacts; independent per-channel enables and analog signal envelopes mean channel gains can transition at different instants. This creates an amplitude/phase-transient qualification for inter-microphone localization, not a datasheet-stated prohibition: compare synchronized impulses, tones and time-varying envelopes across all eight channels with DRE on/off, including transitions. The datasheet does not specify a maximum interchannel phase or gain error *during* DRE transitions, so the 123 dB setting cannot close that requirement by itself.

**Important front-end backtrack:** the retained OPA→TMUX→ADC path is DC-coupled even though the *pod* is AC-coupled before the OPA. TI §8.3.3 explicitly says the **2.5 kΩ input setting is not supported in DC-coupled mode**. To use either the qualified 2.5 kΩ DRE-off **or** DRE-on condition, add a low-voltage-coefficient AC-coupling capacitor in each of the 16 ADC input legs, after isolation, and remove/rework the existing 10 kΩ ADC-pin ground pulldown that would fight the ADC's internal bias. The capacitance is an unresolved design variable, not an automatic 16×22 µF commitment: at 2.5 kΩ, 1 µF effective gives `fc≈63.7 Hz`, while 22 µF effective gives ~2.9 Hz/−0.09 dB at 20 Hz. A proposed value needs the actual low-frequency amplitude/phase requirement, capacitor tolerance, voltage/bias/temperature derating, voltage-coefficient THD, footprint and JLC availability. TI provides adjustable quick-charge time above 1 µF. Using **AC-coupled 10 kΩ** raises the DRE-on datasheet dynamic-range value to 118 dB and permits a smaller capacitance for the same high-pass pole; it is a candidate, not a performance-equivalent 2.5 kΩ substitution.

A **DC-coupled** x140 configuration can avoid 16 new capacitors by setting each `CHx_DC` bit and choosing 10 kΩ or 20 kΩ input impedance, since 2.5 kΩ is unsupported there. This requires proof that the retained OPA's ~1.65 V output common mode, differential offset, output swing and isolation/mux transients meet the converter's DC-coupled limits. TI allows DRE with DC coupling but limits `DRE_MAXGAIN` according to the DC differential offset (§8.3.7). The published 122/123 dB 2.5 kΩ DRE-on and 112/113 dB 2.5 kΩ DRE-off conditions cannot be assigned to this different mode; TI does not provide an equivalent DC-coupled 10/20 kΩ SNR or dynamic-range row. This is the physically smaller route if a measured/characterized noise and phase budget permits it. The 10 kΩ, 117/118 dB DRE-on rows are also not labeled DC-coupled, so claiming those values for DC coupling requires TI confirmation or measurement.

The `IRTWT` is an exact 24-pin 4×4 mm exposed-pad WQFN; the in-stock `IRTWR` row is only 12. It is not pin/footprint compatible with CS5308P. Reserve two distinct I²C addresses, shared reset/broadcast and clock pins, proper 3.3 V AVDD/IOVDD and internal AREG/DREG/VREF decoupling, channel and slot mapping, and XU I²C resources. The existing quiet 3.3 V regulator may be a source candidate only after two-chip power and rail-noise budgets. Do not author XU firmware here.

## Other stocked rows

Two TLV320ADC5140IRTWR (219 stock) share the x140 timing/physical architecture. TI SBAS892A specifies **108 dB DRE-disabled dynamic range at 0 dB/10 kΩ** (not 2.5 kΩ): ~7.96 µVrms planning ADC noise and ~2.0 dB capsule-only SNR penalty at 94 dB SPL before buffer, under its AC-coupled test condition. It also specifies **120 dB DRE-on dynamic range at 2.5 kΩ** and 117 dB at 10 kΩ for weak signals, so it is a qualified lower-performance fallback, not disqualified merely because it is not 6140. PCM4204PAPR (1143) has 118 dB and an explicitly drawn two-device TDM connection (TI SBAS327A Fig. 7), but its −10..+70°C operating range is incompatible with the retained −40..+80°C spoke service envelope; it also needs 5 V analog. ADAU1979WBCPZ-RL (1480) has 109 dB/4.5 Vrms: ~15.96 µVrms ADC input noise and ~5.3 dB capsule-only penalty; ADI's documented gain is post-ADC, so cannot fix input-referred noise. Both are engineering-inferior for this design despite ample stock. `CS5368`, `CS5364`, `AK5538`, ADAU1978, etc. retained/fresh catalog results fail 155/160 or have zero stock; no stocked single eight-channel device clearing 155 was established in this bounded screen.

## Disposition

**Recommended architecture for further source-level proof: two TLV320ADC6140IRTWT**, because it combines passed 160-unit public stock, original 2 Vrms scale, 3.3 V analog compatibility, eight-channel shared TDM, and explicit manufacturer-supported cross-device simultaneous sampling. This is a research decision, not source adoption. The remaining engineering choice is **which analog mode meets a user-approved noise, 20 Hz amplitude/phase and cross-channel transient budget**: 2.5 kΩ AC-coupled DRE off (fixed-gain 113 dB dynamic range), the same with DRE on (123 dB only under specified weak-signal test conditions, with transition qualification), 10 kΩ AC-coupled DRE on (118 dB specified under weak-signal conditions, smaller coupling capacitors), or 10/20 kΩ DC-coupled (fewer components, no directly applicable datasheet noise number). Then close exact capacitor/OPA loading and THD if AC coupled, or common-mode/offset/noise if DC coupled; XU I²C/clock pin contract; power/reset and phase timing; and JLC BOM matcher/placement acceptance. **PCM1865DBTR remains the stock-deep comparator** with unquantified +9 dB noise and no explicit cross-chip phase guarantee. If the TLV timing or chosen analog mode cannot be proven against the agreed budget, keep the ADC and release held; do not silently substitute a weaker part or remove the 150-unit surplus.
