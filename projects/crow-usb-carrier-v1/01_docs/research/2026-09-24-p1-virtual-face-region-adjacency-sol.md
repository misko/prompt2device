## Next three high-value iterations

1. **Redesign XU south clock and flash ownership.** The y=112 trim is rejected because it cuts five XU decoupler full-footprint bounds. Trial disjoint F.Cu rectangles `QSPI [199,112,204.5,119.5]` and `XTAL [208,112,214,119.5]` had raw widths of 5.500 mm / 12 slots versus six QSPI demand and 5.545 mm / 12 exploratory slots versus two crystal nets (`R_XTAL_FB` nicks the latter). These are rectangle diagnostics only, **not** valid face-to-clock-cell reservations. A new shared-zone or P2 source design must preserve local supply/return access, then establish actual region-face segments before these capacities can count. QSPI still must reach `U_FLASH` near x=216,y=124, while oscillator support occupies x≈205..211,y≈118..130. Their local F.Cu paths may cross; test a true split clock cell or an explicit layer transition and return before claiming coexistence. The rejected seven-pose service trial is not adopted.
2. **Resolve the analog south-face strips jointly with AUDIO_EN.** A face-aligned 6-mm band is geometrically possible after relocations but has 0/1 current raw slots. `AUDIO_EN` is distributed from `quiet_power` (south at y=105) to all eight analog cells (south y=84), a 21-mm source-cell separation at the first channel. It cannot be represented by the earlier `control_cell [145,85,183,91]` without a >100-mm witness bridge. Allocate explicit enable bus ports/branch ownership; if enable shares either analog strip, test at least the nine analog slots **plus** its separate demand on the common edge. Do not credit the optimistic ten-slot potential until all current obstacles and layer/reference choices are resolved.
3. **Redesign ADC timing/control ownership, and power by layer/role.** The x=145 trim is rejected because it cuts ADC and analog-channel-6 full-footprint bounds. The proposed `control_cell [145,85,183,91]` has only 0.875 mm / **1 current raw slot** against seven named control obligations, despite 13-slot potential after movable parts relocate. The ADC common and TDM west diagnostics separately have 2 versus 3 and 3 versus 4 current slots. Establish a source-owned shared zone and P2 block-access/proximity obligations before assigning any x=145 virtual face or measured branches; `AUDIO_EN` also needs its distributed analog/quiet-power plan. Power windows can spatially overlap signals because they denote current/return regions, but the schema-2 same-layer reservation screen must not turn an F.Cu power-area bbox into exclusive signal capacity. Keep rail entries at the digital/XU x=185 face and prove copper/current, thermal, and filled reference later.

Only J1..J8, `J_PWR`, `J_USB`, `J_JTAG`, and C_HOLD1..16 are P1-fixed in the reviewed source authority. XU, USB/PLL electronics, decouplers, ADC/timing parts and clock/flash support are source-anchored or floated **P2-movable by governed source revision**, not free space on the current saved board. The 27-ref fixed-set check cannot substitute for full-footprint clearance of those parts. Region-edge changes, shared-capacity changes, and any revised fixed mechanical face require new board/source hashes and independent P1 review. The raw slot measurements above use optimistic footprint/pad rectangles; no route, native filled return, connector FULL, or acceptance is asserted.
