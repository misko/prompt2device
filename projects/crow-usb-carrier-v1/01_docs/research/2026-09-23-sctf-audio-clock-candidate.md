The current Abracon ASFL1-24.576MHZ-EC-T / C17566269 had 86 units in the D7 review, below five boards plus 150 reserve (155). JLC's live component API returned 1,071 stock and 1,071 presale units on 2026-09-23. Raw response: `/tmp/crow-audio-osc-raw/C2901534.json`; recheck at order staging.

Primary manufacturer evidence is [SCTF exact-part approval specification B01, SCTF20215M027](https://static.chipdip.ru/lib/727/DOC043727753.pdf), local `02_parts/SX5M24.576M20F30TNN/SCTF20215M027.pdf`, SHA-256 `c10be001568c12e27692d77260f88cd6cd55611b92835a34a2031d98da835804`. The exact-part document shows 24.576000 MHz, 1.62–3.63 V CMOS output, 15 pF output-load condition, 10 mA maximum supply current, 45–55% duty, 5 ns maximum rise/fall, VOH >= 0.9 VDD, VOL <= 0.1 VDD, ±20 ppm at 25 °C and ±30 ppm temperature drift over -40 to +85 °C. OE at pin 1 enables when high or open and disables when low; the authored pin 1 tie to 3V3X stays valid. Pins 2, 3, 4 are GND, output and VDD. The existing 10 nF C_AUDIO_OSC matches the p.4 test-circuit bypass value. Existing [Abracon ASFL1 specification](https://abracon.com/Oscillators/ASFL1.pdf) has 24.576 MHz 3.3 V HCMOS, matching functions, 15 pF load, but 15 mA max current, 40–60% symmetry, 10 ns max edges, and ±50 ppm selected stability.

Physical top view is 4/3 above 1/2 for **both** parts. SCTF p.4 specifies 5.0 x 3.2 mm body, 2.54 x 2.30 mm land-center pitch, 1.8 x 1.3 mm lands; the old Abracon lands were 2.54 x 2.20 mm, 1.7 x 1.5 mm. The new TSX footprint uses Y-up coordinates with pin 1 at (-1.27,-1.15); native KiCad uses Y-down with pin 1 at (-1.27,+1.15). The three other pins follow 2 right/lower, 3 right/upper, 4 left/upper in physical top view. A new native footprint and selected BOM identity are source-owned; the historical Abracon dossier is retained.

The oscillator still drives the same two clock inputs via the authored 3.3 V buffer path: CS5308P MCLK and XU316 X1D11. No topology, rail, pin or net changes are proposed. The exact SCTF approval spec does not give a phase-jitter value. Abracon's 1 ps RMS line is marked reference-only and says contact Abracon for specific frequencies, so it was not a guaranteed system limit. Clock phase/noise performance, load on final routes, and edge integrity remain first-article SI/audio measurements; this candidate must not be described as measured equivalent. The board-level ADC/XMOS clock timing and firmware gates remain as before.
