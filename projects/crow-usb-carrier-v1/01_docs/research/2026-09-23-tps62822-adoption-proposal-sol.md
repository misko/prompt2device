# Crow TPS62822DLCR three-rail source-design proposal (read-only, 2026-09-23)

**Disposition.** Replace the three `TPS62825DMQR` buck *source identities and 6-pin DMQ lands* with exact `TPS62822DLCR` / JLC C473385 and a new TI DLC0008B 8-pin land pattern. Retain each 0.47-µH Coilcraft `XFL4015-471MEC`, 47-µF Murata output MLCC, 120-pF feedforward capacitor, and the current feedback ratios. Change **only** the 3V3X 453-kΩ top resistor's exact MPN to stocked same-size Viking `ARG03BTC4533` / C2686428, and change the six 0805 input capacitors to exact Samsung `CL21A106KOQNNNE` / C1713, subject to the source E-CAP screening and first-article limits below. Retain all three 100-kΩ bottom resistors, the 1V8 210-kΩ top and CORE 49.9-kΩ top. This is a source-design candidate, not an electrical or PCB qualification; no source, PCB, firmware, or order files were edited. [TI TPS6282x Rev C](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62823.pdf), [TI TPS62825 Rev I](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62825.pdf).

## Actual hard rails versus the present authored estimates

The current `power_tree.yaml` windows **3.243–3.394 V**, **1.822–1.899 V**, **0.88–0.92 V** are rounded calculations from the old TPS62825 VFB extrema, exact dividers, opposite ±0.1%/±25-ppm/°C resistor corners over 100°C and ±50-nA FB leakage. They are source engineering *DC setpoint envelopes*, **not** the XMOS/flash manufacturer's permitted operating ranges. The primary XU316 data sheet retained in the project (`02_parts/XU316-1024-TQ128-C24/XM-014532-PC-v2.0.0.pdf`, §15.2) states tile/PLL **0.855–0.945 V**, 1V8 I/O/USB **1.62–1.98 V**, and 3V3 I/O **2.97–3.63 V**, USB 3V3 **3.00–3.60 V**. The exact [Winbond W25Q128JW Rev H](https://www.winbond.com/hq/product/code-storage-flash-memory/serial-nor-flash/?__locale=en&partNo=W25Q128JW) `02_parts/W25Q128JWSIQ/part.yaml` requires **1.70–1.95 V**; it narrows the shared 1V8 rail. CORE PLL narrows 0V9 to 0.855–0.945 V. At the 3V3 source rail, the XU316 USB 3.00–3.60 V constraint is controlling. These are at the *device pins*, including DC, ripple, transient and ground-return error.

The old dividers are deliberately preserved because the 1V8 `210k/100k` ratio gives a worst source DC low **1.82216 V**, 32.9 mV above the existing `U_1V8_OK` worst **1.7893-V rising release**. Retuning it to nominal 1.800 V with `200k/100k` would give a DC low ~1.76368 V, below the existing supervisor’s worst 1.7893-V rising-release threshold; reset release at that adverse regulator corner cannot be guaranteed without a separate supervisor redesign. Retuning merely to `205k/100k` gives ~1.79292 V low, only 3.6 mV above that release threshold. Keeping 210k leaves **51.9 mV** between the 1.89808-V DC high and flash 1.95-V hard high. The earlier independently reviewed **40-mV combined overshoot, ripple and ground-shift allocation** leaves ~11.9 mV at the flash high; this is a source allocation to verify on the first article, not a converter guarantee. The other at-pin source budgets can be ≤100 mV combined positive/negative dynamic error for 3V3 (still ~107 mV upper /143 mV lower hard margin) and ≤20 mV for CORE (still ~12 mV upper /11 mV lower hard margin). Their exact waveform and measurement limits must be recorded before release. 1V8 negative headroom to the flash 1.70-V minimum is ~122 mV; the supervisor falling-trip and propagation behavior must separately be observed in brownout.

## New source footprint, courtyard and model basis

[TI Rev C DLC0008B package outline and example board layout, PDF pp.29–31](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62823.pdf) give an **8-pin 2.0×1.5-mm VSON-HR, 0.5-mm pitch, no exposed center pad**. Pin functions are `1 EN`, `2 FB`, `3 AGND`, `4 NC`, `5 PGND`, `6 SW`, `7 VIN`, `8 PG`. With pin 1 at *top left* and coordinate y positive upward, use four left pads at **x=–0.65 mm**, y=+0.75,+0.25,–0.25,–0.75 mm for pins 1–4; right pads **x=+0.65**, y=–0.75,–0.25,+0.25,+0.75 mm for pins 5–8. TI's example land is **0.60×0.25 mm** with 0.05-mm corner radius and 0.25-mm copper gap along rows. Explicit F.Cu/F.Paste/F.Mask on all eight, no B.Cu, no mask/paste rotation or custom pad expansion. Pin 3 AGND should return quietly to the local ground by the FB divider; pin 5 PGND takes the input/output power return; join on the ground plane consistent with TI §10 layout. Pin 4 NC remains unconnected; pin 8 PG remains open, as TI permits. Bind those exact connections in `crow_usb_digital.tsx`, symbol, `part.yaml`, schematic parity and native footprint. No via in any pad is implied. The existing `DMQ0006ALand()` is electrically and mechanically wrong for DLC.

A sensible native courtyard is at least **2.4×2.6 mm**: the ±0.95-mm x pad edges need 0.25 mm each side, while the maximum 2.1-mm body length needs 0.25 mm at each end. If the pin-1 silk marker protrudes, extend the courtyard to enclose it; the installed KiCad `Package_SON:Texas_VSON-HR-8_1.5x2mm_P0.5mm` reference uses a wider ~2.76×2.5-mm shape (its 2.5-mm length is smaller than the 2.6-mm maximum-body-plus-clearance target here) and a 0.80×0.25-mm pad, which is **not the TI 0.60-mm pad example**. Own and verify a project-native TI geometry rather than blindly using that KiCad footprint. TI's outline gives body x **1.4–1.6 mm**, y **1.9–2.1 mm**, z **≤1.0 mm**. A conservative 1.6×2.1×1.0-mm bounding STEP generated from these published maxima can serve collision checking, explicitly tagged as drawing-derived; the installed KiCad STEP reference is absent in this machine, so do not claim a verified vendor 3D model. Check component rotation/centroid against exact JLC C473385 before PCBA export. A native footprint/pad-pair and courtyard check is required for the new package; ordinary board design-rule floors remain unchanged.

## LC, capacitor and sequence transfer

TI TPS6282x Rev C Table 3 checks **0.47 µH + 47 µF nominal** for stability and recommends FF for large COUT. Its table 2 uses Coilcraft XFL4015-471ME-family 0.47 µH and 120-pF FF; Eq.5 yields 120 pF for the retained 100-kΩ lower resistor. The current `XFL4015-471MEC` C18221164 has 2,793 public units and the exact 120-pF `CC0402JRNPO9BN121` C106996 has 212,877. TI requires **≥5 µF effective output**, while existing Murata `GRM32ER71A476KE15L` 47-µF bank estimates are 11.48/12.95/12.95 µF after the project's bias/tolerance/lifecycle screen, with C84494 stock 20,757. This is stronger cap headroom than TPS62825's ≥10-µF output requirement, but neither TI's table nor Murata's typical model proves assembled loop/transients over PVT. Retain the exact inductor only after checking hot/current-biased inductance and peak against TPS62822 **2.7-A minimum** high-side limit; the prior 2.2-MHz typical / 0.329-µH engineering ripple/peak screen is about 1.59/1.80 A at 3V3, 1.65/1.13 A at 1V8, and 1.04/1.72 A at CORE (recalculate exact hot L and frequency; typical fSW is not a minimum). TI's 3-A sibling `TPS62823DLCR` C2693497 stock 7,232 is a pin/BOM compatible reserve, but not needed by these static estimates.

TI requires **≥3 µF effective input**, same as TPS62825; both TI datasheets recommend 4.7 µF nominal near VIN/PGND for typical use. Old Samsung `CL21A106KOCLRNC` C318695 has only **112** units, below six-per-board threshold **180**. Use same-footprint 0805 10-µF/16-V/X5R/±10% Samsung **`CL21A106KOQNNNE` C1713**, public stock **83,222**. Samsung's exact [KOQNNN family page](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL21A106KOQNNN.do) lists this E packaging code as mass production and includes typical curves. At 5.13 V, direct interpolation of its DC-bias array gives **47.21%** retention; at the page's 0.01-V low-AC point the separate AC curve gives **70.55%**. Product **33.31%** is *not a joint PVT guarantee*. Use a more adverse **30% combined engineering retention**, plus the present –10% initial, –15% temperature, –10% lifecycle factors, to calculate `2×10×.30×.90×.85×.90=4.131 µF` estimated per input bank, exceeding 3 µF. The curve is typical 25°C/1-kHz and the combined allowance is selected, not characterized. Update exact cap MPN, E-CAP evidence and all six placement/manifest references; retain the same 0805 lands. Verify measured input ripple/startup/current and exact lot capacitance before first-article acceptance; the X5R part is rated only to +85°C, so local capacitor temperature under the 70°C ambient/enclosure case must remain within that rating. Raw official Samsung HTML and JLC JSON are retained with this memo.

`CORE_EN` continues from `U_1V8_OK`; the TPS62822 EN high/low guaranteed thresholds **1.0/0.4 V** match the old device's levels, but supervisor output and pull-up timing must be recomputed with its leakage and the actual 5V_BUCK ramp. The new regulator provides active discharge through SW while VIN stays present, with **75-mA minimum / 400-mA typical** at VSW=0.4 V over the stated table range. The old TPS62825 publishes the same minimum/typical point; behavior at CORE voltages other than that test point and the complete rail-decay time still require board-level proof. TI documents prebiased startup and ~**1.25-ms typical** soft start, same typical class as the old part; these are not max sequence times. Preserve external `U_1V8_OK → CORE_EN → U_CORE` and `U_CORE_OK` / `U_XU_3V3_OK` wired-reset sequencing, and first-article scope monotonic ramp, 1V8/3V3 validity before reset release, CORE decay, rapid restart, VIN-first collapse, 3V3-versus-VDDIOB18 powered-core exception, flash readiness, and hot/low-VIN brownout. XMOS's §14 requires monotonic supplies and 1V8 I/O valid before reset release; its 3V3 I/O must not exceed the 1.8-V-mode absolute 1.98 V while VDDIOB18 is absent and core is powered.

## Thermal comparison and release record

At 70°C project ambient and 125°C TI operating-junction ceiling, TI gives **JEDEC θJA 114.1°C/W** for DLC and **129.5°C/W** for accepted DMQ TPS62825. At max 3V3X output power 3.394 W, their *JEDEC-board proxy* package-loss allowances are **0.482 / 0.425 W**, requiring η **≥87.56% / ≥88.88%** respectively. If the source's **85% parent-current planning allocation** were taken as actual loss, the estimates would be **138.3 / 147.6°C**. Thus the new package proxy is less restrictive, but neither existing nor proposed device is thermally qualified by a generic 85% allowance. The DLC EVM θJA 69.9°C/W and DMQ EVM 71.4°C/W are board-specific, not Crow guarantees. Do not require a pre-prototype efficiency measurement for the *source-design commit* if the old accepted source remained open here; record first-article `TJ≤125°C` under simultaneous max 3V3/1V8/CORE load, 5V_BUCK extremes, 70°C ambient/enclosure, and voltage bounds at the pins. Before PCB release, a board-specific thermal/loss estimate and thermal copper/layout check should show plausible margin; if not, enlarge copper or choose a different family member/topology rather than declaring the parent 85% budget a guarantee.

For implementation review, update the new exact part dossier and TI PDF checksum, native footprint/model provenance, TSX/manifest/exact-parts/JLC identities, all three buck pin maps, capacitor and resistor dossiers, E-CAP source, `power_tree.yaml` derived-bound commentary, sourcing and all affected parity/gates. Regenerate and run E-TOPO, E-CAP, E-MARGIN, BOM/stock, footprint/courtyard, KiCad schematic/netlist and native DRC checks on the *composed* root before source acceptance. Those checks can validate source consistency and CAD geometry; assembled hot/sequence/ripple performance remains a first-article gate. Public stock is a dated screen, not order-time supply acceptance.
