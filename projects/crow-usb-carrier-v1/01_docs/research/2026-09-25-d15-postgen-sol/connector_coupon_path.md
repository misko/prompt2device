# Read-only path to a full-outline six-hole connector coupon

No coupon was generated by this note. The D15 3313A candidate has
`FAILED_RESEARCH` status and is not a fabrication or connector-FULL source.

The reusable deterministic extraction pattern is
[`connector_fit_prototype.py`](../connector_fit_prototype.py): it pins a source
board SHA, duplicates native connector footprint instances and four Edge.Cuts
lines, writes in a temporary directory, canonicalizes ordering and cloned
UUIDs, then reloads and compares every pad geometry and pose. Its current
hard assertions cover **11 connectors only**, a 220 × 120 mm exploratory
outline, and 1.63-mm nominal thickness. It has no holes, neighboring
component envelopes, or full process stack; its saved board is nonqualifying.

The relevant six-hole geometry is already pinned, read-only, by
[`extract_geometry.py`](../2026-09-25-expanded-locked-full-outline-mechanical-article-terra/extract_geometry.py)
and [`native_geometry.json`](../2026-09-25-expanded-locked-full-outline-mechanical-article-terra/native_geometry.json)
to expanded-locked board `fe8d2c9a9922eeab2371d0187da9407ac590687a77b03a8a099c35a75b5ddd16`:
outline `[10,20,240,150]` mm (230 × 130), four copper layers,
11 connector poses, and H1 `(30,40)`, H2 `(130,40)`, H3 `(211.5,45)`,
H4 `(18,82)`, H5 `(130,142)`, H6 `(230,130)`, each
`MountingHole:MountingHole_3.2mm_M3`. The expanded-locked nominal board
target is 1.63 mm; the unaccepted D15 trial changes that target to 1.58 mm.
The earlier USB4215-only registration coupon targets 1.60 mm and is not a
full-field or stack-equivalent substitute.

The smallest deterministic *datum-only* PCB representation would duplicate
the exact 11 connector and six hole footprint instances (17 total), the
four native outline lines, and controlled board stack/setup from a future
approved source. Extend the existing canonicalization assertions from 11 to
17 footprints, add six-hole identity/pose/pad-drill checks, and pin source,
library, process and output hashes. Retain both declared simultaneous
connector populations from `rules/connector_assemblies.yaml`. Do not infer
process equivalence from the current prototype's layer-count/thickness copy:
its extractor does not preserve the entire dielectric/copper/mask stack or
project rules.
The physical article can remain electrically unrouted and unpowered, but a
governed process specification must retain representative four-layer
laminate/copper, connector-land finish, mask, routed-edge/drill process and
assembly profile; omitting them would change stiffness and connector-joint
behavior.

That 17-footprint core is **insufficient for connector FULL**. The article
must also reproduce all populated-neighbor mechanical obstruction relevant to
grip, latch, cable, bend, assembly and both simultaneous service groups;
the read-only six-hole packet records only each connector's three nearest
nominal courtyards. A conservative implementation would clone all 569
electrical footprint bodies/courtyards or approved body surrogates from the
same exact board, while deliberately leaving the article unpowered and
separately proving those surrogates' dimensions. It must preserve the six
mounting restraints and use an approved fixture/enclosure/cable drawing;
nominal courtyard boxes alone cannot grade reaction or access.

## Can the expanded-locked board be the deterministic source?

**Yes for research geometry extraction; no for manufacturing input under
D14.** Its hash-pinned KiCad file and copied floorplan are enough to generate
and verify a per-ref mechanical manifest for the 569 electrical footprints
plus six holes. The board has 11 actual connector refs and therefore 558
other electrical-footprint refs. A source extractor should record for each
ref its exact footprint-library identity, native XY/rotation/side,
F.Fab/F.CrtYd polygons, pad and hole geometry, 3D-model reference when
available, and an explicit `body_height_unverified` flag until part drawings
or measured assemblies bind height. Compare all 575 refs and four Edge.Cuts
segments on reload; refuse extra/missing refs, pose drift, and any missing
declared component body. The existing expanded-locked
`extract_geometry.py` pins only the 11 connectors, six holes and three
nearest nominal courtyards per connector, so extending it to the entire
population is required.

The copied `rules/assembly.yaml` names 24 `user_supplied` refs: J1–J8 and
`C_A1N/P` through `C_A8N/P`. They are **fitted after JLC assembly**, not
design-DNP bodies; the full-field mechanical population must represent them.
The current file declares no `dnp_by_design` row. `not_assembled` therefore
cannot be interpreted as a permission to remove those 24 bodies from a
physical-access coupon. For a conservative test article, use all 569 actual
fitted bodies at exact poses. A smaller article could use controlled
non-electrical dummies for some of the 558 nonconnector refs only after a
3D service/fixture-envelope exclusion audit proves each omitted ref cannot
affect any of the 19 targets. Each retained dummy needs a bounded maximum
XY/height/keepout, pose, attachment and process behavior; connector joints
themselves must use the actual parts. The three-nearest 2D courtyard lists
are insufficient to pick that minimum.

Safe preparatory outputs from this private board are a hash-bound JSON/CSV
population and geometry manifest, marked PDF/SVG mechanical review views,
and a nonmanufacturing 3D envelope model with unknown heights flagged.
Keep them under `01_docs/research`, with source hashes and `NO_FAB` status;
do not publish a `.kicad_pcb` coupon, Gerber, drill, NC, solder-stencil,
BOM/CPL, pick-and-place, or order package from the D14 board. The 1.63-mm
KiCad thickness remains a nominal target, not a controlled finished stack,
edge process, board stiffness or assembly profile. A later authorized
article must bind a new approved source/process and independently verify
parity against this research manifest if its geometry is carried forward.

A future separate decision would have to name an accepted exact board and
stack/process, authorize a narrowly bounded unpowered article and its
fabrication payload, and bind mates, cables, enclosure, restraint/load cases,
measurement uncertainty and numeric acceptance limits. D11's 19 targets,
base PASS and zero unknowns remain the connector-FULL threshold; D13/D14
grant no current fabrication or order authority. This note makes no coupon,
qualification, ESD, P1/P2/P3, release or order claim.
