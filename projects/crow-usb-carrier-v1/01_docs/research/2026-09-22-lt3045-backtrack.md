# LT3045 quiet-regulator backtrack

Status: adopted into Crow USB source; realized layout, transient measurement, audio qualification, thermal verification, and order-day allocation remain downstream gates.

The unavailable `LT3041ADE#TRPBF` source instance is replaced by exact `LT3045EDD#TRPBF` (JLC `C514420`) in the carrier source. ADI Rev.D is retained under `02_parts/LT3045EDD-TRPBF/` with SHA-256 `9132e595759a58a0920e7c63997872df02ca9ce598ead76c6f7a31051405eba6`. The exact DD package is a 3 mm x 3 mm, 10-lead DFN plus EP. The TSX footprint uses the manufacturer-recommended 0.70 mm x 0.25 mm outer lands, 0.5 mm pitch, and 1.65 mm x 2.38 mm EP. The physical map is 1/2 IN, 3 EN/UV, 4 PG, 5 ILIM, 6 PGFB, 7 SET, 8 GND, 9 OUTS, 10 OUT, EP11 GND.

The candidate uses EN from `LDO_EN`, leaves PG unconnected, Kelvin-senses the output through OUTS, and retains the 33 kohm SET resistor. A new `R_LDO_ILIM=300 ohm` uses the already-documented `RC0402FR-07300RL`; Rev.D specifies 450-550 mA current limit for that value. The 250 mA design allocation therefore has 200 mA guaranteed current-limit margin and 250 mA nameplate capacity margin. Minimum VIN-to-maximum declared VOUT headroom is 0.72 V, leaving 0.27 V beyond the 0.45 V maximum dropout specified even at 500 mA.

The old 2 nF SET network had no specified RMS-noise value in either regulator datasheet. The candidate therefore uses 4.7 uF plus the retained 1 nF capacitor: Rev.D specifies 0.8 uVrms typical from 10 Hz to 100 kHz at 500 mA with 4.7 uF. A 100 kohm / 11 kohm PGFB divider enables fast startup until approximately 3.03 V nominal; resistor and PGFB-trip corners screen to approximately 2.91-3.15 V. ADI's 4.7 uF fast-start example reaches 90% in 10 ms typical, but that example does not guarantee this candidate's ramp. The full startup/hold-up/reset sequence and board-level audio noise remain owed.

The 33 kohm, 0.1% SET resistor gives 3.3 V nominal. Combining full-temperature 98-102 uA SET current, resistor tolerance and +/-2 mV output offset gives a screening range of 3.228766-3.371366 V. The reconciled conservative engineering band is 3.22-3.38 V. CS5308P VDD_A permits 3.13-3.47 V, OPA2320 permits 1.8-5.5 V, and the held supervisors/buffers permit the reconciled band. No user requirement fixes 3.35 V as a hard maximum. The former 3.23-3.35 V declaration was optimistic and was not guaranteed by the LT3041 circuit either.

At 5.05 V input, the declared 3.22 V output floor, and the independently audited 250 mA allocation, pass loss is 457.5 mW before ground current. Adding LT3045's conservative 25 mA maximum ground-current bound (specified at 500 mA) gives 583.75 mW. The source contract assigns a 650 mW board engineering capacity at 70 C ambient, leaving 66.25 mW or 11.35% power reserve and requiring effective theta-JA no greater than 84.6 C/W to remain at or below the 125 C E-grade junction limit. The datasheet's 34-36 C/W DFN measurements assume a 2500 mm2, four-layer, 1 oz internal/2 oz outer test board and cannot be transferred. Native thermal extraction and physical temperature measurement must still prove the realized copper/plane/via geometry, neighboring heat, and junction margin.

E-CAP now screens C_LDO_IN at 11.011 uF effective against the 4.7 uF input minimum. C_LDO_OUT and C_OPA_BULK each screen at 11.011 uF, or 22.022 uF together, against the 10 uF output minimum. The calculation reuses the established exact-part engineering bound: 47 uF, -10% tolerance, 35% voltage retention, -15% temperature, and -12.5% lifecycle. Output ESR below 20 milliohm, ESL below 2 nH, the OUTS/SET/output-cap Kelvin topology, input-loop damping, and load-step behavior remain realized-layout qualifications. At 3.3 V the datasheet states no minimum-load requirement; its 10 uA minimum applies only below 1 V. Shutdown current is specified at no more than 10 uA for E/I grade, but the whole held rail discharge is dominated by the existing loads and dump circuit. The independently audited conservative aluminum hold capacitance is 752 uF and the present timing comparison remains a system transient obligation.

The source candidate is ready for adoption. Downstream qualification remains: measure startup, power loss, dump, reset and isolation ordering; qualify board-level audio noise; realize/review EP11 thermal copper, Kelvin returns and capacitor loops; verify JLC model/rotation/paste; and recheck exact two-pool stock at ordering. This candidate does not authorize generated KiCad, fabrication, ordering, or release.
