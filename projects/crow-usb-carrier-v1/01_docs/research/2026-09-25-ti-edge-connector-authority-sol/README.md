# Fixed north-edge connector authority, exact TI board — research only

The read-only [native census](receipt.json) binds the 569-ref TI board SHA-256 `d0c065dc16de081a5410b7a22e474f0a99c6fdeb0b7dd1f6c37422ace4a9fcf7` and retained manufacturer PDF hashes. Native Edge.Cuts form a closed `[20,20,240,140]` mm outline; their **line-stroke** bounding box begins at y=19.95, whereas the physical board-edge design datum is the y=20 centerline. The census covers fixed `J_PWR`, `J1`–`J8`, `J_USB`, and inboard `J_JTAG`; no pose, source, checker, or board is changed.

For `J_PWR` and every `J1`–`J8`, the outer F.CrtYd polygon begins at y=19.955: **0.045 mm beyond the nominal y=20 edge**. This is an actual nominal courtyard-polygon overlap, not a numerical rounding error. It is **not evidence of a 0.045-mm mechanical body overhang**. The minimum native F.Fab *shape* bound for all nine is y=20.45, 0.45 mm inboard; `J_PWR` has F.Fab annotation text at y=20.35175, which is not shell material. All RJ45 pad bounds begin at y=23.26 and `J_PWR` pads at y=23.60. KiCad's generic `GetBoundingBox(False,False)` begins y=19.975 here because it includes the F.CrtYd graphic; labeling that aggregate as “body” would incorrectly convert drafting geometry into material geometry. The Würth nominal STEP/F.Fab registration and [drawing rev 001.003](https://www.we-online.com/components/products/datasheet/615008160221.pdf) put the nominal RJ45 shell mouth about 0.5 mm inboard. The [Molex 43650 D8 drawing](https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/salesdrawingpdf/436/43650/436501000_sd.pdf) states that the right-angle header must be within 10.16 mm maximum of the PCB edge to avoid receptacle interference; it does not require a body overhang. Its retained F.Fab shapes likewise start 0.45 mm inboard. These are **nominal CAD/drawing facts**, not measured assembled clearances.

`J_USB` is a different edge case: native F.CrtYd starts y=19.45 (0.55 mm beyond the datum), F.Fab stroke starts y=19.95, and pads begin y=21.00. The retained [GCT USB4215 drawing A](https://gct.co/files/drawings/usb4215.pdf) and footprint mark a mouth-at-edge arrangement, but this census does not infer populated-shell registration or grant a similar 0.045-mm exception. `J_JTAG` is wholly inboard (courtyard y≥45.955).

A typed P1 edge-cell exception for `J_PWR` or `J1`–`J8` would have to name only the **exact fixed connector and its courtyard-only north attachment**, not expand generic region capacity or hide a pad/drill. It needs the hash-bound outline centerline, footprint/F.CrtYd/F.Fab and manufacturer drawing/STEP registration; explicit limits for body, mouth, free fingers, pins, shield tabs and locating features; and a signed finished-edge registration receipt from first articles at left/center/right before and after soldering, including uncertainty, part and board lots, finished thickness and routing process. The exception must still verify all pads/drills remain inside valid board material, exclusive inboard owner-cell occupancy and every foreign-region/route/return obligation. A mismatch, stale hash, or body/material overhang outside the approved tolerance must fail closed. The source record cannot treat F.Fab or nominal STEP as a production tolerance envelope. Mated-connector fit, service access and connector FULL remain separate pending evidence.
