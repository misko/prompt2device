# TPS26625 channel-8 UVLO tap: segment-rule proposal only

**Decision.** A static low-current interpretation of `R_SPOKE_UVLO8.1`'s source-side branch is credible, but a reduced-width rule for that branch is **not yet justified**. Preserve `N12V_PROTECTED`'s governed `INPUT_TRUNK` 1.20-mm minimum, 0.20-mm clearance, and 2.85-A source allocation everywhere. The bounded integrated trial [`248360b5`](2026-09-25-ti-tps26625-signal-completion-sol/README.md) connects the UVLO loop but fails native DRC: its 1.20-mm feed track at `(187.3,45.8)` is only 0.0946 mm from `R_SPOKE_UVLO8.2`, where 0.2000 mm is required. This note proposes a future contract and its rejection gates; it is not that contract.

## Electrical bound and its limits

The governed TSX `03_tscircuit/src/crow_retained_analog.tsx` declares `R_SPOKE_UVLO8` as `1M`, with pad 1 on `N12V_PROTECTED` and pad 2 on `SPOKE_UVLO8`, which terminates at `U_SPOKE8.2`. `U_SPOKE8.1` and `.4` and `C_SPOKE_IN8.1` are **other endpoints on the same source net**. The generated `circuit.json` binds the resistor to Yageo `RC0402FR-071ML`. TI [SLVSDT4F](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2662.pdf), §9.3.1 and pin 2 table, explicitly uses a 1-MΩ IN-to-UVLO resistor when UVLO is unused, says the pin must not float, and describes pin-current limiting to <60 µA at its 60-V operating scale. TI lists UVLO leakage ±100 nA for 0–3.5 V and up to 38 µA at 5 V; those pin conditions do **not** bound source-side copper fault current. The retained primary PDF is SHA-256 `ff038eaa557fb618f93c0b6276a5ae08d0d3d9fbff1dddf0c45974a89120aefa`.

[Yageo's exact `RC0402FR-071ML` record](https://www.yageogroup.com/component-documentation/download/specsheet/RC0402FR-071ML) gives 1 MΩ ±1%, ±100 ppm/°C, −55 to +155 °C, and 50-V maximum continuous rated voltage. Combining the adverse initial tolerance and full 130 °C offset from 25 °C gives a conditional `R_min = 1 MΩ × 0.99 × (1 − 0.013) = 977.13 kΩ`. If the protected node is within the project's **13.2-V operating ceiling** and the UVLO pin stays at or above RTN, the **steady resistive** upper bound is `13.2/977.13k = 13.509 µA`. The project's [secondary TVS component screen](../03_src/rules/protection_paths.yaml) uses 24.4 V for its stated 10/1000-µs clamp point; **if** that node actually stays ≤24.4 V during that pulse, the corresponding resistive bound is 24.971 µA. This is not a measured node waveform, a general surge claim, or a bound at 60 V; resistor voltage rating, tolerance, TVS waveform, and UVLO-clamp behavior must be kept distinct.

The source-side copper from a trunk tee to resistor pad 1 is **before** the 1-MΩ element. Normal DC current through a sole resistor load is microamps, but a short to RTN on that pad/stub can draw the external source's prospective fault current without passing through the resistor. Its parasitic capacitance/ESD coupling also draws transient displacement current `C_parasitic × dV/dt` before the resistor; no branch capacitance, edge rate, short-current waveform, via `I²t`, or hot copper rise has been qualified. The 3.4-A/10-ms bounded source episode is itself conditional and does not qualify a thin UVLO stub. The downstream `SPOKE_UVLO8` copper *after* resistor pad 2 is already a separate signal net and does not require weakening the source net.

## Narrowest future topology contract

A future `nets.yaml` segment exception, if justified by fault/thermal evidence, should name **only** `N12V_PROTECTED`'s cut-branch from one native source-trunk tee to `R_SPOKE_UVLO8.1`. It must enumerate its native tracks, vias, and zone contacts on every layer, with an exact source/netlist semantic hash and pad identities. Removing the tee edge from the conductor graph must leave the trunk and every other `N12V_PROTECTED` endpoint connected to the source, while the downstream component contains exactly the resistor's pad 1. It may not contain `U_SPOKE8.1/.4`, `C_SPOKE_IN8.1`, buck/input endpoints, any other spoke, test pad, capacitor, or another trunk path. A zone touch, alternate via path, or changed fanout invalidates the cut. The source node, resistor pad, and each primitive must be checked against native copper, not accepted from a drawn rectangle or route label.

The checker should keep the existing **1.20-mm/0.20-mm trunk rule as default** and permit a narrower primitive only when the entire candidate branch passes that graph proof, normal and worst-fault/transient current envelopes, pad/via/trace hot IR and `I²t`, and source provenance. `min_width`, fault-current waveform, and allowed length remain **unset** in this proposal; a null field fails closed. A generated KiCad rule area or netclass override alone is insufficient because it could cover common copper on the same net. Re-run full native DRC and V-PROCESS with no new issue identity and verify the resistor's post-pad signal net remains `SPOKE_UVLO8`.

Negative controls for a future focused test: (1) add `U_SPOKE8.1` or `.4`, `C_SPOKE_IN8.1`, or another supply pad to the candidate branch; (2) join it to a zone or second tee; (3) shorten or remove `R_SPOKE_UVLO8`, swap its pads/nets, or add a capacitor/test point before it; (4) use a narrow common-trunk segment or narrower via/clearance; (5) omit short-to-RTN, edge-current, or hot `I²t` evidence; (6) change the source/netlist or native track identities while retaining an old receipt. Each must fail before any lower-width DRC override is emitted.

The lower-complexity path is a coupled source-placement recut: place the 1-MΩ resistor close enough to a **full-width** tee that a 1.20-mm source-side feed clears pad 2 and all foreign envelopes, then route only the already separate `SPOKE_UVLO8` net at signal width. This retains the current rule and avoids claiming that a source-side short is microamp-limited. The rejected 0.0946-mm clearance and dVdT first-via 0.055-mm courtyard margin remain explicit constraints for that recut. No P1/P2, process, current, or thermal admission follows.
