**Capability contract for any future part change:** eight simultaneous 48kHz, 24-bit ADC channels are best carried as eight 32-bit TDM slots at 48kHz, giving 12.288MHz bit clock (`8 × 32 × 48k`) and 24.576MHz audio MCLK (2× BCLK). [XMOS lib_xua](https://www.xmos.com/documentation/XM-012296-UG/html/doc/rst/opt_i2s.html) documents eight channels per TDM data line and 16/32-bit words, while its [channel-count guide](https://www.xmos.com/documentation/XM-012296-UG/html/doc/rst/opt_channels.html) documents USB Audio channel configuration; [lib_i2s](https://www.xmos.com/documentation/XM-007055-UG/html/doc/rst/lib_i2s.html) documents the TDM controller API. A candidate must prove physical one-bit port allocation, input timing/format and clock domain, USB Audio2 high-speed packet budget, I²C control for the new quad ADC pair, boot flash and debug, rail sequencing and consumption.
