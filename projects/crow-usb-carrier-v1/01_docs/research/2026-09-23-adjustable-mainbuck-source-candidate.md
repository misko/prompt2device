# Adjustable 5 V main buck: source engineering candidate

The fixed TPSM63603V5RDHR is unavailable in the JLC catalog. This source candidate uses the pin-family adjustable TPSM63603RDHR (C5219327) and TI's 5 V application divider, 40.2 kΩ top (Yageo RT0402BRD0740K2L, C852775) and 10 kΩ bottom (RT0402BRD0710KL, C190095). The FB pin 25 now senses the divider midpoint; all VIN, VOUT, enable, ground, bootstrap, VCC, VLDOIN, PG, and RT connections retain their prior functions. FB routing near the module and AGND return are P3 placement requirements. The native RDH0030A land was copied without geometric change from the fixed-family option. This is a **source engineering candidate**, not a fabricated-board qualification.

Primary evidence is the local TI SLVSFS5A PDF in `02_parts/TPSM63603RDHR/` (sections 6.5, 7.3.2, 8.2.1.2.2, 10; Table 7-1), exact Yageo PDFs in the resistor dossiers, the existing TI TPS62825 SLVSEF9I and ADI LT3045 Rev D dossiers, and `2026-09-22-power-bounds-adoption.md`. ## Voltage and load budget

`power_topology.py --margin` calculates the adverse divider window by using 0.985–1.015 V FB reference, 40.2 kΩ/10 kΩ opposing 0.1% initial tolerance plus 0.25% over a 100 °C resistor excursion, and ±10 nA FB current: **4.91668–5.12437 V**. The declared **4.91–5.13 V** contract encloses it. TI's ±1% FB accuracy is specified at 1 V output, no load and 200 kHz; the extra ±0.5% in this calculation is an explicit project engineering allowance for uncharacterized line/load/temperature change. It is neither a TI all-load guarantee nor a substitute for first-article loaded/ripple/temperature measurement. If that measurement violates 4.91–5.13 V, revise the divider or contract before release.

All three TPS62825 parent VIN rows now use 4.91–5.13 V; their 2.4–5.5 V input window contains it. Their separately screened output envelopes and allocated maximum loads yield `(3.394×1.0 + 1.899×0.3 + 0.92×1.2)/(0.85×4.91) = 1.21426 A` total buck input for digital conversion. Allocate another 0.250 A analog load, 0.025 A adverse LT3045 ground-current allowance, 0.001 A for direct sense/divider loads, and 0.005 A for held controls: **1.49526 A**, leaving **0.40474 A** against the unchanged 1.9 A normal-output allocation. This is a declared consumption envelope, not a simultaneous-startup peak-current proof. The selected 85% converter efficiency is already the source contract; lower measured efficiency requires rerunning the allocation.

The 5.13 V high endpoint is below TPS62825 and TPS389001 5.5 V limits and the 5 V-side capacitor ratings. The 5 V held rail's TMUX and logic see diode-lowered voltage. LT3045 parent VIN is now declared 4.10–5.13 V; at low buck the D_HOLD plus hot Q_PRE combined drop receives only `4.91−4.10 = 0.81 V`, an explicit P3/first-article voltage-drop allocation. The held rail uses 752 µF accepted effective hold bank; this is not an assumption about the local digital rail hold time. The existing PWR_SENSE 30.9 kΩ/10 kΩ and TPS389001 threshold corners give about 4.649–4.758 V trip (plus roughly 3 mV from SENSE current), so the top trip remains at least 0.149 V below the new 4.91 V normal low endpoint. Its CT, reset and held-analog sequence stay unchanged; normal power-down timing remains a waveform qualification item.

## Capacitors, heat, and startup

The three Murata GRM32ER71A476KE15L 5 V output parts estimate **33.31125 µF** effective versus TI's 25 µF minimum after 70% DC-bias derating (30% retained), 10% tolerance and 12.5% lifecycle reserve. The exact Murata simple model previously returned 14.7 µF each at −55 °C/5.099 V, or 31.3%; 30% retained is an adverse model proxy for the 5.13 V ceiling, **not** a Murata guaranteed production minimum. LT3045 input C_LDO_IN similarly estimates 11.10375 µF against 4.7 µF. Each TPS62825 input pair of Samsung 10 µF parts estimates `2×10×0.24×0.9×0.85×0.9 = 3.3048 µF` versus 3 µF. The exact Samsung typical DC/AC curves give 27.14% combined retention at 5.116 V; 24% at the slightly higher 5.13 V ceiling is a selected adverse proxy, not a guaranteed joint PVT/aging value. Output-bank source values and 3V3_ADC effective-cap banks are unchanged; `early_design_check.py --capacitance` passes all 15 banks. First-article ripple, load-step stability and representative lot-characteristic checks remain required.

At 1.9 A, 5.13 V and 85% efficiency, the module dissipates **1.720 W**. At 70 °C ambient and 125 °C junction limit, the selected JLC04161H-7628G four-layer 1 oz board must realize **θJA ≤31.98 °C/W**. TI's 33.5 °C/W test-board figure uses four-layer 2 oz copper and does not establish this board's thermal result. Native layout, hot load testing and actual thermal margin are required; the full 1.9 A allocation must not be silently replaced by the 1.495 A estimate.

The module output and three digital-input banks sum to 201 µF nominal ceramic capacitance, at most 221.1 µF from their +10% tolerances. A uniform 3.5 ms ramp to 5.13 V would average **0.324 A** charge current; TI's 3.5 ms datum is the minimum time to 90% reference, so this is only a charge estimate, not a peak-current bound. The 940 µF nominal held bank is isolated behind 22 Ω precharge, bounding the ideal initial resistor current to 5.13/22 = **0.233 A** before diode drop and giving a 20.68 ms nominal RC time constant. Five constants are **103.4 ms**; at 1.35 µA CT current and 1.17 V threshold, the existing 1 µF C_PWR_CT must retain at least **0.1193 µF** effective to delay Q_PRE that long. CT bias retention, digital-regulator startup charging, converter current limit, hot/low-VIN behavior and rail ordering require first-article waveforms. No switch-current specification is used as a 5 V output-current guarantee.

The source-stage acceptance is limited to the explicit DC engineering screen and budget above. Physical release gates remain: native RDH land/FB/AGND layout, ≤31.98 °C/W thermal realization at full allocated load, 4.91–5.13 V loaded/ripple/temperature window, 0.81 V hot hold-path drop allocation, precharge/reset waveforms, and stable operation with the actual assembled capacitor bank.
