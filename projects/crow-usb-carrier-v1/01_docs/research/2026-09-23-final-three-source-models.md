The original geometry files are covered by repository MIT `LICENSE`; no manufacturer PDF or third-party CAD geometry was copied. Manufacturer drawings below remain dimensional authority.

The Molex model fits inside the source contract's conservative 10.0 mm transverse × 10.25 mm axial × 10.0 mm full header envelope; its 8.87 mm total model Z span includes the below-board peg. The Samtec model fits within the contract's 7×8×7 mm header envelope. Both contract reserves are source planning margins rather than manufacturer tolerance guarantees. Their mate, grip, latch operation, installed cable route and populated-neighbor service remain physical qualification targets. The Molex drawing's 17.56 mm **mated** dimension was not put into the unmated header model. The Samtec `-K` notch, plastic mould profile, curved tails, and manufacturing variation are omitted. TI package corner relief, true terminals and thermal details are omitted.

**Native verification.** `pcbnew.FootprintLoad` opened all 3 edited footprints and found all 42 numbered pads (Molex 2, Samtec 10, TI 30). Parsed VRML primitive bounds, including rotated vertical peg/tail cylinders and model-Y inversion, match all three stated native-frame X/Y/Z envelopes. A separate temporary fixture at `/tmp/crow-final-three-fixture-20260923/` placed each model at 0° and 90° (6 instances); `kicad-cli pcb render` loaded and displayed all six in top and front views, including signed above/below-board geometry. This diagnostic fixture is outside the project and is not a Crow P1 candidate. The footprint diff consists solely of two added model clauses and one replacement of a broken model URI; every pad, silk and courtyard byte is unchanged.

**CAD/provenance limit.** Public Molex STEP link `https://www.molex.com/pdm_docs/stp/43650-0200_stp.zip` redirected to a limited product page, and no exact STEP was retained. Samtec's exact-part site offers CAD behind an email form; none was submitted. TI's RDH CAD is served by Ultra Librarian under separate registration/terms, and TI-hosted forum reports have flagged RDH footprint/model alignment; it was not imported. These original drawing-derived VRML files have no vendor-CAD parity or exact connector mating geometry. Independent `MODEL-SELF`, model-to-board registration, pin-1/connector-mouth review, and full placement/connector gates remain owed on an exact later candidate.
