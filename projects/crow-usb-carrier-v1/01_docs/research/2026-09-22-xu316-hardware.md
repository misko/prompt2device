# Conditional XU316 hardware dossier

Retrieved 2026-09-22. This dossier selects **XU316-1024-TQ128-C24** conditionally for the new Crow carrier. It covers hardware only; it does not author firmware, an XN file, or a binary.

## Concrete topology

```text
externally powered Crow carrier
  5 V local rail
    -> 3V3X buck -> XU316 USB_VDD33 + VDDIOL/R/T + boot flash + clock logic
    -> 1V8 LDO -> XU316 VDDIOB18 + USB_VDD18
    -> 0V9 buck -> XU316 VDD + bead/1 uF -> PLL_AVDD

24.000 MHz crystal -> XU316 XIN/XOUT (system/USB reference)
24.576 MHz oscillator -> low-skew buffer -> CS5308P MCLK and XU316 X1D11
XU316 X1D10 -> buffer -> CS5308P ASP_BCLK, 12.288 MHz
XU316 X1D01 -> buffer -> CS5308P ASP_FSYNC, 48 kHz
CS5308P DOUT1 -> Ioff-capable buffer -> XU316 X1D24
USB-C device receptacle -> ESD/choke as validated -> pins 59/60 USB_DM/DP
USB VBUS -> sense-only no-backpower network -> spare 3.3 V GPIO
```

The XU316 is the USB device and TDM controller. The Pi 5 is the USB host. Local carrier power is independent of Pi VBUS; VBUS is detected only. When local power is absent, nothing may back-power through VBUS, D+/D-, TDM, or GPIO. When VBUS is absent, firmware must leave the USB PHY detached even if local rails remain on.

## Audio pins and timing

| Role | Physical pin | GPIO / 1-bit port | Domain | Electrical direction |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 24.576 MHz MCLK | 23 | X1D11 / P1D0 | IOL = 3.3 V | input to XU316 |
| 48 kHz frame sync | 20 | X1D01 / P1B0 | IOL = 3.3 V | output from XU316 |
| 12.288 MHz bit clock | 22 | X1D10 / P1C0 | IOL = 3.3 V | output from XU316 |
| eight-slot data | 107 | X1D24 / P1I0 | IOT = 3.3 V | input to XU316 |

These are not arbitrary GPIO choices. The official XK-AUDIO-316-MC manual maps X1D11/P1D0 to MCLK, X1D01/P1B0 to LRCK, X1D10/P1C0 to BCLK, and X1D24/P1I0 to its first TDM-capable ADC input. All resources are on tile 1 and are independent 1-bit ports.

The wire format is eight 32-bit slots at 48 kHz: `8 * 32 * 48,000 = 12.288 MHz`. Each slot carries 24 meaningful MSBs. Configure CS5308P minimum-slot TDM with DOUT1 carrying channels 1–8 in slots 0–7. The compatible contract is active-high FSYNC, one BCLK-wide pulse, slot 0 beginning one bit clock after the rising FSYNC edge. CS5308P permits the falling FSYNC edge after at least one BCLK and requires it before the next frame rising edge by at least one BCLK. XMOS lib_i2s exposes FSYNC offset 1 and length 1 and documents the 8 x 32, 48 kHz / 12.288 MHz combination. Firmware authorization is still required to bind those settings and the physical ports into the XN/build.

## Package, footprint, and escape

The official package is a 14 x 14 mm TQFP-128 on 0.40 mm pitch, 16 x 16 mm nominal lead span, with a 4.70 mm nominal exposed VSS paddle designated physical pad 129. Pin 55 is NC in Figure 2 and must remain unconnected. The allocated CSV contains one row for each of pins 1–128 plus EP/pad 129.

XMOS gives package tolerances and directs designers to IPC-7351B; it does not publish one universal land pattern. The source-owned candidate footprint adds the exposed pad and a typical 4 x 4 thermal-via grid to gull-wing geometry. It is suitable for schematic/placement development but requires assembly-house confirmation of toe/heel/side fillets, paste segmentation, via fill/cap, and courtyard before release. No primary STEP model was found, so none is claimed.

`escape_check.py --style leaded --pitch 0.4 --escapes-worst-side 32 --pins 128` reports `jlc_4layer_advanced` as the minimum unconditional escape tier. Two-layer and standard four-layer are conditional on a proven escape corridor. Use six layers if the full board routing/plane strategy needs it; this dossier does not claim that package escape alone mandates six layers.

## USB and fixed-voltage caution

Pins 59/60 are the package-fixed USB D-/D+ pair. Route as 90 ohm differential over an unbroken reference, tightly coupled, with no stubs and as few vias as possible; XMOS specifies pair skew no more than 1 mm. Pins 61/62 require 3.3 V/1.8 V USB supplies.

The bottom I/O domain is **always 1.8 V**. It contains XIN/XOUT (33/34), TDI/TDO/RST_N/TMS/TCK (36/37/38/44/51), the LV_L_N/LV_T_N/LV_R_N selectors (40/43/52), and six GPIOs. Use a 1.8 V XTAG/JTAG interface and a 1.8 V reset pull-up/output. Float each LV_x_N input to select 3.3 V for its corresponding L/T/R domain, or ground it to select 1.8 V. Never strap those selector pins to 3.3 V.

## Boot, reset, and clocks

Boot QSPI pins are ROM-fixed: X0D01 pin 2 CS_N, X0D04 pin 127 D0, X0D05 pin 128 D1, X0D06 pin 1 D2, X0D07 pin 3 D3, and X0D10 pin 4 clock. They share VDDIOL, selected here as 3.3 V. The flash must accept normal QSPI boot from power-up and be ready within 300 us, or RST_N must remain low longer. Do not leave the flash in QPI mode.

Use the XMOS-recommended **Epson FA-238 24.0000MD30X-W5** crystal: 24 MHz, 12 pF load, <=60 ohm ESR, with 1 Mohm feedback, 680 ohm drive resistor, and 22 pF load capacitors as the datasheet example. The oscillator must run before reset releases.

For audio, **ASFL1-24.576MHZ-EC-T** is a concrete 3.3 V HCMOS candidate and is the same oscillator family used in earlier XMOS audio hardware. Fan it to CS5308P and X1D11 with a low-skew 3.3 V buffer and source damping determined from first-layout SI review. Its -20 to +70 C grade matches the C24 XU316 commercial range; select a wider-temperature suffix if board requirements exceed that.

Because IOL/R/T are 3.3 V while IOB is 1.8 V, the simple internal-POR condition does not apply. Hold pin 38 RST_N low until 0.9 V, 1.8 V, and all 3.3 V VDDIO domains are valid. **TPS386000RGPR** is a concrete active, four-channel, open-drain supervisor candidate; divider thresholds and reset delay must be calculated in the schematic. Pull its combined reset output up only to 1.8 V.

## Power, decoupling, and budget

XMOS requires monotonically rising rails, recommends that VDD and VDDIO ramps finish within 50 ms, requires VDDIOL/R/T valid before reset release, and warns that VDDIOB18 must not be the last rail when 3.3 V VDDIO exists. Follow the reference-board precedent: establish 3V3X and 1V8 before enabling 0V9, then release external reset only after every domain is valid.

| Rail | XU316 datasheet load | Design allowance | Candidate |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 0V9 VDD | 225 mA typical, 830 mA max at C24/I24; plus HS USB VDD increment up to 9 mA and PLL | 1.2 A minimum; thermal design against 0.75 W XU core maximum | ST1S32PUR, adjustable 4 A buck |
| 1V8 | USB PHY 30 mA typ / 36 mA max in HS; bottom I/O switching not numerically specified | 0.30 A pending pin-load model | TLV75518PDBVR, fixed 500 mA LDO |
| 3V3X | USB PHY 0.8 mA typ / 1 mA max in HS plus three GPIO banks, flash, clocks/buffers | 1.0 A pending GPIO load census | second ST1S32PUR set to 3.3 V |
| PLL 0V9 | 5 mA typical | included in 0V9 budget | 600 ohm @100 MHz bead, DCR <1 ohm, then 1 uF |

The 225/830 mA core numbers are datasheet characterization, not a prediction of this firmware. A final typical rail estimate requires XMOS AN02023 with the authorized task/core/clock configuration. Regulator losses and junction temperature require the actual 5 V input tolerance, copper area, airflow, and simultaneous ADC load. Do not treat the allowances as a completed thermal analysis.

Place at least twelve 100 nF low-inductance capacitors around VDD, one 100 nF at every VDDIO supply pin, and at least 10 uF bulk on VDD and every VDDIO supply. Put USB rail bypass at pins 61/62. Put 1 uF immediately after the PLL bead at pin 41. Tie unused MIPI_VDD09 and MIPI_VDD18 to ground as XMOS instructs.

## Sourceability and firmware gate

The chosen XU316 exact part was observed at DigiKey and Mouser and has a JLC catalog entry. The reference board proves the silicon, USB PHY, QSPI boot, tile-1 audio port family, and multichannel hardware arrangement. The official reference-design ZIP and PDF download endpoints returned HTTP 406 in this bounded run; only the official HTML manual was usable, so the dossier does not claim a pinned ZIP hash.

No vendor precompiled image targets this Crow pinout. Before schematic pin freeze, authorization is required for a board-specific XMOS build containing an XN port map, eight-input/zero-output UAC2 descriptors, 48 kHz restriction, TDM offset/length, VID/PID identity, VBUS sensing, reset/power hooks, and flash image. That dependency does not invalidate the hardware choice, but it is a real freeze gate. Later bench qualification should verify the 1-BCLK FSYNC pulse, one-bit data offset, slot/channel order, all 24 meaningful bits, USB suspend/detach, and ALSA capture; purchasing an evaluation board is not a prerequisite to begin the board design.
