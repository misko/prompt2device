# Crow three-rail replacement screen — TLV62569, TPS62A02, and stocked TPS6282x DLC

**Scope and verdict (2026-09-23, read-only).** `TLV62569PDDCR` / JLC `C398365` is **FAIL for direct substitution on all three rails** despite clearing the DMQ land-spacing problem and the 165-piece public stock threshold. The 2-A, 2.5–5.5-V input headline covers the nominal voltage/current envelope, but the retained 0.47-µH / 47-µF / 120-pF network is outside its validated LC/compensation choices and the present resistor networks fail the authored DC windows under the same adverse resistor screen. No manufacturer-guaranteed full-temperature TLV FB leakage or loaded-output bound was found. Its data sheet does not specify active output discharge, which the present TPS62825 provides. Its 3V3X hot thermal result is unresolved.

`TPS62822DLCR` / JLC `C473385` is now the **first source-redesign candidate, not an admitted replacement**: the exact reel suffix has public stock 1,979, and 3-A sibling `TPS62823DLCR` / `C2693497` has 7,232. TI explicitly validates the 0.47-µH / 47-µF combination, recommends 120-pF feedforward with the retained 100-kΩ bottom divider, specifies ±1% FB over –40 to 125 °C, 50-nA maximum FB leakage, active output discharge, and ≥3-µF effective input / ≥5-µF effective output. Project E-CAP estimates exceed these cap minima but are engineering estimates, not guaranteed lot/PVT minima. This family needs a new 8-pin DLC footprint and exact pin map; very small DC window margins and 3V3X thermal remain unproved. `TPS62A02PDDCR` / JLC `C22445521` is a **secondary stocked investigation candidate, not an admitted source replacement**. TI documents the same DDC pin functions as TLV62569PDDCR, 2 A, active output discharge, a 2.7-A minimum high-side current limit, 0.47-µH use, and 591–609-mV FB regulation over the full –40 to 125 °C table (594–606 mV only for 0–125 °C). Fresh public stock is 1,850. It still needs a new DDC footprint, redesigned low-resistance feedback dividers, renewed E-CAP/LC qualification, and especially 3V3X thermal proof. The forced-PWM `TPS62A02APDDCR` / C25687169 has the same electrical/package family and public stock 760; it avoids the unspecified light-load PSM voltage rise but has the usual FPWM light-load power/EMI tradeoff. No source part, footprint, firmware, PCB, order, or supplier record was changed. Public stock is not reservation or order-time acceptance.

## Exact retained contract and primary comparison

The project source is `03_tscircuit/src/crow_usb_digital.tsx` and `03_src/rules/power_tree.yaml` in `/home/mouse9911/gits/circuits-worktrees/crow-usb-carrier-v1-20260922/projects/crow-usb-carrier-v1`. `U_3V3X`, `U_1V8`, `U_CORE` take `N5V_BUCK` at 4.91–5.13 V and must respectively deliver **3.243–3.394 V at 1.0 A**, **1.822–1.899 V at 0.30 A**, and **0.88–0.92 V at 1.2 A**. CORE alone uses `CORE_EN` from `U_1V8_OK`; PG on all present DMQ converters is explicitly unconnected. All three use Coilcraft `XFL4015-471MEC` 0.47 µH ±20%, two Samsung 10-µF input ceramics, one Murata 47-µF output ceramic, and 120-pF feedforward. Source E-CAP estimates **3.3048 µF effective input** per bank and **11.48/12.95/12.95 µF effective output**, respectively; those are conservative engineering estimates from typical capacitor curves and reserves, not guaranteed PVT/lot minima. The present TPS62825 has active SW output discharge when EN is low, provided VIN remains present [TI TPS62825 Rev I, §7.4.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62825.pdf).

[TI TLV62569 Rev C](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv62569.pdf) gives PDDC pins **1 EN, 2 GND, 3 SW, 4 VIN, 5 PG, 6 FB**; 2.5–5.5 V VIN, 0.6 V–VIN VOUT, 2 A IOUT, 106.2 °C/W DDC JEDEC θJA, 1.5-MHz **typical** switching, 900-µs **typical** soft start, 3-A PDDC high-side current limit **typical** at 25 °C, and 0.588/0.600/0.612-V VFB min/typ/max at VIN=5 V/TJ=25 °C. Its table does not state a full-temperature FB leakage bound. EN must not float; core's present reset-driven EN topology can drive it, subject to actual low/high levels, supervisor delay, and prebiased restart. PDDC PG may remain open as TI instructs when unused. The data sheet describes start into a prebiased output but no active shutdown discharge; TI's [product comparison](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV62569) explicitly recommends TPS62A02 for higher output accuracy **with output discharge**. Treat TLV core power-down/restart decay as unbounded until a separate controlled discharge and measured sequence exist.

**TLV filter conflict.** TI Table 4 tests **1 or 2.2 µH**, not 0.47 µH, with output capacitance choices by voltage class and 10–47 µF operating guidance. The 0.9-V class points are 1 µH/2×22 µF and 2.2 µH/100 µF; 1.8-V and 3.3-V classes have other checked 1/2.2-µH cells. Those values are application test evidence, not a universal stability guarantee. TI's example uses 2.2 µH, 4.7-µF input, 10-µF output and optional **6.8 pF** feedforward for 100-kΩ bottom resistance. Retained 0.47 µH/47 µF/120 pF cannot inherit the TPS62825 loop qualification. The current 3.3048-µF input bank also falls below TI's 4.7-µF typical application recommendation; that 4.7 is not labeled a guaranteed universal minimum, but a redesign cannot cite this example as already met. At 47 µF nominal, both TI matrices anticipate at most a 50% effective-C reduction (23.5 µF); the project's 11.48/12.95/12.95-µF screens are substantially below that anticipated value, so even a marked nominal cell would not transfer unchanged. All three E-CAP records must change and actual bank retention, startup/ripple/phase margin must be checked before adoption. [TI TLV62569 Rev C, §8.2.2.2–8.2.2.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv62569.pdf).

## DC feedback screen: present values fail, and retuning TLV alone is insufficient

Use the project's adverse resistor screen: each resistor ±0.1% initial and opposing ±25 ppm/°C ×100 °C TCR, hence top/bottom ratio factors `k_low=0.9965/1.0035`, `k_high=1.0035/0.9965`. For TLV, use TI's **25 °C only** 0.588–0.612-V table endpoints. With FB leakage artificially set to zero (optimistic), the old divider values yield:

| Rail | Retained top/bottom | Optimistic TLV DC range, V | Required, V | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 3V3X | 453 kΩ / 100 kΩ | 3.23306–3.40383 | 3.243–3.394 | Fails both sides |
| 1V8 | 210 kΩ / 100 kΩ | 1.81419–1.90623 | 1.822–1.899 | Fails both sides |
| CORE | 49.9 kΩ / 100 kΩ | 0.87937–0.91953 | 0.880–0.920 | Fails low side |

For *any* nominal divider ratio with the same tolerance/TCR screen and zero leakage, feasible `Rtop/Rbottom` intervals are 3V3X **[4.54702, 4.51404]**, 1V8 **[2.11338, 2.08827]**, CORE **[0.500087, 0.499757]**: each lower bound exceeds its upper bound. This is a mathematical incompatibility with the existing windows even before FB leakage, PSM uplift, ripple, load shift and the lack of full-temperature VFB bounds. Reducing divider impedance cannot repair that tolerance-span result. TLV is therefore unsuitable without changing rail-window requirements or adopting a different accuracy grade; this task authorizes neither. [TI TLV62569 Rev C, §6.5 and §8.2.2.2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv62569.pdf).

## Highest-priority stocked option: TPS62822/23 DLC reel variants

[JLCsearch public raw records](https://jlcsearch.tscircuit.com/components/list) at 18:43 UTC show exact `TPS62822DLCR` **C473385, 1,979 units**, and exact `TPS62823DLCR` **C2693497, 7,232 units**; both exceed the 165-piece threshold. By contrast, `TPS62822DLCT` C2072011 had 38 and `TPS62823DLCT` C2072030 had 72. The `R` and `T` suffixes are packaging variants of the same respective DLC device, but public availability cannot be inferred from one suffix. No quantity is reserved. TI's [TPS6282x Rev C datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62823.pdf), §§5–9, specifies the DLC family as 2.4–5.5 V, adjustable 0.6–4 V, with 2-A TPS62822 and 3-A TPS62823. Both cover the three rail envelopes. The 3-A sibling gives larger 3.7-A minimum high-side current limit versus 2.7 A for the 2-A part, and lower typical high-side FET resistance (26 versus 35 mΩ); the 2-A part already exceeds the engineering static peak estimates. No efficiency/thermal guarantee follows from those typical values.

The DLC 8-pin map is **1 EN, 2 FB, 3 AGND, 4 NC, 5 PGND, 6 SW, 7 VIN, 8 PG**. `AGND` may float per TI but a grounded quiet return is preferable; `NC` may float; PG may float if unused. The present 6-pin DMQ uses 1 EN, 3 FB, 4 GND, 5 SW, 6 VIN; a source pin/footprint replacement and E-TOPO revision are compulsory, including handling separate AGND/PGND. TI states active SW output discharge when EN low and VIN remains present; 75-mA typical at VSW=0.4 V is **not a guaranteed minimum/core decay time**. EN guaranteed high/low thresholds are 1.0/0.4 V, compatible in principle with `CORE_EN` but the actual supervisor waveforms and reset sequence must be measured. TI documents prebiased startup, with ~1.25-ms **typical** soft-start, not a max sequencing bound. PG presently remains unconnected, as allowed by the data sheet.

The primary filter evidence is unusually close to the retained design: TI Table 3 **checks 0.47 µH + 47 µF**, with CFF recommended for large COUT; Table 2 uses a Coilcraft XFL4015-471ME-family 0.47-µH inductor and 120-pF CFF. Equation 5 gives **120 pF** for 100-kΩ bottom resistance, which all three rails retain. TI §9.2.2.6–7 specifies **≥5 µF effective output** and **≥3 µF effective input**. The project's 11.48/12.95/12.95-µF outputs and 3.3048-µF inputs clear those *estimated* minima, but input only by 0.3048 µF and the existing E-CAP estimates derive from typical capacitor curves/reserves. Qualify the exact capacitor MPNs over actual DC bias, temperature, tolerance and age, and verify loop/transients on the board; Table 3's checked nominal cell is not a universal PVT performance guarantee. TI recommends 4.7-µF *nominal* input in its typical application, while its hard text says 3-µF effective minimum. The present 2×10-µF nominal input bank needs evidence of the latter. TI lists the exact output filter family, but no board-level 3V3X/1V8/core transient acceptance is established.

TI specifies FB **594–606 mV over –40 to 125 °C in PWM**, FB input leakage **50 nA max** (screen either direction), 0.2%/A **typical** load regulation and 0.05%/V **typical** line regulation. Applying the same adverse resistor screen to the *present* divider values, with ±50-nA leakage, yields: 3V3X **3.243321–3.393193 V** (nearest window margin **0.321 mV**), 1V8 **1.822162–1.898076 V** (**0.162 mV**), CORE **0.885835–0.913022 V** (**5.835 mV**). Thus the nominal reference screen just fits, but it does **not** prove the authored rail windows: 3V3X/1V8 margins are too small to absorb unspecified PSM uplift, loaded-output error, ripple, line/load shift, FB ground error, ageing or statistical overlap. The data-sheet reference bound is PWM; the device enters PSM at light load. Do not turn the table arithmetic into a false pass. A source adoption may need different, tighter/tracking divider MPNs or an adjusted architecture while retaining the rail windows; ripple and reset rails require measured and budgeted bounds.

For the hot 3V3X rail, DLC JEDEC θJA **114.1 °C/W** gives a 70-to-125 °C dissipation screen of **0.482 W** and required efficiency at 3.394-W output of **≥87.58%**. The present parent 85% allocation would imply ~**138.4 °C** on that JEDEC board. TI also reports 69.9 °C/W on its EVM, demonstrating board dependence, not project qualification. Current DLC-family EVM efficiency curves are typical only. Thermal analysis or measurements on the actual copper/enclosure at hot VIN/load, plus synchronized 1V8 and CORE power-down/restart, remain hard acceptance gates. This conclusion holds even if the 3-A sibling is selected.

## Stocked higher-accuracy option: bounded TPS62A02 screen

[TI TPS62A02 Rev E](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62a01.pdf) lists PDDC pins **1 EN, 2 GND, 3 SW, 4 VIN, 5 PG, 6 FB**, matching TLV PDDC function by pin; the present DMQ map is different. TI's specified VFB is 591–609 mV for –40 to 125 °C PWM, and 594–606 mV for 0–125 °C PWM. The full-temperature line is used here. TI lists FB leakage maximum 100 nA (sign not specified; screen either direction), high-side current limit **minimum 2.7 A**, 1-ms soft start **typical**, and 76-mA output discharge **typical** only at VIN=3 V/VOUT=2 V. DDC JEDEC θJA is 132.1 °C/W. PG is optional, and `CORE_EN` input threshold/actual drive must be checked on the new rail sequence. Output-discharge timing at 0.9 V with actual VIN/cap/load is not guaranteed by the 76-mA typical point. Prebiased startup is documented. PSM `TPS62A02PDDCR` can rise above nominal at light load without a numeric bound; forced-PWM `TPS62A02APDDCR` avoids that mode but needs parent-input, heat and EMI re-screening.

With **candidate** lower-impedance dividers, the same ±0.1%/±25-ppm×100 °C opposing resistor screen and ±100-nA FB leakage gives these *steady PWM reference* bounds (not loaded output guarantees):

| Rail | Candidate top/bottom | Calculated DC screen, V | Window, V | Nearest margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3V3X | 45.3 kΩ / 10 kΩ | 3.245009–3.391695 | 3.243–3.394 | 2.0 mV |
| 1V8 | 10.5 kΩ / 5 kΩ | 1.822389–1.897937 | 1.822–1.899 | **0.39 mV** |
| CORE | 10 kΩ / 20 kΩ | 0.883435–0.916642 | 0.880–0.920 | 3.36 mV |

Formula: `Vmin=0.591×(1+(Rt/Rb)×k_low)−100 nA×Rt×1.0035`, `Vmax=0.609×(1+(Rt/Rb)×k_high)+100 nA×Rt×1.0035`. These are illustrative values, **not selected/stocked resistor MPNs**. 1V8 has essentially no remaining DC shift budget for actual regulation/load/FB-trace error; all three need measured ripple, load-step and start-up allocations. Use lower-tolerance/tracking dividers or revisit device/rail architecture if those margins do not survive. The current 453k/210k/49.9k top and 100k bottom networks and E-TOPO feedback declarations cannot be carried over unchanged. Candidate divider draw is approximately 60/120/30 µA (3V3X/1V8/CORE), versus existing values, and must be added to parent power/load accounting. [TI TPS62A02 Rev E, §6.5 and §8.2.2.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62a01.pdf).

TI allows **0.47 µH** for TPS62A02 with its recommended output-capacitor choices, but its Table 8-3 checked cells are at 1 µH with 10/22/2×22 µF; the present *nominal 47 µF*, typically retained 11.48/12.95/12.95 µF, is not an exact marked 0.47-µH/47-µF cell. TI's broad 10–47-µF output guidance and optional 120-pF 1.8-V feedforward example do not qualify all three current 120-pF compensation networks. TI also describes 4.7-µF input as sufficient for most applications; current E-CAP only estimates 3.3048 µF each. Keep the existing 0.47-µH Coilcraft only as a candidate, recalculate peak ripple and hot inductance, characterize actual input/output ceramics, and prove loop/stability/startup/load-step for each rail. At **2.4 MHz typical** and assumed 0.329 µH effective lower screen, calculated ripple/peak at VIN=5.13 V and each maximum output/current is 3V3X 1.455/1.727 A, 1V8 1.515/1.057 A, CORE 0.956/1.678 A. This is an engineering estimate, not a guaranteed fSW/inductor/current-limit result. [TI TPS62A02 Rev E, §8.2.2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62a01.pdf); [Coilcraft XFL4015 data sheet](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/84927b8b-f089-421b-a7f4-a0fa23afe908/xfl4015.pdf).

## Hot thermal, sequence and source changes still owed

At the source's 70 °C ambient and the device's 125 °C operating-junction ceiling, using TI's JEDEC θJA *only as a screening proxy*, DDC dissipation allowance is 0.518 W for TLV and 0.416 W for TPS62A02. At 3V3X max 3.394 W output this requires efficiency **≥86.76% TLV** or **≥89.07% TPS62A02** if that θJA applies. The current parent allocation is 85%, which would estimate junctions **133.6 °C TLV / 149.1 °C TPS62A02** at 3V3X max. TI's efficiency curves are typical; the actual four-layer copper, thermal coupling, enclosure, loss and hot efficiency must be measured or bounded. The 1V8/CORE arithmetic is less restrictive but not a thermal acceptance. DDC body placement/courtyard, SW/input-return loop, FB noise and 3V3X reset threshold remain separate physical checks.

The existing `U_1V8_OK → CORE_EN → U_CORE` topology and `U_CORE_OK` supervisor can be reused conceptually, but TPS62A02 startup and active-discharge timing differ from TPS62825, and TLV gives no controlled output discharge. Scope 1V8 rise/fall, CORE enable edge and decay, 0V9/1V8/3V3X completion before `XU_RESET_N` release, hot/low-VIN brownout, rapid restart, prebiased output and load-step overshoot/droop. The current core source memo allocates only about 10–25 mV dynamic margins against XMOS supply/reset limits; the illustrative low-resistance divider screens above leave much smaller DC margin, so those old dynamic allocations cannot be inherited. The 900-µs TLV and 1-ms TPS62A02 soft-start figures are **typical only**, not guaranteed inrush/sequence times. The package's output-discharge typical point is no guarantee of a rapid core fall or safe restart. Preserve all current rail limits and parent 1.9-A/85%-allocation requirement until a full recomputation and measurement.

A real TPS62822/23 DLC adoption would first change the buck pin map, source-native 8-pin footprint, exact MPN/JLC links and package authority; retained feedback/LC parts are candidates subject to the narrow DC/cap/thermal screens above. A TPS62A02 adoption would additionally change `crow_usb_digital.tsx` buck pin map and source-native DDC footprint, three exact MPN/LCSC links, all six feedback resistor values/MPNs (and possibly tolerances), feedforward selection, input/output cap banks if qualified retention requires it, E-CAP and `power_tree.yaml` feedback/converter evidence, sourcing/manifest/TSX parity, thermal and reset/sequence qualification. The present source correction remains an **unresolved regulator package issue**. No electrical screen here proves JLC fabrication/PCBA, a complete P1 DRC, or an order.
