These are three diagnostic sublanes, **not** one valid `xmos_service_escape` allocation contract. The current checker schema permits one `through_lane`, one `axis`, and one slot demand per allocation, while this allocation spans three differently oriented endpoint groups. Moreover, `XU_RESET_N` also owns `R_XU_RST_PU.2`, `U_CORE_OK.1`, and `U_XU_3V3_OK.6` in `digital_power`; the JTAG trial includes only its connector and XU ends. The complete exact-ref/net denominator therefore remains open. No geometry has been added to the requirements YAML.

The saved board has one In1.Cu GND zone with `IsFilled() == false`, so it supplies no saved native filled-reference proof under any lane. The checker itself deliberately reports reference/pour continuity unmeasured for `crow-p1`; its optimistic capacity is not a clearance, signal-integrity, return, or route result. Endpoint pockets also have to be checked for foreign pads/courtyards and actual access, especially along the decoupled XU east edge and near the flash. Connector text-inclusive bounds extend beyond the JTAG trial body pocket and must be assessed separately rather than silently called a physical keepout.

The next placement owner should preserve all existing XU supply-pad proximity and local returns while reopening the QSPI south gap and crystal/flash window; then independently remeasure the JTAG entry with the PLL parts and all reset fan-in, save a zone-filled exact-hash isolated board, and prove continuous native reference under each proposed path. Board integration owns the combined service allocation, with `clock_flash_debug`, `debug_connector`, `digital_power`, and `xmos_core` owning the endpoint constraints. Until those checks and an exact contract exist, this proposal earns no P1 acceptance.
