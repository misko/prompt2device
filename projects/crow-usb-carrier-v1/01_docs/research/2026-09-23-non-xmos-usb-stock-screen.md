# Non-XMOS USB capture bridge screen — 2026-09-23 03:30–03:32 UTC

**Decision:** no screened dedicated non-XMOS bare USB-audio bridge both has primary evidence for at least eight simultaneous 48 kHz/24-bit capture channels and meets the five-board JLC rule of **5 + 150 = 155 units per code**. This is limited to the named parts and public JLC catalog observation below; it is not a market-wide absence or a JLC assembly allocation assertion. No source part, firmware, or layout was changed.

**General MCU: unqualified hardware candidate; firmware implementation outside current authorization.** The [ST STM32H743/H750 reference manual](https://www.st.com/resource/en/reference_manual/dm00314099-stm32h742-stm32h743-753-and-stm32h750-value-line-advanced-arm-based-32-bit-mcus-stmicroelectronics.pdf) specifies SAI TDM with up to 16 slots and 24/32-bit audio samples, plus USB OTG HS requiring an external ULPI PHY for high-speed operation. JLC lists STM32H743VIT6 **C114409 stock 3520** (LQFP100) and Microchip USB3300-EZK-TR ULPI PHY **C108383 stock 23018** (QFN32); each independently exceeds 155. STM32H743VIT6TR C5271084=814, STM32H750VBT6 C404010=1615, and USB3300-EZK C108954=327 also exceed 155. Evidence is retained with UTC times and raw hashes in `stm32_search.jsonl` and `ulpi_search.jsonl`. This is a plausible stocked *hardware* architecture for detailed qualification, not an adopted design. [ST's X-CUBE-USB-AUDIO](https://www.st.com/en/embedded-software/x-cube-usb-audio.html) documents **USB Audio Class 1.0**, not a ready eight-capture-channel USB Audio Class 2.0 image. USB descriptors/streaming, SAI DMA and clock synchronization, ADC control, and host validation would require board-specific firmware work outside the user's present authorization. The selected XU316 also owes board-specific firmware; programmability does not disqualify the MCU hardware. The MCU+PHY pair therefore does **not yet** close the present USB architecture or all-JLC-stocked design requirement.

**Boundary for decision:** the selected XU316 remains below its 155-unit JLC target; no screened dedicated replacement closes it. Replenishment of a fully qualified JLC catalog part or detailed qualification and an architecture decision for a stocked MCU+PHY path is needed. Firmware authoring remains separately unauthorized for either programmable path. External distributor stock alone does not meet the stated JLC-populated SMD rule. No supplier was contacted and no purchase was made.
