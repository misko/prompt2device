# Stocked adjustable TPS62825 source candidate for digital 1V8 and 3V3X

This isolated candidate replaces only the unstocked fixed-output TPS6282518DMQR and TPS6282533DMQR with the already used pin-compatible adjustable TPS62825DMQR. U_CORE remains the existing 0.9 V source. TI [SLVSEF9I](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62825.pdf) §§6.3, 6.5 and 8.2.2.2 specifies adjustable 0.6–4.0 V output, 2 A maximum output, VFB 594–606 mV in PWM, IFB leakage maximum 0.05 µA, a bottom feedback resistor no higher than 100 kΩ, and 120 pF feedforward across the top resistor with 100 kΩ bottom. The two new dividers use exact YAGEO 0.1%, ±25 ppm/°C parts and exact YAGEO 120 pF ±5%, 50 V C0G parts. The existing U_CORE divider is unchanged; its existing 200 kΩ bottom and missing feedforward capacitor are a separate inherited TI-recommendation discrepancy, not certified by this candidate.

Unrounded algebra uses `VOUT = VFB × (1 + RTOP/RBOTTOM) ± IFB × RTOP`, opposing resistor initial ±0.1% plus full independent ±25 ppm/°C × 100°C from the nominal 25°C point (±0.35% each), and ±0.05 µA FB leakage. It gives 1.8221619–1.8980762 V and 3.2433207–3.3931930 V. The YAML uses outward rounded DC bounds. These are source setpoint screens, not guaranteed transient or ground-shift bounds. Per-part resistor dissipation is under 0.02 mW, versus 62.5 or 100 mW ratings. Nominal values remain within converter output and 2 A current range; declared rail maxima are 0.30 A at 1V8 and 1.0 A at 3V3X. Relative to the old power-tree high outputs, maximum combined output power rises by about 0.08 W and maximum 5V input draw by about 0.02 A at the declared 85% efficiency, inside the previously calculated 0.43 A 5V allocation margin. Efficiency itself is an engineering allocation, not a device-wide guaranteed minimum.

The source graph's 1V8 consumers are XU316 VDDIO/USB18, W25Q128JW flash, SN74AXC4T245 VCCA, supervisors and pullups. CS5308 is **not** powered by 1V8; its U_ADC_1V8_OK merely senses this rail. [XMOS XU316 primary](../../02_parts/XU316-1024-TQ128-C24/XM-014532-PC-v2.0.0.pdf) operating VDDIO/USB18 is 1.62–1.98 V; [Winbond flash primary dossier](../../02_parts/W25Q128JWSIQ/part.yaml) gives 1.7–1.95 V, the tightest 1V8 maximum. 3V3X consumers are XMOS USB33, Abracon ASFL1 oscillator, SN74LVC gates, translator VCCB, supervisors and pullups. XMOS USB33 is 3.0–3.6 V; [Abracon ASFL1 primary](https://abracon.com/Oscillators/ASFL1.pdf) gives 2.97–3.63 V. Thus all listed DC source windows fit. The 1V8 high screen leaves 51 mV below flash max; 3V3X leaves 206 mV below XMOS USB33 max.

With the separate adjustable-supervisor candidate's preliminary worst high release values 1.7893 V and 3.1743 V, the DC setpoint low margins are 32.7 mV and 68.7 mV respectively. First article must confirm combined low-side ripple/ground shift under 20 mV at reset release and high-side overshoot/ripple/ground shift under 40 mV on 1V8 (leaves at least 11 mV before flash 1.95 V); these are explicit acceptance allocations, not TI guarantees. The 3V3X high-side combined budget can be much larger but remains a waveform measurement. XMOS requires monotonic rail ramps to operating minima and rail validity before reset release; verify this and the existing 1V8_OK → CORE_EN → CORE_OK / 3V3X_OK wired reset chain on power-up, hold-up and collapse. The TI 1.25 ms soft-start entry is typical, not a worst-case startup bound. The existing adjustable-supervisor source candidate must compose before threshold conclusions are adopted.

The cap model screen has been propagated: the 1V8 bank conservatively uses retained Murata -55°C / 3.38 V 16.9 µF typical model point even though its DC high is only 1.899 V, then 35% nominal retention ×0.9 initial ×0.875 lifecycle = 12.95 µF. The 3V3X bank conservatively uses the retained higher 5.05 V / -55°C 14.7 µF typical point, then rounded-down 31% nominal retention ×0.9 ×0.875 = 11.48 µF. Both exceed TI's 10 µF effective minimum as engineering screens; primary model curves are not production guarantees. The retained 0.47 µH / nominal 47 µF LC is marked in TI Table 8-3. PCB transient/stability and actual-capacitance lot qualification remain open.
