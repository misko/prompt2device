Every other pose, all 569 footprint identities, all pad number/net/layer/shape identities, and all 27 P1-fixed refs are preserved. All moved full native envelopes stay inside analog_ch8 and have no footprint-envelope collisions.

The single tested 0.20 mm F.Cu `ADC8N` link from `C_ADC_AC8N1.2` to `C_ADC_CM8N.1` is **15.977464 mm**, 0.200000 mm longer than the reviewed y=59.85 local link. Its full copper stays at least 0.200001 mm from the foreign `usb_vbus_sense` planning rectangle. After the named capacitor-pad launch, the route clears that capacitor's full envelope by 0.205001 mm. The closest nonendpoint full body/courtyard envelope is `C_A8N` at 0.205001 mm, followed by moved `U_SPOKE8` at 0.260626 mm and moved `R_B8P` at 0.285001 mm. Only the named launch and receiving capacitor envelopes are traversed. The minimum different-net native copper gap is 0.256489 mm. These margins meet the illustrative 0.20 mm research screen, but two are within 0.005 mm of its threshold and are not manufacturing/SI qualification.

The full native profile uses the archived `.kicad_pro`/`.kicad_dru`, generated eight POFV rule areas, V-PROCESS, and zone-refilled KiCad DRC. The baseline, coupled-unrouted, and coupled-routed boards each report **199 violations and 499 opens**, with identical violation identities (+0/−0) and zero V-PROCESS failures. The measured related-pad center distances (old→new, mm) are in `receipt.json`. The ADC8 cap to `U_ISO8.6` improves 7.742→7.380; cap to `C_ADC_CM8N.1` worsens 13.871→14.061. `R_B8P` to `C_A8P.2` improves 4.006→3.806; its VMID relation to `R_B8N.2` improves 9.515→9.352. The TI primary calls for close input/output capacitors and short ILIM/dVdT/RTN loops; those absolute separations are not demonstrated acceptable. This candidate therefore satisfies the bounded geometry/native-DRC screen but is **rejected for physical/P2 promotion pending a separately justified support-group recut and return/thermal review**. No canonical source, P1, or P2 acceptance is changed.

Reproduce with `python3 probe.py` from this directory, with the pinned frozen TI packet at the path in the script. The saved filled board is the sole native artifact.
