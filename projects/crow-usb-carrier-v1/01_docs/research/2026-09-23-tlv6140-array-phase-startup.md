# TLV320ADC6140 AC coupling: array phase and startup decision

2026-09-23 UTC; bounded read-only addendum to `/tmp/crow-tlv6140-ac-front-end-20260923.md`. No source/firmware edits. Candidate remains two TLV320ADC6140IRTWT with DRE off, 2.5 kΩ AC differential input, two `GRM32ER71A476KE15L`/C84494 in parallel on **each** of 16 legs, and each 10 kΩ pulldown moved to the switched/mux side. Manufacturer evidence is TI [SBAS992A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv320adc6140.pdf), TI [SLYT796A](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/slyt796a/slyt796a.pdf), and Murata [SimSurfing for exact base die](https://ds.murata.com/simsurfing/mlcc.html?oripartnumbers=%5B%22GRM32ER71A476KE15L%22%5D&partnumbers=%5B%22GRM32ER71A476KE15%22%5D). Retained primary JSON `/tmp/crow-capbank-evidence-20260923/c_dcbias_capchange.json` and Murata netlists under that directory. These are typical small-signal models, not production phase limits; the simple netlist states 100 Hz–6 GHz applicability, so 20 Hz is an extrapolated first-order screen.

## Shared shift versus channel spread

TI's application study measured the AC-coupled x140 input bias at about **1.5 V**. Retained OPA outputs rest near **1.65 V**, so the two capacitor plates differ by roughly **0.15 V DC** when powered. All eight channels share board ambient and rail/reference environment; placing one channel at −55 °C/5.05 V capacitor bias and another at nominal +tolerance on the *same board* is physically inconsistent. The prior 6.13°/0.85 ms opposite-adverse-state span is **not a plausible normal within-board channel-mismatch estimate**; it illustrated a broad global operating envelope and should not drive array selection.

Murata's 25 °C/0 V simple model is 29.6 µF per capacitor; at 0.92 V/25 °C it is 30.6 µF. Its independently measured 25 °C/0.5 Vrms DC-bias curve changes only +1.22% at 0.15 V, and stays within about +3.33% across 0–1 V. Those data do not compose into a guaranteed capacitance under the actual audio waveform, but show that normal channel-to-channel bias differences around 0.15 V are a much smaller cause of spread than ±10% part tolerance. For a deliberately broad *same-board* 25 °C/0–0.92 V planning screen, combine two caps/leg, both at the same tolerance extreme: `2×29.6×0.90=53.28 µF` to `2×30.6×1.10=67.32 µF`. At nominal 2.5 kΩ ADC input, the **added** coupling pole is 1.195–0.946 Hz. At a typical 60 µF pair it is 1.061 Hz. All channels then share roughly the same added phase, about **+3.04° at 20 Hz**, +0.608° at 100 Hz, +0.0608° at 1 kHz. That common frequency-dependent phase is not a relative array timing error; it changes the *absolute* phase/gain response compared with the CS path.

| Frequency | Coupling phase at low-C / high-C same-board corners | Maximum modeled channel phase spread | Corresponding group-delay spread | Gain spread |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 20 Hz | +3.419° / +2.707° | **0.712°** (0.099 ms *phase-delay equivalent*) | **0.098 ms** | 0.0058 dB |
| 100 Hz | +0.685° / +0.542° | **0.143°** (0.00397 ms phase equivalent) | **0.00396 ms** | 0.00023 dB |
| 1 kHz | +0.0685° / +0.0542° | **0.0143°** (0.0000397 ms phase equivalent) | **0.0000397 ms** | <0.00001 dB |

Group delay is the derivative of phase with frequency for the first-order high-pass, `f_c/[2π(f²+f_c²)]`, not simply phase/frequency. The numbers are capacitor-only at TI's **typical**, unspecified-tolerance 2.5 kΩ input, without capacitor voltage-coefficient distortion, thermal gradients, cross-chip ADC phase, or production C minima/maxima. Same-reel parallel pairs may have correlated tolerance; taking both to ±10% deliberately covers that rather than assuming statistical averaging. The −55 °C Murata 0.92 V model is 18.7 µF/device; applying ±10% to two caps at *that common temperature* gives ~0.978°/0.134 ms modeled differential spread at 20 Hz, still far below the physically inconsistent 6.13° combination. A true worst-case bound cannot be claimed because TI provides only typical 2.5 kΩ input impedance and Murata provides typical model curves.

The inherited pod's **1 µF ±5% film** into **100 kΩ ±1%** bias adds a 1.59 Hz nominal pole. With opposite component tolerances on one board its approximate channel spread is **0.544° / 0.075 ms group delay at 20 Hz**, 0.110° / 0.00304 ms at 100 Hz, and 0.0110° / 0.0000304 ms at 1 kHz. Thus the proposed TLV coupling can add phase mismatch of the *same order* as the inherited pod input stage at bass frequencies, not a new ~0.85 ms channel skew. The OPA 10 Ω/30 nF shunt's isolated nominal pole is ~530 kHz, so its component-tolerance phase at 20–1000 Hz is tiny by that simple estimate; the exact feedback/filter/mux network requires a circuit-level calculation for a complete array budget. The two analog high-pass stages add **common** phase, roughly +4.55° pod +3.04° new coupling = +7.59° at 20 Hz nominal, while differential errors depend on each channel's components.

## Charge and mute: what external paths actually prove

The new 10 kΩ pulldown on the pre-cap switched node defines that node when TMUX is open and preserves the isolation-off discharge intent. The **ADC-side capacitor plate is not externally DC terminated**; TI's 2.5 kΩ figure is an **AC input impedance, typical**, not a documented guaranteed resistor to the 1.5 V internal bias. TI `INCAP_QCHG` permits typical 2.5/12.5/25/50 ms quick-charge *duration* for coupling caps larger than 1 µF, but SBAS992A does not specify charging current or a maximum capacitor value for which 50 ms guarantees settling. Hence the external 10 kΩ alone cannot bound the capacitor's differential voltage or ADC-side recovery after shutdown; **there is no valid guaranteed passive upper bound** from the published values. Do not claim 50 ms is sufficient for 94 µF nominal.

For an *illustrative* RC scale only, if the ADC's powered input behaved as a DC 2.5 kΩ bias resistor, a `103.4 µF` upper-tolerance pair with TMUX on and low-impedance OPA would have `τ≈(2.5k+~10Ω)×103.4µF≈0.260 s`, requiring ~1.20 s for 1% residual and ~1.80 s for 0.1%; with TMUX off and 10 kΩ left termination, `τ≈(2.5k+10k)×103.4µF≈1.29 s`, or ~5.95/8.93 s. These are **not guaranteed** because that DC-path assumption is undocumented, the internal quick-charge path may dominate, and supply state may leave the ADC plate floating. The source hardware/software contract should configure the longest 50 ms quick-charge mode, hold the audio stream muted until a verified settled condition or conservative measured delay, then enable recording; on power-off retain the mux and ADC reset/rail sequence and verify no off-rail injection or false audio. This contract requires implementation later in the authorized XU firmware workstream; no firmware is authored here.

## Decision

**Choose the TLV6140 AC-coupled two-cap cell as the leading source candidate for isolated implementation**, rather than reject it for the former 0.85 ms figure. The credible same-board model gives ~0.1 ms added differential group-delay spread at 20 Hz and falls rapidly with frequency, alongside the inherited pod stage's ~0.075 ms tolerance spread. That is an engineering basis for preserving eight-channel array function through source work, **not a guaranteed phase match or a manufactured acceptance threshold**. Preserve a focused analog validation gate for exact-part large-signal bass THD and per-channel transfer/phase, input-impedance and real power-cycle settling. The absence of a specified charge current prevents a paper-only startup guarantee; it does not force rejection of the ADC architecture. The 150-unit exact-part reserve remains satisfied under the corrected aggregate C84494 quantity 360 and two-ADC quantity 160.
