# Crow passive and clock model source repair

**Scope and status.** Source-only model coverage for six instances: L_U_CORE, L_U_1V8, L_U_3V3X, F_IN, Y_AUDIO and Y_XU. Four project-authored VRML models were derived from retained primary manufacturer drawings and attached to four native footprints. No vendor CAD, Crow PCB, placement, routing, assembly or release result is claimed. The models carry simplified rectangular bodies and illustrative contacts; the manufacturer PDFs remain dimensional authority. These original geometry files are under the repository MIT `LICENSE`; no manufacturer PDF or third-party model geometry was copied into the VRML files.

**Verification.** KiCad `pcbnew.FootprintLoad` loaded 4/4 edited footprints and all 12 native pads. Parsing the authored VRML boxes found exact declared XY/Z bounds for 4/4 models, Z minimum zero, and positive contact-to-pad XY overlap for 12/12 pads after KiCad's model-Y inversion. Removing only the new model clause restores each footprint byte-for-byte to the parent commit, so pads, silk and courtyards are unchanged. A separate temporary fixture outside the project placed each footprint at 0° and 90° (8/8 instances); `kicad-cli pcb render` loaded and displayed all eight bodies in top and front views, with body volume above the front-side PCB. The fixture is `/tmp/crow-passive-fixture-20260923/`, diagnostic only and not a Crow PCB candidate.

**Limits.** The model bodies use maximum plan/height bounds where tolerances are given; they are rectangular occupied envelopes and can overstate corner volume. F_IN height lacks a tolerance on the retained page. Terminal curves, chamfers, lid details, solder joints, actual marker graphics, manufacturing distribution and mounted coplanarity are absent. These source models support body visibility and coarse orientation/clearance inspection only. They do not independently close `MODEL-SELF`, `MODEL-REG`, vendor-CAD comparison, CPL rotation, thermal/EMI behaviour or any placement/release gate; later exact board and independent review must reopen those checks.
