investigation** before connector FULL in D11. It does not change ordinary

manufacturing-order gates. It creates no current routing admission.

## Prospective private design scope

After actual independent P1 engineering admission and the affected P2 placement
review, a bounded private route investigation may be prepared before physical
connector FULL. Its existing runtime input packet and independent review must
freeze source, board, sidecars, stack, nets/pads/layers, connector-neighbor
assumptions, fixed and mutable objects, allowed output path, stop criteria and
acceptance consumer. Routing through an unbounded mechanical uncertainty is
not admitted. The chosen execution path must enforce those inputs and scope;
if it cannot, dispatch remains blocked. A PASS task delivery, coarse screen,
or this decision alone cannot meet that prerequisite.

This narrowly extends D13/D14's research scope prospectively; TI remains
prototype-only and its DESIGN_CLEAN finding stays open. It does not permit
ordinary P3 completion, P5 promotion, release, fabrication payloads, supplier
submission, order, spend, or physical testing. All 19 connector FULL targets
remain. Existing failed attempts and investigation budgets are not reset.
Ordinary conductors and the modular plan keep their current admission checks.
