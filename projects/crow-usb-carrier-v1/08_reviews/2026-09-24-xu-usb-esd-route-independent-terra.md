KiCad 10.0.4 `CONNECTIVITY_DATA` on that saved board proves two distinct copper components: `USB_DP` contains `U_XU.60` and `U_USB_ESD.1` plus nine tracks, while `USB_DN` contains `U_XU.59` and `U_USB_ESD.2` plus six tracks. Neither component contains the other pair endpoint. It also confirms the connector remains unconnected: ESD DP is not connected to `J_USB.A6` or `.B6`, and ESD DN is not connected to `J_USB.A7` or `.B7`.

The supplied refilled native DRC JSON is SHA-256 `6be363cb7e25eab36b323aa88c01f3f664351c7d252edc933f65067d0e931e90`. It has the same diagnostic-rule baseline total: 170 violations (80 clearance, 64 hole clearance, 10 library-footprint issues, eight via diameter, eight annular width), 499 unconnected items, and zero schematic-parity findings. The intermediate north-only candidate had the same 499/zero parity and two intentional `track_dangling` findings; they disappear after the ESD connection. This is a no-new-diagnostic-DRC delta, not a clean board.

The saved board has 15 F.Cu tracks and no via on the pair. Independent centerline lengths are 62.752565 mm DP and 63.667873 mm DN, or 0.915308 mm DN-long skew. The minimum track-to-track copper gap is 0.150000 mm, meeting the scratch rule's 0.145-mm minimum and 0.150-mm optimum. However, six launch tracks are 0.150 or 0.250 mm wide: they pass only because the scratch `.kicad_dru` adds the `usb_xu_launch_neck` scoped width exception. Re-running native DRC against the checked-in P1-r4 source native settings reports six USB `track_width` violations against the 0.410-mm `USB_HS_width` rule (with parity still zero). The stepped U_XU pad departure and the 0.410-mm arrival at the 0.300-mm ESD pads therefore still need a manufactured impedance/taper design and an authorized rule/source treatment.

I sampled every pair-track centerline at 0.050 mm or less on the same saved/refilled board: all 2,550 points fall in the filled `In1.Cu` GND polygon. That verifies the narrow geometric claim only. It does not prove uninterrupted return current, via/antipad behavior, impedance, or connector/ESD launch performance.

The connector-side A/B alias routing, full path length/skew budget (only 0.084692 mm remains before the declared 1.0-mm local limit), impedance control, and integration into canonical `route.yaml` remain open. No canonical file or P1/P3 acceptance state is changed by this review.
