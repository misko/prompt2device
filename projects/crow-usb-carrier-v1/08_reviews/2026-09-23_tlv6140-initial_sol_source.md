# Independent source review — dual TLV320ADC6140 candidate

Reviewed isolated candidate `9e604a927be7027e046e6dee872a17616597dea1` against base `545c251c`, and the author's ongoing source-completion changes. **The signal architecture is viable, but the source candidate is held on shutdown sequencing and is not yet ready to compose.** Exact native/dossier/ownership and configuration-contract changes are in progress. This review does not demand a routed PCB or first-article measurement before source adoption.

## Primary electrical checks

TI [SBAS992A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv320adc6140.pdf) RTW pin table matches proposed TLV pins 1–24 plus exposed thermal pad grounded. AVDD and IOVDD on 3V3_ADC (declared 3.22–3.38 V) fit TI's 3.0–3.6 V operating range. AREG/VREF/DREG are locally bypassed; MICBIAS and GPIO1 remain unconnected. Address straps A=00 and B=01 produce 7-bit 0x4C/0x4D per TI Table 50. The inherited pod permutation yields ADC A slots 0–3 = Pods 4,3,2,1, then ADC B slots 4–7 = Pods 5–8; the candidate source comment initially said this correctly. XU physical pins 93/94 are X0D35/36, 1L0/1M0 one-bit IOR ports in local XMOS XM-014532, suitable for I2C.

TI [SCPS221G](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9406.pdf) DCU pin table matches proposed TCA9406 pin map and 1V8 VCCA / 3V3_ADC VCCB. OE high up to 5.5 V is permitted while VCCA=1.8 V; ADC_READY can drive it. Either supply absent or OE low isolates ports. External 4.7 kΩ pullups plus translator internal 10 kΩ require routed bus-capacitance/rise-time qualification, but present no DC incompatibility. TCA ambient operating maximum 85°C exceeds project 70°C ambient.

Common ADC_BCLK 12.288 MHz and FSYNC 48 kHz give exactly eight 32-bit slots at 256 BCLK/frame, supported in TI Table 6 and below the ADC's frequency limit. Shared TDM/I2C multi-device use and high-Z unused slots are primary-supported. Both TLVs must have `TX_FILL=1` **before** enabling shared SDOUT outputs, with disjoint slot registers (A 0–3, B 4–7). `P0_R7=0x31` sets 32-bit TDM and high-Z fill. TX_OFFSET=0 is a valid exact-frame standard Figure 23 option if XMOS sample edge and framing match; TI's >0 advice for higher clocks is not an absolute requirement at this 12.288-MHz design. No guaranteed cross-die aperture skew is published; common clocks support engineering adoption with first-article channel-phase qualification, not a guaranteed array-timing figure. Do not route MCLK directly to default GPIO1, which may drive an IRQ output.

TI 3.3-V, 48-kHz, four-channel, PLL-on, DRE-off current is **21.3 mA typical per ADC**, or 42.6 mA for both. This is substantially below the inherited 0.25-A quiet-rail allocation before retained OPA/reference/control loads; source owner must record a conservative complete load reserve, not treat typical current as a maximum. TI's 2-Vrms differential range supports the intended 1.2-Vrms input with DRE disabled. New coupling uses two 47-µF C84494 per leg (32 per board), with pulldowns on TMUX side and internal ADC bias on the other. Bounded Murata typical-model analysis gives ~0.098-ms within-board differential group-delay spread at 20 Hz and smaller spread above; the model is extrapolated below its 100-Hz floor. Large-signal bass distortion and actual power-cycle settling remain first-article gates. TI explicitly recommends low-voltage-coefficient input caps and adjustable `INCAP_QCHG`; 50-ms quick charge is a selectable typical duration, not a settling guarantee. The 10-µF VREF cap exceeds TI's 1-µF minimum by nominal value; model/effective screen and longer `VREF_QCHG` selection are needed.

Direct JLC snapshots show C1852023 188 vs `2×5+150=160`, C840107 16,905 vs `1×5+150=155`, and C84494 56,473 vs `(32+10)×5+150=360`; public stock is not a PCBA allocation. The ADC's 28-piece margin over the hard reserve is narrow. Reused JLC codes whose per-board counts increase also require aggregate screening; order-time full exact-part inventory remains a gate.

## Exact source repairs before composition

1. Replace provisional generic RTW TSX land with TI exact `qfnd450a`/RTW0024A land: 0.6×0.25-mm 24 pads, 0.5-mm pitch, 3.8-mm opposing pad-center lines, 2.6-mm EP. Add matching native KiCad footprint and exact TLV/TCA primary dossiers; qualify the TCA DCU native pin/land geometry. The author's uncommitted follow-up has corrected TSX to ±1.9-mm center lines and 0.6-mm pad length and added native footprints; rerun exact TSX/native pin and geometry match on its final commit. Keep volatile JLC stock counts in a dated research snapshot rather than static part dossiers per `02_parts/contracts.md`.
2. Update manifest, exact-parts CSV, floorplan/module ownership, active power and effective-cap rules, and diagnostic source census. The 9e604a92 draft changes only four TSX files, so inherited generated artifacts and rules still describe CS5308. Do not publish a canonical board from a provisional overlap; first physical placement already shows coupling-cap overlap.
3. Commit a hardware/software **register contract**, with no firmware implementation: address/port map, 2-ms post-SHDNZ I2C delay, P0_R2=0x99 internal-AREG/VREF_QCHG-long wake (the first wake transaction, not a later write), per-die slots and TX_FILL sequencing, 2-Vrms/DRE-off/2.5-kΩ/HPF-all-pass/linear-phase settings, TX_OFFSET and host edge agreement, input/VREF quick charge and muted capture until measured settle, readback/status, and warm-reset low-time rule. TI specifies ≥100 ms between true power cycles, <1 V/µs supply ramps, and power-down t3/t4 ≥10 ms at default or ≥100 µs after programming P0_R5 SHDNZ_CFG=00. These are source sequencing requirements, not mere measurement to-do items. TI describes the default ~25-ms ramp-down for active recording; before configuration the device is in sleep, but §10 does not publish a sleep exception. No device-damage claim follows from this timing text.
4. **Repair the shutdown hardware before source acceptance.** The draft `PWR_EN` feeds two 100-kΩ resistors in parallel and 15 nF (`τ≈0.75 ms`) into `U_DUMP`, which switches `Q_DUMP`/1 Ω and disables the LDO. It has no `PWR_EN`→`ADC_RESET_N` path: POR/digital-bad pulls SHDNZ only as 3V3_ADC degrades, so even programmed 100-µs separation is not established. The author's uncommitted follow-up adds a 5V-held-domain inverter and open-drain MOSFET to assert SHDNZ early; verify that final pin/net mapping and worst-case propagation. Before configuration SHDNZ_CFG is default, so independently bound ≥10 ms using the **reduced but complete sleep-mode load**, actual cold/effective hold capacitance, diode-lowered starting 5V_LDO_HOLD, LDO dropout at that load and adverse dump delay; or obtain a TI primary sleep-mode exception. The full 250-mA rail allocation is not automatically the sleep load. Equally, 5V_BUCK−4.10 V is not all usable capacitor swing because `D_HOLD` and `Q_PRE` precede the bank. At a possible 4.35-V held start and 4.10-V *normal-load design floor*, 752 µF supports only 18.8 mA for 10 ms; the true minimum input during a sleeping ADC can be lower when the LDO's load-specific dropout and ADC 3.0-V operating floor are used. At the adopted buck minimum 4.91 V and full 0.81-V path-drop reserve, held start is 4.10 V. Six 470-µF capacitors screened to 1.523 mF effective would fall 0.328 V at a **complete 50-mA held-domain load** in 10 ms, leaving 3.772 V, above 3.38-V regulated high plus ADI's 330-mV dropout maximum at 50 mA by 62 mV. The 50-mA bound must include the LDO GND-pin current (ADI Rev D max 7 mA at 50-mA output load in dropout), held-domain logic/TMUX/supervisors and all 3V3_ADC loads; TI OPA2320 max 1.7 mA per amplifier over temperature gives 27.2 mA for sixteen channels, plus 3.38 mA VMID strings and ADC sleep current. This is a possible source screen, not yet completed in the draft.

5. **Bound both ends of the dump delay.** The author's first uncommitted revision changes C_DUMP_TIME from 15 nF to 10 µF while leaving 50 kΩ pull-up; nominal RC is **0.5 seconds**, not 10 ms. Nexperia 74LVC1G14 Table 8 gives falling threshold 1.32–1.87 V at 4.5-V VCC over −40…125°C, so the timer may wait hundreds of milliseconds after PWR_EN falls—longer than the held bank can support. It may never perform the intended deliberate dump before its own VCC dies. Select a capacitor/logic topology from the adverse earliest trip ≥10 ms and latest trip while 5V-held logic remains operable, including VCC falling as the capacitor discharges, then verify Q_DUMP/LDO_EN relative order. Nexperia allows input overvoltage and partial-power Ioff, so the RC input rising above falling VCC is not itself a clamp-injection defect. Removing the static 200-Ω OPA bleed can free 16.5 mA; LT3045 does not require that bleed for output-cap stability, but switched-dump and restart/pop behavior must remain coherent. First-article waveform testing follows a passing source calculation.

No firmware code was authored in this review. The root's later core-feedback, D9 and main-buck commits are outside the candidate's `545c251c` base and must be preserved during composition; their absence here is not an ADC defect.

## 05:03 UTC in-progress repair update (not yet a committed candidate)

The author has replaced the invalid X7R timer with a provisional eight-parallel 15-nF C0G / 150-kΩ network and grown the hold bank to fourteen exact Panasonic 470-µF capacitors; the bleed remains. Fourteen × 470 µF × an explicit engineering 0.54 tolerance/cold/life retention gives 3.553 mF. At the accepted 4.91-V buck minimum and retained 0.81-V feed-path drop reserve, held start is 4.10 V. At 70-mA aggregate hold load, the bank takes 38.1 ms to fall to 3.35 V, the TI 3.0-V ADC operating floor plus ADI LT3045 350-mV maximum dropout at 300 mA; full 3.38-V regulated output lasts 19.8 ms to 3.71-V input. The 70-mA envelope must include OPA2320 sixteen-channel maximum 27.2 mA, 200-Ω bleed 16.9 mA, VMID 3.38 mA, eight TMUX2821 maximum 1.12 mA, LT3045 dropout-region GND current reserve and held-domain controls; it must cover the post-wake/pre-P0_R5 interval as well as sleep. TI §8.4.2 forbids writing P0_R5 while asleep; P0_R2 exit is the sole allowed sleep transaction, followed by ≥1-ms wait. TI's quoted 5-µA sleep current is for external clocks stopped.

My independent numerical timer screen, using Nexperia VT− voltage bounds over a linearly falling 4.10-V hold supply, ±1-µA input leakage, R ±1%, C ±5% plus a ~2% C0G reserve, gives approximately **14.8-ms earliest** and **29.4-ms latest** dump assertion. This lies between default 10-ms TI t3/t4 and 38.1-ms held-to-ADC-minimum horizon with ~8.7 ms latest margin. The timer equation and current census must be recorded in the author's committed source note, with exact stock and component ownership; first-article rail/SHDNZ/Q_DUMP waveform remains a physical qualification. An alternate exact stocked YAGEO 100-nF NP0 `CC1206JKNPO9BN104`, JLC `C577121`, public 1,987, is also under consideration to reduce parallel parts. The preceding §4–5 describe earlier draft defects and are superseded only if the final committed source actually contains this revised network and a complete source screen.
