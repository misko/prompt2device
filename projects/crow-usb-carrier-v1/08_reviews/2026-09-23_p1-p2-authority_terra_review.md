# P1/P2 authority review

## Decision: acceptable for adoption

ADR 0011 correctly separates a fresh P1 floorplan predicate from P2 local placement without making P1 finish P2. P1 remains hash-bound and requires anchors, regions/corridors, source/native parity, P1-class native defects clear, and body/courtyard/model coverage; it names P2 measured failures without accepting them. P2 is isolated, bounded, and non-promoted. The ordinary driver [3c] remains unchanged.

The proposed boundary preserves the connector FULL predicate exactly: base PASS, zero unknowns, and all 19 physical targets. FULL is still required before any P3 critical-local proof, route preparation/import/any routing, P5 promotion, release, or order. A P2 geometry change reopens relevant P1 connector-neighbor evidence. No prototype path, board pointer, retry reset, accepted prior attempt, or physical waiver is introduced. The three failed attempts remain consumed; a fresh campaign reassessment and new bounded candidate remain mandatory.

The language agrees with modular ownership: P1 establishes a floorplan, P2 owns local block placement, and P5 owns integrated placement. It adds no circular requirement that P1 close P2 distances. The declarative adoption patch only activates ADR 0011 and its conditional contract footer; it preserves the existing canonical-driver hard stop and all physical gates.
