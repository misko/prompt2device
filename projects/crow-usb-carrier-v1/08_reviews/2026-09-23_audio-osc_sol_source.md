| Clock quality boundary | The exact SCTF document provides **no phase-jitter specification**. Abracon prints an RMS 1 ps figure but marks it **reference only** and directs users to request a specific-frequency value, so there is no binding 1 ps product requirement to enforce. Nevertheless, moving from a stated reference figure to no figure weakens pre-build confidence in a clock called “low-jitter” by the architecture. No noise spectrum, ADC dynamic-range impact, CS5308P falling-MCLK/falling-BCLK phase budget, two-load capacitance/route-edge budget, or first-article measurement has been supplied. Keep those as explicit physical qualification gates; do not call the SCTF part measured equivalent. |
