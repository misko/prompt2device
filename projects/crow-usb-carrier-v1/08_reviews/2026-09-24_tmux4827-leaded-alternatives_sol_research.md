# Crow TMUX4827 package alternative screen — 2026-09-24

**Decision:** no ready, stock-qualified non-BGA substitution found in this bounded public-record screen. The retained [TMUX4827YBHR part contract](../02_parts/TMUX4827YBHR/part.yaml) uses eight dual SPDT isolators per board, one shared active-high `AUDIO_EN`, floating A throws for mute, a held 5 V rail, recommended analog I/O of −12 to +12 V, and at 85 °C maximum 0.225 Ω on resistance, 0.05 Ω flatness, and 0.1 µA off/power-off leakage. TI lists [TMUX4827](https://www.ti.com/product/TMUX4827) only in YBH 9-ball DSBGA. Its center B2 GND via and 0.10 mm center-neighbor copper gap are therefore not removed by a package suffix change.

Public [jlcsearch](https://jlcsearch.tscircuit.com/) derivative catalog results below were fetched 2026-09-24 19:27–19:28 UTC with `python3 skills/jlcpcb-fab/scripts/jlcsearch.py discover MPN --out /tmp/crow-iso-MPN.json`. Stock is a point-in-time catalog observation, **not** JLC allocation or assembly acceptance. **Migration scope if the electrical envelope is deliberately relaxed:** TS5A23159DGSR is the clearest package escape to prototype. Rework the eight isolator symbols/pins and VSSOP footprints, tie both control pins with correct polarity, keep the floating mute throws and downstream pulldowns, then qualify audio attenuation/distortion and bias range, hot-plug/±12 V fault survival, held-rail current, power sequence, native DRC, and the larger placement/routing cells. If ±12 V tolerance and current audio limits remain requirements, keep this as a research candidate only; solve the YBH fabrication acceptance or undertake a broader protection/switch architecture redesign.
