# Independent schematic topology and ratings review — 2026-09-23

## Findings by electrical path

1. **External input, fuse, reverse polarity and transient.** `J_PWR.1 → F_IN.1/2 → Q_IN.5 (drain) → Q_IN.1–3 (source) → N12V_PROTECTED` is the intended P-channel reverse-hookup chain. `D_QIN_GS.1` is at the source, `D_QIN_GS.2` at the gate; `R_QIN_G` pulls the gate to ground. `D_IN` is cathode-to-protected-12V and anode-to-ground. The drain/source and gate orientation agrees with Diodes DS37204 Rev.2-2; the clamp and fuse identities agree with the retained exact manufacturer records. Under the declared 13.2 V operating ceiling, the 15 V TVS standoff does not conduct normally. The *24.4 V / 10-1000 µs* TVS figure in `protection_paths.yaml` is a component-compatibility screen, not an all-pod surge-withstand claim. Q_IN 60 V VDS and TPSM63603 36 V operating/42 V absolute input bounds exceed that stated clamp; no sustained 24.4 V operating claim is made.

2. **Eight independently limited spoke outputs.** For **8/8** channels, TPS26625 IN and SHDN join `N12V_PROTECTED`, OUT reaches only its `N12V_PODi` connector pins 1/3/7, ILIM has the selected 44.2 kΩ to its local `SPOKE_RTNi`, dV/dt has 10 nF to that RTN, OVP is tied to RTN, and the system GND pin is distinct. TI SLVSDT4F requires local RTN/GND separation for these programming networks; the apparently isolated RTN nets are therefore intentional. All eight connector audio P/N and ground/shield pin maps are uniform. The exact TI 44.2 kΩ current-limit table gives 0.145–0.159 A at its stated 24 V/1 V-drop condition, rather than a universal 12 V fault guarantee. At the declared normal 0.10 A, the manufacturer 0.8 Ω maximum on-resistance contributes ≤80 mV. The `power_tree.yaml` remaining copper/contact budget and source floor must still be proven on a routed board and mated cable.

3. **Converters and held quiet analog rail.** TPSM63603 adjustable FB divider is 40.2 kΩ/10 kΩ, nominal `1 × (1+40.2/10)=5.02 V`; EN/SYNC is tied to protected VIN, VLDOIN to its own 5 V output, VCC has a local net, and CBOOT/RBOOT are deliberately shorted as allowed by TI SLVSFS5A §7.3.10. The TPS62825 rails each have VIN on 5 V, separate SW/inductor/output and FB dividers: 210 kΩ/100 kΩ for nominal 1.86 V, 453 kΩ/100 kΩ for 3.318 V, and 49.9 kΩ/100 kΩ for 0.899 V with 0.6 V reference. CORE EN is supervisor-controlled after 1.8 V rises. The Schottky `D_HOLD` has anode on 5 V buck and cathode at the held-feed node; Q_PRE/R_PRE implement charge prefeed and bypass. LT3045 IN/OUT/OUTS/SET/ILIM/PGFB pins follow ADI Rev.D; OUTS shares the output-capacitor/load net as required. The 16 × 470 µF held-bank nominal total is 7.52 mF; effective ESR/capacity, R_PRE pulse heating, shutdown and dump waveforms remain physical tests, as the rules explicitly state.

4. **Eight analog inputs and two ADCs.** In **8/8** channels, connector P/N reaches separate AC-coupled OPA2320 inputs, filtered differential TMUX4827 B throws, then separate paired 47 µF AC-coupling networks into the TLV320ADC6140 differential input pins. TMUX `A` throws are intentionally unused, `SEL=AUDIO_EN` selects the B audio path when high, and each local 10 kΩ pulldown sits before the ADC coupling capacitors. The two ADCs have distinct hardware I²C straps (`A ADDR1/ADDR0=0/0`; `B=0/1`), shared reset, BCLK, FSYNC and `SDOUT`, and individual AREG/DREG/VREF bypass. The shared SDOUT is **conditional on programming**: TI SBAA383C §3 maps distinct slots and requires TX_FILL=high impedance on both devices before their ASI outputs are enabled; reset TX_FILL drives zero in unused cycles. The board-specific firmware contract must implement/read back this sequence. Without it, the two output pins can contend and eight-channel recording is not established; that is an explicit software/bring-up dependency, not evidence of a hardwired schematic short in the specified configured state.

5. **Level translation, XMOS, USB and boot.** SN74AXC4T245 group 1 DIR high translates XU 1.8 V BCLK/FSYNC to 3.3 V; group 2 DIR low translates ADC data and 3.3 V audio oscillator back to XU 1.8 V. The independent TCA9406 uses VCCA=1.8 V, VCCB=quiet 3.3 V, four domain-correct I²C pullups and an ADC_READY-controlled OE. XU316 core, I/O, USB 1.8/3.3, and bead-filtered PLL pins terminate on their intended rails; RESET_N has a 1.8 V pullup and open-drain supervisor pulls. QSPI pin order matches XMOS XM-014532-PC boot ROM (CS 2, CLK 4, D0 127, D1 128, D2 1, D3 3) and W25Q128JWSIQ 1.8 V flash; the XMOS internal boot-strap pulldowns select QSPI absent an external high strap. The 24 MHz crystal and 24.576 MHz audio oscillator are distinct clocks. USB-C CC1/CC2 each have a 5.1 kΩ Rd, D+/D− from both plug orientations meet XMOS pins 60/59 with local ESD, while VBUS reaches only the sense FET/capacitor/ESD network, not the carrier power rails. The 4.7 µF VBUS capacitor and its derating are a project engineering screen; actual USB attach/detach behavior and RF realization remain later gates.

## Explicit open facts and dispositions

- **Source/protection qualification — blocking design-clean/order:** No actual isolated 11.4–13.2 V source and cable has been shown to meet the `power_tree.yaml` one-fault 2.185 A delivery or aggregate-fault envelope (≤3.4 A peak, ≤10 ms cumulative above 2.85 A, then ≤2.85 A or off until deliberate rearm). The 4 A fuse is *not* claimed to clear a bounded persistent fault. Shorted-TVS/common-trunk energy and eight eFuse retry overlap need source/cable waveform and first-article evidence. A pulse above 13.2 V can propagate to the pod outputs because OVP is disabled; the pod input withstand for such a pulse is **not established**. Do not promote the component-only TVS screen into system surge survival.
- **Ratings at actual environment — blocking design-clean/order:** The source calculations assume an engineering 70 °C carrier ambient and selected hot resistance/thermal allocations; the final board’s ambient, copper losses, connector/cable resistance, capacitor effective values, LT3045/output-capacitor ESL, eFuse retry temperature, crystal drive and startup are unmeasured. Low-current 85 °C-rated logic/translator parts require a finalized local ambient and self-heating check. The 10.8 V pod connector floor is barely met by the allocated path budget; post-route and hot four-wire evidence must be judged against the exact requirement.
- **Programming/operation — blocking functional claim:** No board firmware or programmed flash image exists in this packet. TI two-ADC slot/TX_FILL, reset and 2 ms post-SHDNZ initialization, high-pass disable, XU USB descriptors and 1.8 V JTAG/flash programming require a separate authorized firmware and first-article test path. `SOUND` here does not assert USB enumeration or audio capture.
- **Native ERC boundary:** Re-export of the exact schematic produced the same normalized netlist. Scratch `kicad-cli sch erc --severity-all` found no electrical errors; it emitted 4,155 warnings: 2,613 off-grid endpoints and, in this isolated review tree without installed project symbol/footprint library tables, 974 symbol-library and 568 footprint-link warnings. These do not establish a new electrical topology error, but the owning native schematic/presentation review must disposition them on the canonical project before schematic-stage acceptance.
