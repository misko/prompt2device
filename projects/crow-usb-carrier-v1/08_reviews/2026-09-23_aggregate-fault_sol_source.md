# Independent review of Crow aggregate-fault source study — 2026-09-23

**Verdict: BOUNDED REPAIR before checker adoption.** The no-new-breaker architecture is a legitimate conditional source-stage option under the current one-fault/normal-service requirements. The study correctly rejects multiplying the TPS26625's 0.159 A table-conditioned IOL by eight as a hard-short maximum, and correctly separates multi-fault service interruption from the required normal and one-fault 10.8 V delivery. It does **not** yet give a mechanically checkable external-source fault envelope. This is a contract/timing repair, not evidence that a new board breaker is needed. Read-only review of root `7453376b`, the study, and exact Littelfuse 451/453, Diodes DMP6023LFG and TI TPS2662 primary PDFs.

The two open phrases are “≤2.85 A RMS over the applicable thermal window” and “≤1.576 A²s per isolated pulse or pulse train including incomplete cooldown.” No window, event termination, or cooldown law is defined, so a supply with periodic 4 A retries can claim a new pulse allowance indefinitely. The 1.576 A²s number is simply 50% of Littelfuse's **nominal** 3.152 A²s melting entry; that entry is neither a guaranteed hot non-opening threshold nor an invertible thermal-memory model. A rectangular 4 A/98.5 ms conversion is only I²t arithmetic. It must not become a checker pass condition for arbitrary waveforms. The 2.85 A continuous screen is itself a project hot engineering allocation, not the fuse manufacturer's guaranteed current at 70 °C; retain its stated first-article hot verification.

A concrete **sufficient source-stage repair** that avoids an arbitrary cooldown window is:

1. The external isolated source and cable deliver **11.4–13.2 V at J_PWR at 2.185 A continuously without foldback/hiccup** in normal and one static branch-limited fault. This preserves the existing delivery screen for the **static table-conditioned** one-fault allocation; hard-short fast-trip transients use the aggregate source-fault envelope and may interrupt service without opening F_IN. Do not promise healthy-spoke service in multi-fault states.
2. In any aggregate/TVS fault, total current **at J_PWR including source-output/cable-capacitor discharge** is ≤**3.4 A instantaneous peak**. The cumulative time above the 2.85 A hot continuous allocation is ≤**10 ms over that entire fault episode**. Thereafter source current is ≤2.85 A or zero for as long as the fault persists. Further >2.85 A retries are prohibited until an explicit source/input power-cycle after fault removal; ≤2.85 A hiccup retries may continue. Recovery never overshoots 13.2 V. This is an example conservative procurement envelope, not a claim that an identified supply already meets it. Its event and rearm semantics must be explicit in `protection_paths.yaml`/`power_tree.yaml` before a checker branch accepts it.
3. Keep the existing eight exact TPS26625 ILIM/dVdT populations and require actual 11.4–13.2 V hot-short, retry-overlap, cable and post-fuse C_IN1–3 discharge waveforms to qualify the implementation. The source cap is measured at J_PWR; it does not bound local post-Q_IN capacitor discharge into a branch or shorted TVS. The 0.159 A TI IOL row remains a static screen at VIN=24 V, VIN−VOUT=1 V, not an all-fault guarantee. The source must remain stable with the buck and eight eFuse auto-retries; multi-fault 10.8 V service is not required.

Why these provisional numbers are defensible: at the inherited 50 mΩ hot Q_IN allocation, **3.4 A** dissipates 0.578 W. Even the primary Diodes minimum-pad steady θJA=123 °C/W gives a conservative 70+0.578×123=**141.1 °C**, below its 150 °C junction limit, without needing a pulse-specific thermal assumption. The allowed 10 ms excess-current interval carries at most 3.4²×0.010=**0.1156 A²s total** (3.7% of the nominal fuse melting entry); increment above a fully hot 2.85 A baseline is only (3.4²−2.85²)×0.010=**0.0344 A²s**. These ratios justify a cautious source-stage reserve, **not** a guaranteed fuse non-opening result. Q_IN's 25 mΩ maximum is specified at VGS=−10 V; the 50 mΩ hot allocation, actual gate clamp and minimum-pad copper/thermal path remain first-article gates. At the 2.85 A sustained allocation Q_IN's conservative static screen is 70+2.85²×0.05×123≈**120 °C**. The formerly allowed fuse-clearing mode remains unqualified: 8 A at 50 mΩ is 3.2 W, and Littelfuse permits up to 5 s to open at 200% rating; no Q_IN survival proof covers that combination.

The suggested 3.4 A/10 ms envelope is deliberately stricter than the study's 4 A/98.5 ms interpretation. If supply research shows it cannot be met, replace it with another **explicit** peak/total-excess-time/rearm envelope whose Q_IN and fuse screens can be independently recomputed; do not turn the nominal 1.576 A²s allowance into a general retry permit. An exact supply SKU is not needed to make the source architecture and checker alternative concrete, but a measured supplier load-line, cable waveform, hot F_IN/Q_IN behavior, and real multi-branch retry tests remain required before procurement/release. A checker alternative may validate the declared source envelope and fitted F_IN/Q_IN/eight eFuses while retaining negative tests for undefined repetition, missing source evidence and an unqualified fuse-clearing path. It must label this as a conditional external-source architecture rather than reporting a fabricated aggregate-breaker threshold.
