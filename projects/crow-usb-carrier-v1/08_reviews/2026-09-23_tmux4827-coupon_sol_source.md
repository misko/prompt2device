# Independent TMUX4827 escape-coupon review

2026-09-23. Reviewed `/tmp/crow-tmux4827-coupon-20260923/{README.md,make_coupon.py,coupon.kicad_pcb,coupon.kicad_pro,drc.txt,gerbers/*}` against TI SCDS457B YBH0009-C02 pp. 32–34 and current JLC 4-layer BGA guidance. **Verdict: ACCEPT as a diagnostic native-geometry coupon for a no-inter-ball-lane escape; do not treat it as PCB-fab or PCBA acceptance.** No Crow source/framework edits.

All nine package identities match TI's bump-side-down top-view pin table and the actual F.Cu Gerber net attributes: A1 S1A→NC_A_P, A2 SEL→AUDIO_EN, A3 S2A→NC_A_N, B1 D1→ADC_P, B2 GND→GND, B3 D2→ADC_N, C1 S1B→FILTER_P, C2 VDD→5V_LDO_HOLD, C3 S2B→FILTER_N. Ball centers are a 3×3, 0.40-mm-pitch grid. Eight 0.09-mm F.Cu tracks run directly outward from the eight perimeter lands to diagnostic test lands; no between-ball lane is attempted. B2 has a centered Ø0.35/0.20 through via with `filling yes` and `capping yes` serialized in the KiCad board, connected to B.Cu/GND. TI package drawing: <https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmux4827.pdf> (retained `/tmp/tmux4827.pdf`, SHA-256 `3af04d7ae37b0e43c71c1b663a2d055667d94d46b5dc87c9ca2de24ec2295028`).

The eight perimeter copper lands are Ø0.25 mm; B2 copper is Ø0.35 mm to accommodate the via. Both F.Mask and F.Paste Gerbers show nine **Ø0.25 mm circular flashes** at U_ISO1 ball centers, including B2; solder-mask web nominally 0.15 mm. Thus the B2 mask-defined aperture is 0.25 mm over 0.35 mm copper. TI shows 0.23 mm example copper land and permits mask-defined lands though NSMD is preferred; TI's example stencil is 0.25 mm rounded geometry based on 0.075 mm thickness. The coupon's 0.25 mm *circular* paste openings are not identical to TI's rounded example or JLC's default 0.23 mm rounded-square BGA 0.4-pitch apertures. JLC states that it normally optimizes apertures and may change supplied paste files. See <https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/opening-process-standard-of-stencil>.

The nearest B2-via-copper to adjacent Ø0.25 copper pad is exactly `0.4−(0.35+0.25)/2 = 0.10 mm`, equal to JLC's published 4-layer BGA via-to-pad minimum. Nearest B2 drilled-hole edge to neighboring pad copper is 0.175 mm. The via annulus is 0.075 mm; the coupon's *own* `coupon.kicad_pro` sets 0.075 mm minimum annulus, 0.10 mm Default netclass clearance, 0.09 mm board copper floor, 0.10 mm hole-to-copper, and 0.25 mm hole-to-hole. KiCad reports 0 violations/0 unconnected pads/0 footprint errors. These local project settings are actually consumed by KiCad DRC, but DRC checks only those selected limits. The project `jlc_4layer_advanced` tier states 0.09 mm width/space, ≥0.25/0.15 via and via-in-pad available; JLC's 2026-09-09 BGA page explicitly gives 0.10 mm via-pad and track-pad, 0.09 mm trace-trace, ≥0.25 mm BGA pad, and ≥0.35 mm outer pad recommendation for filled vias. Those numbers support this *nominal* geometry. JLC's page does not independently guarantee a Ø0.35/0.20 capped via's 0.075-mm annular ring yield or the mask-defined aperture at the exact 0.10-mm copper gap. See <https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/bga-design-guidelines---pcb-layout-recommendations-for-bga-packages>.

Specific boundaries for parent integration: (1) a strict 0.10-mm clearance exactly at the published via-pad floor has no CAM/tolerance margin; obtain actual uploader/DFM and fab process acceptance before claiming manufacturability, rather than citing KiCad DRC alone; (2) generate and inspect Excellon drill and explicit fill/cap process instructions before any actual coupon or Crow board submission—Gerbers shown here contain no drill file or manufacturing order; (3) full Crow board via census/process checker must include this second Ø0.35/0.20 protected-via geometry while retaining the existing LT3045 Ø0.50/0.20 Type VII selector and excluding ordinary Ø0.30 vias; (4) preserve B2 mask/paste aperture intent through JLC CAM/stencil adjustment, confirm populated DSBGA and footprint/rotation, and evaluate decoupling/outer escape routing in context. The coupon does not satisfy these gates and does not validate audio/isolation behavior.
