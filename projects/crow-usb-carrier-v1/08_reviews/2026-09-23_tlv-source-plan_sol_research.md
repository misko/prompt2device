## Primary evidence and scope

TI [SBAS992A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv320adc6140.pdf) §7.5 specifies 2 Vrms differential full scale, 112 dB typical SNR and 113 dB typical dynamic range for the **AC-coupled, 2.5 kΩ per-pin, DRE-off, 0 dB** condition at 3.3 V AVDD, 48 kHz and 32-bit TDM. Its 2.5 kΩ impedance is *typical*, not a production minimum/maximum. TI §8.3.3 says 2.5 kΩ is unsupported for DC coupling, the default quick-charge timing covers up to 1 µF, and larger external coupling caps need increased `INCAP_QCHG`; it gives no guarantee that a 50 ms setting settles a ~94 µF nominal pair. TI §8.3.1.3 expressly supports shared I²C/TDM, distinct addresses, slot tri-state and multi-device simultaneous sampling. TI [SBAA383C](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/sbaa383c/sbaa383c.pdf) gives a two-chip slot 0–3/4–7 implementation; 8×32×48 kHz = 12.288 MHz BCLK meets the 25 MHz bus ceiling. Shared SHDNZ/BCLK/FSYNC, address/slot mapping, and firmware configuration remain hardware/software contracts, not presently functioning firmware.

At the retained 1.2 Vrms differential maximum, fixed 0 dB gain keeps 4.44 dB below the 2 Vrms full scale. The planning `2/10^(113/20)=4.48 µVrms` ADC noise compared with ~10.33 µVrms capsule self-noise at 1 Pa implies about 0.75 dB **ADC-only** SNR degradation. It is derived from a typical A-weighted dynamic-range point, not a worst-case system guarantee; OPA/filter/pod tolerance, weighting, bandwidth, supply noise and exact assembled signal chain remain. The current CS5308 reference is quieter on paper (~0.08 dB ADC-only penalty); the source candidate therefore changes the expected noise margin but no user-locked 123 dB ADC/SNR-loss threshold exists. DRE-off avoids independent gain transitions that could disturb array comparison; do not attach DRE-on 123 dB claims to this plan.

The AC-coupled topology is physically justified: 16 ADC legs each get two parallel exact Murata `GRM32ER71A476KE15L` / JLC C84494 capacitors after the isolation switch, and the retained 10 kΩ ADC-pin pulldown moves to the switched **pre-cap** node. TI's powered ADC internal bias must then be allowed to establish the ADC-side common mode. The existing 10 C84494/board plus 32 added gives 42/board and `(42×5)+150=360` required exact-code public units; observed C84494 stock 56,473, raw `/tmp/crow-tlv-front-C84494-20260923.json`, SHA-256 `25f71731690125aa3037bcdbe141cc56129d4ee1d60939356dbdc928ce62458c`. Two ADCs/board need `(2×5)+150=160`; observed C1852023 stock 188, raw `/tmp/crow-tlv-front-C1852023-20260923.json`, SHA-256 `5793081797651404707ef69c02f9e761bc7fe9b81613c5a09d215eb4e22dd4b7`. Both are retained JLC public-catalog observations at 2026-09-23 04:09 UTC, not allocation, exact BOM matching, or placement acceptance. Stock margin on ADC is only 28 units and needs a fresh screen before source adoption.

## Corrected array-phase interpretation

The front-end research file's **6.13° / 0.85 ms at 20 Hz** juxtaposes −55 °C/5.05 V low-cap and nominal high-cap states *within one board*. That is not a plausible same-board channel-mismatch estimate. The superseding note correctly treats common temperature/bias as largely a **shared transfer shift**. At 25 °C and 0–0.92 V, its Murata-model-plus-±10% same-board planning range is 53.28–67.32 µF per leg, giving added first-order pole 1.195–0.946 Hz with the TI typical 2.5 kΩ input. Recalculation gives ~0.712° modeled phase spread, **0.098–0.099 ms group-delay spread** and ~0.0058 dB gain spread at 20 Hz. Nominal added common phase is ~3.04° at 20 Hz, on top of ~4.55° from the retained pod coupling stage. The new modeled bass channel spread is of the order of the existing pod-stage ~0.075 ms tolerance spread, not the earlier 0.85 ms estimate. Phase/frequency is only a phase-delay equivalent; group delay is the phase derivative.

These are **engineering screens, not guaranteed phase bounds**. Murata's supplied model is typical small-signal, and its simple netlist starts at 100 Hz, so the 20 Hz figure extrapolates. Real capacitor large-signal C/voltage-coefficient distortion, per-channel/batch tolerance, TI input-impedance spread, thermal gradients, cross-ADC phase, and complete OPA/filter response are not bounded. TI [SLYT796A](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/slyt796a/slyt796a.pdf) shows high-K ceramic coupling can add bass distortion; its measured 47 µF X5R is not this exact X7R parallel pair and cannot guarantee assembled THD. The original research file should be explicitly marked superseded or corrected in the durable project tree before anyone uses its 0.85 ms figure for an architecture decision.

## Source-stage gates and owner boundary

An isolated schematic/BOM/footprint candidate is defensible under a source-engineering estimate standard because the topology, primary operating mode, nominal headroom, timing architecture, and D7 exact-code arithmetic have concrete support. It must keep the output **held** while these are unresolved: exact Murata large-signal bass THD and C distribution; complete 16-leg transfer/phase/noise over operating conditions; TI input-impedance spread; OPA stability, loading and bias; switch off/on and unpowered rail injection; ADC-side bias/quick-charge startup and repeat power-cycle mute duration; two-chip clock/reset/slot/channel identity and first-article simultaneous-impulse drift. No guaranteed passive settling time follows from a nominal 2.5 kΩ AC impedance. A 50 ms quick-charge register setting is only an initial implementation choice, with measured or TI-confirmed settle criterion required. New WQFN package/rail/reference decoupling, I²C addresses/clock/reset resources, and JLC placement/BOM acceptance also remain.

The owner should decide if any **actual change in array function** is unacceptable after engineering quantification: added shared 20 Hz high-pass phase and bass THD, possible interchannel phase/gain variation, additional startup mute/settling time, and roughly 0.75 dB ADC-only nominal noise penalty relative to capsule self-noise at 1 Pa (versus the quieter former ADC reference). There is no user-specified universal 20 Hz phase, settling, THD, or SNR-loss threshold to invent. Source implementation may proceed as a reversible candidate; adoption/release needs measured bounds and acceptance of those tradeoffs. Do not claim static phase calibration registers repair analog frequency-dependent phase or capacitor distortion.
