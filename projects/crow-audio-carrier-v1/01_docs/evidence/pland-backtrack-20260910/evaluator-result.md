## What the gate should say

For each eligible copper pad, search the existing bounded landing-point set, **48 directions**, **1.000 mm default centerline reach**, one actual copper layer at a time, and a **2.000 mm width cap**. For a pad/layer, candidate widths are the positive, cap-bounded `track_width` minimum values of rules that remain potentially applicable after sound invariant partial evaluation. Validate each final Track at its own width; return the first actual witness that satisfies its final width rule and every relevant Track/obstacle clearance pair.

The result is `VALIDATED_CONSTANT_WIDTH_WITNESS`, with start/end coordinates, width, one copper layer, resolved width-rule identity, and actual limiting pair/clearance information. It establishes a lower bound on widths achievable by this particular constant-width, pad-obstacle sampling model. A width smaller or larger than the witness may fail different rules. It is **not a maximum**, nor is it necessarily the widest member even of the finite candidate set, because the search stops at its first witness.

When no candidate validates, report `NO_VALIDATED_WITNESS`. This is a non-admitting search result, with its finite candidate count and sampling limits, rather than a physical claim that the pad cannot escape. Unsupported inputs are a distinct named coverage failure; no-floor exclusions are only for an actually proved absence of applicable declared floors. Zero denominator remains non-admitting and legible. No width or clearance requirement is reduced to obtain a witness.

The existing helper's “30 µm grid” is a target step with a size cap, not a guaranteed 30 µm pitch on every land. Its implementation caps each axis at five intervals and appends the center: at most **37 generated points**, before shape filtering/duplicate effects, rather than the printed “<=25.” ADC roundrects here produce 29 points. Preserve its actual sample set for this repair, but correct the disclosed bound and describe the adaptive spacing. Do not silently increase sampling or reach to make a current result pass.

The current `max_landable`, `check_board`/CLI messages, P-LAND policy row, script contract, callers, and tests promise a widest landable value and a numeric `SHORT BY` deficit. Production adoption must openly migrate those semantics. A first witness cannot be put into a field called maximum, and `floor - witness` is not a width deficit. Likewise a routed track wider than a lower-bound witness does not refute that witness model. The existing routed `MODEL-REFUTED` logic must be replaced by an appropriate direct-candidate validation/check or explicitly scoped comparison; it cannot retain its present maximum-based inference under the new result.

## Why this architecture is different and bounded

The search never computes a clearance-dependent width and feeds that width back into the rule evaluator. It validates a finite declared set of widths directly. Rules compile once into a complete expression tree. Scalar net/class/type/layer facts partially evaluate before geometry, including both A/B orderings for a pair. Fixed obstacle-area membership is cached. Candidate-area membership remains dependent on the exact start, end, radius and layer; it is evaluated on the final candidate. Rule order is preserved and the last matching constraint of the requested kind wins. One-sided net/class restrictions remain real rules: they are not discarded as “baseline” or because the other side lacks a net clause.

For a fixed pad/layer, at most `P × 48 × |W|` candidates are considered, with `P <= 37` for the retained simple-polygon helper and `|W|` bounded by the finite parsed width constraints. Across this board the declared width values are 0.18, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.35, 0.36, 0.60, 0.75 and 1.20 mm. There is no convergence test, oscillation counter, open-ended numerical loop or unbounded width sweep. Early rejection of a candidate on one actual violated pair is sound. Early acceptance requires every potentially limiting pair to validate.

Spatial pruning must bound actual geometry, using native item bounding boxes and an expansion of `reach + largest searched radius + maximum possible pair clearance`. The clearance ceiling must include board rules, every supported custom minimum, native effective item clearances and local/footprint overrides, or reject unsupported overrides. A pad-center-only neighbor radius is not a sound bound for large/off-center pads. A global ceiling is useful for pruning; it must never become the clearance assigned to an unrelated pair.

## Monotonicity and finite-search limitations

MEASURED native width-transition station: a 0.400 mm track intersects a nearby width-relaxation area and satisfies its 0.200 mm floor. The same centerline at 0.200 mm misses the area and violates the 0.600 mm default floor. Thus shrinking a valid track can make it invalid. The native oscillation station accepts 0.200 mm and rejects 0.600 mm because the wider capsule activates a 0.300 mm clearance restriction. For centerline-to-pad distance 0.400 mm, the rejected fixed-point update alternates between `2 × (0.4 - 0.1) = 0.6` and `2 × (0.4 - 0.3) = 0.2`. Global feasibility monotonicity and a finite fixed-point convergence assumption are rejected.

PROVED for fixed endpoints/layer: ideal round-ended capsules `K(r) = segment + radius-r disk` are nested as radius grows. Intersecting a fixed area is therefore a monotone atom. Negation, arbitrary Boolean combinations, last-match precedence and changing numerical constraints do not preserve monotonicity of the final feasibility predicate.

`finite-width-proof.json` gives the explicit incompleteness example requested by root. Let the baseline width floor be 0.500 mm, an area begin 0.150 mm from the centerline and relax the floor to 0.200 mm on overlap, and clearance be 0.200 mm with a 0.400 mm centerline-to-obstacle distance. The declared set `{0.2, 0.5}` finds no valid width: 0.2 misses the relaxation, while 0.5 has only 0.15 clearance. **Width 0.35 is valid**, with the relaxed 0.2 floor and 0.225 clearance. This is an executed mathematical property check, not a third native coupon. It proves why exhaustion must be named `NO_VALIDATED_WITNESS`, not geometric impossibility.

A separate event-partition solver could in principle partition radius at finitely many area-distance thresholds. Within each open cell, supported constant-valued rules are fixed and feasibility reduces to a width interval. Native integer units, tangency, geometry coverage, threshold ordering and boundary validation would all require their own proof and regressions. No event solver is selected or implemented; obtaining a maximum would add work unnecessary to the chosen witness claim.

## Honest language boundary and mandatory production guards

The architecture requires a real S-expression reader and complete condition parser, preserving parentheses and all relevant rule attributes/constraints. The private parser implements empty/omitted conditions, `==`/`!=`, `&&`/`||` with left association, unary `!`, parentheses, simple `A`/`B` NetName/NetClass/Type/Layer atoms, insideArea/intersectsArea atoms, and multiple minimum-valued width/clearance constraints per rule. The matrix verifies simple wildcard matching and F.Cu/B.Cu layer clauses. Full grammar admission is not implied by those cases.

The subsequent maintained author must close these specific guards before adoption:

1. **Final native copper admission:** validate start-in-pad and final candidate shape/layer, not just a polygon sample or pad center. Define covered custom/multi-outline/hole geometry and per-layer pad shapes. Unsupported/unreadable geometry is named, never passed. The current private loader rejects holes and retains each simple outline; it is not a universal pad/zone reader.
2. **All clearance sources in pruning:** include native effective/local/footprint clearances in `Cmax`, or reject them. Current FID1/2/3 demonstrate real local overrides. Never discard a remote large pad by center alone. Keep exact per-pair clearance assignment after pruning.
3. **Single-item width rule domain:** prevalidate B-dependence for every width AST. The prototype raises on B scalar-property access but its B-only area atom can currently reduce to false and appear floorless. That generic behavior is not acceptable: either prove native single-item B semantics for the supported form or emit named unsupported coverage, including B-only `insideArea` expressions. No silent no-floor result.
4. **Complete rule attributes:** do not ignore a relevant rule-level layer, additional constraint, min/max/opt field, severity/exclusion attribute, unknown expression or unsupported function. The private parser rejects relevant max/opt/other constraint fields and unknown rule attributes; maintained parsing must name the exact rule/field rather than crash or weaken it. Functions involving connectivity, filled copper-zone area membership, AB objects, numeric geometry properties and other native language features remain outside this prototype and must be explicitly rejected or separately implemented/tested.
5. **Class and wildcard semantics:** native effective clearance handles the measured compound-class case; compound `NetClass` equality, one-sided custom-class clearance, multilayer Pad.Layer properties, arbitrary string escapes and the exact wildcard alphabet still require explicit admission/tests. Python fnmatch accepts bracket syntax beyond the demonstrated KiCad `*`/`?` patterns; do not silently expose that as equivalent native language. Width rules with unsupported class semantics cannot become no-floor exclusions.
6. **Board minimum versus custom override:** the prototype replaces the baseline clearance when a custom rule matches. The current board has minimum clearance 0.127 mm and minimum track width 0.150 mm, while its custom clearances are at least 0.150 mm and declared width minima at least 0.180 mm, so no below-board-minimum case is exercised here. The native matrix likewise has no custom-below-board-min case. Establish actual native precedence from authority plus a maintained oracle, or reject such configurations as unsupported. Do not assume a lower custom value overrides an absolute minimum. Local override/custom precedence also needs a deliberately conflicting maintained case; no such generic claim is proven here.
7. **Public-result migration:** replace maximum/deficit/refutation language at every caller and policy/contract/test boundary. Preserve the full census and nominal-pour/physical-via distinction. `NO_VALIDATED_WITNESS` must remain non-admitting without claiming an unproven impossibility.
