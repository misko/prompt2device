## DMP6023LFG-13 — Diodes Incorporated 60 V P-channel MOSFET

Exact PDF: `datasheets/23.pdf`, SHA-256 `1ea244be5e0fe4195ad20cb2da0960ac622b6efc19cf9e579c9eb8ef5777fa27`, 502,161 bytes. Visual coverage: PDF page 1 bottom view, top view, terminal labels, equivalent circuit, and ordering row for `DMP6023LFG-13`; PDF page 5 package-outline pin numbers and suggested pad layout. Pages 2–4 and 6 were rendered and inspected for document completeness but contain electrical curves/tables or tape details rather than additional terminal identity drawings.

Manufacturer-derived identity is pins 1–3 = Source, pin 4 = Gate, and pins 5–8 = Drain. In the page-5 suggested board-pad pattern, positions 8→5 run left-to-right on the upper drain edge and 1→4 run left-to-right on the lower source/gate edge. Rotating that pattern 90° clockwise gives dossier pins 1→4 from top to bottom on the left, with drain positions 8→5 from top to bottom on the right. That is an allowed rotation, not a mirror.

The bottom-view figure and suggested pad layout show the four drain terminals tied to the common exposed drain region; the equivalent circuit names one drain. The dossier retains physical identities 6–8 as explicit `fused: true` aliases to artifact pad 5. There is no separately numbered exposed-pad identity in the manufacturer terminal diagram. Source pins 1–3 on `12V_PROTECTED`, drain pins 5–8 on `12V_FUSED`, and gate pin 4 on `Q_IN_GATE` are the expected rail/rail/control net kinds for a P-channel high-side device.

### Q_IN — VERDICT: PASS

- Q_IN pins 1–3 (`SOURCE`): expected three source terminals on one power rail; dossier shows three distinct pads on `12V_PROTECTED`.
- Q_IN pin 4 (`GATE`): expected the sole gate terminal on a gate-control net; dossier shows `Q_IN_GATE`.
- Q_IN pins 5–8 (`DRAIN`): expected four manufacturer identities on the common exposed drain region and a power rail; dossier shows artifact pad 5 on `12V_FUSED` and explicit fused aliases for identities 6, 7, and 8.
- Q_IN physical order: expected the page-5 suggested pad order up to rotation; dossier's listed coordinates are the same pattern after a 90° clockwise rotation. The automated winding field is N/A because of the composite drain land and was not used as evidence.

No FAIL was found. The eight connector instances remain QUESTION because the allocated dossiers omit the orientation landmarks needed for an independent mirror judgment and the allocated manufacturer drawing cannot establish the application harness pinout.
