Exact two-circuit facts used by the J9 harness:

- orderable housing `43645-0200`, two circuits on 3.00 mm pitch;
- accepts the `43030` female-contact family and mates the `43650` header family;
- two-circuit drawing table dimensions A = 3.00 mm and B = 6.85 mm;
- nominal body dimensions used for planning are 14.00 mm axial length,
  6.85 mm lateral width, and 8.28 mm maximum height;
- drawing mated overall length is 17.56 mm;
- integral positive mating lock and a polarized interface;
- the exact product record says `Keying to Mating Part: No`.

Therefore this project describes J9 as **polarized, shrouded, and
positive-latching**, not keyed. The selected factory-precrimped leads are
loaded from the exact drawing mating-face cavity view: cavity 1 red
`+12V_IN`, cavity 2 black `GND`, followed by a tug and continuity/polarity
check. This record does not claim physical seating or latch access.
