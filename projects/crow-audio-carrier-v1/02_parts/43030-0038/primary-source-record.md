# Molex 43030-0038 primary-source fact record

Accessed: 2026-09-01
Authority status: cited exact manufacturer product facts

Primary record: [Molex 43030-0038 product record](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/part-detail/430300038).

The exact contact is a tin-plated Micro-Fit 3.0 female crimp terminal for
18 AWG / 0.75 mm2 wire, with 1.85 mm maximum insulation diameter and an
8.5 A product current rating. It is the contact factory-installed on both
selected J9 precrimped leads.

The board's complete planned input load is 0.90 A. The project nevertheless
uses the Micro-Fit family's conservative 5 A IEC value as the current-screening
ceiling, rather than treating 8.5 A as an installed thermal proof. J9 uses
factory-precrimped leads; no field crimp height, crimp tool, or hand-built
termination is inferred from this product record.
