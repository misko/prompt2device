# HellermannTyton 113-00022 primary-source fact record

Accessed: 2026-09-01
Authority status: cited exact manufacturer product facts

Primary record: [HellermannTyton article 113-00022](https://www.hellermanntyton.com/products/cable-ties-with-fixing-elements/t18mr/113-00022).

The selected internal restraint is exact article `113-00022`, type `T18MR`
(ordering alias `T18MR0C2`; product description `T18MR-PA66HS-BK`). It is a
black, heat-stabilized PA66 screw-mount cable tie for a #4 / M2.5 screw, with
3.1 mm fixing hole, 110 mm length, 2.5 mm width, 1.5-20.0 mm bundle range,
80 N minimum tensile strength, and 53.4 N published mechanical strength.

Molex PS-43650-001 requires at least 12.70 mm of free wire behind two- through
four-circuit housings before wires are tied, twisted, or otherwise biased. The
J9 assembly therefore places this restraint only after that unconstrained run.
There is no J9 cable gland and no PoE termination. The exact enclosure mounting
point, screw/washer stack, installed reaction, lead bend and clearance remain
physical qualification.
