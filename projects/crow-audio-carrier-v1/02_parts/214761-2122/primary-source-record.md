Accessed: 2026-09-01
Authority status: cited exact manufacturer product facts

Primary record: [Molex 214761-2122 product record](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/part-detail/2147612122).

`214761-2122` is the selected red, 150 mm, 18 AWG / UL10002
female-to-pigtail Micro-Fit lead. Molex identifies the installed female contact
as `43030-0038` and gives an 8.5 A current rating. It is loaded only into J9
housing cavity 1 for `+12V_IN`; the free pigtail end is the selected termination
to the protected COTS 12 V appliance output.

The record closes lead identity, color, length, gauge, contact, and intended
cavity. It does not prove the installed route, bend, restraint, pigtail
termination, current-temperature rise, or supply polarity.
