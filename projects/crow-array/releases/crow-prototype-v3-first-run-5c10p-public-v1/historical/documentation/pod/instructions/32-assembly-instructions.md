## Original Pico D1-only preparation

Use original RP2040 Pico SC0915 matching official retained Rev3 power schematic.
Before carrier attachment, a qualified assembler removes only MODULE D1
MBR120VLSFT1G VBUS-to-VSYS diode, not carrier D1/D2/D3. Preserve module R10=5.6k
and R1=10k GPIO24 VBUS-sense divider, connector/data parts, VSYS capacitor and
every other component. No Pico H/W/2 substitution. Inspect lifted pads, debris,
bridges and damage; retain before/after photographs, source/lot/revision,
operator and carrier serial linkage. Segregate SC0915-CROW-D1OFF-R3 variant.

Unpowered ohms/diode checks in both polarities complement microscopic D1-absence
evidence. Compare actual circuit; capacitor transients are not automatically
shorts and infinite resistance/zero leakage is not claimed. Unexpected sustained
low-impedance VBUS/VSYS conduction fails. Quarantine topology/identity differences
without expanding the modification. All 40 castellated joints and full LS33
registration/pool/relief/contact/no-bearing conditions remain required.
