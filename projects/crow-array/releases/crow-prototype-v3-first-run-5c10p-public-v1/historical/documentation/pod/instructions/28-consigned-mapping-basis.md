# LS22 supplied-SMT candidate: geometry and handling basis

This is a source-bound **candidate** for independent fabrication review, not a confirmed JLC placement preview, vendor acceptance, inventory allocation, or order authorization. It keeps all LS13 `Assembly: SMT` references machine-placeable, but proposes customer-supplied/consigned SMT material because exact JLC C-numbers are absent from accepted source and exact Western-supplier observations are retained. No manual, DNP, or mechanical status changes are made. A vendor's actual consignment acceptance and capacity remain order holds.

## Source and geometry method

The candidate maps each exact accepted LS21 native PCB SHA-256, each LS13 physical identity, and its `02_parts/P*/part.yaml` plus `manufacturer.pdf`. The accepted PCB itself embeds its footprint body outlines, pad coordinates, pin numbers, and marks. `make_consigned_candidate.py` recomputes per-reference pad bounding-box center minus native footprint anchor in board axes; the companion `geometry-audit.csv` retains every measured result. On these exact boards, the measured offset is (0,0) mm for every SMT reference; it is **a result**, not a filled-in default. The maker PDFs and native F.Fab outlines require independent review to establish whether the pad center is the actual mounted body center. The exact CTS CB3LV and SP0504 custom footprints were drawn against their maker's recommended layout and pin frame; `01_docs/evidence/reviews/repair7-physical-facts.md` describes the SP0504 top-view pad1/GND mapping, and CTS Rev K page 4 shows the marked pin-1 corner. The polarized diode and semiconductor PDFs are retained with their respective part dossiers; pad1/cathode and pin1 inspection is mandatory.

For angle, [JLCPCB's KiCad 10 BOM/CPL guide](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-generate-the-bom-and-centroid-file-from-kicad) describes the native KiCad position export as a JLC input route (its Method 1), and recommends the [Fabrication Toolkit](https://github.com/bennymeg/Fabrication-Toolkit) with automatic translation (Method 2). At public commit `7f627b6be76081b962161b9eb9fe3a326a18c915`, the toolkit's [exact transformation table](https://github.com/bennymeg/Fabrication-Toolkit/blob/7f627b6be76081b962161b9eb9fe3a326a18c915/plugins/transformations.csv) gives 270° for SOIC/TSSOP and 180° for SOT-23/SOT-23-5, with no position correction. Its [source algorithm](https://github.com/bennymeg/Fabrication-Toolkit/blob/7f627b6be76081b962161b9eb9fe3a326a18c915/plugins/process.py) uses pad-bounding-box center by default, adds named-footprint rotation corrections when enabled, and leaves unmatched native rotations unchanged. This mapping therefore derives the candidate final angle from that published export convention and the accepted native angle, rather than asserting a per-MPN JLC catalog zero-angle. It does **not** claim the toolkit table knows these actual JLC/consigned part presentations. All SMT references are currently top-side; no bottom mirroring convention is inferred.

The relevant public guide and toolkit source text-byte excerpts are retained in `03_src/fabrication/ls22/population/public-export-excerpts.md`, also bound by the candidate manifest. The custom `CrowPhysical:SP0504BAHTG` name does not match the toolkit's `^SOT-23` rule, but its actual maker top-view pad numbering and lead frame match generic KiCad `SOT-23-5`; the candidate therefore carries the 180° family correction as an explicitly labeled *frame-transfer inference*. This is not proof of a JLC consigned-part zero angle.

Center inference means the nominal body has symmetric opposing extents about that center; it does not certify tape presentation or vendor recognition. An independent reviewer must inspect the actual PDF drawings (not only this table) and may reject any inferred centroid.

The source route is cross-checked with maker pad/polarity evidence, but no vendor preview has occurred. Native KiCad/F.Fab pin frame is not a tape/reel frame. The candidate must undergo independent review of every asymmetric package's printed pin1/cathode indication against the maker drawing and JLC assembly preview before ordering. Nonpolar R/C and both fuse families have 180°-equivalent electrical orientation, but body-center and solder-land fit still need review. A manufacturer's symmetric package body does not establish JLC-specific zero degree.

## Consigned handling and inspection instruction

Proposed flow, not performed: procure only the exact maker/MPN/package in the adopted BOM and preserve distributor, lot/date code, package/reel label, quantity, MSL and ESD records. Segregate by board/type and reference, label with full MPN and quantities for five carriers/forty pods plus only separately authorized attrition, and inspect identity and polarity marks against the retained maker document before release to an assembler. Maintain original tape orientation; do not re-reel or substitute parts without controlled review. Obtain assembler acceptance of the exact supplied-part list, minimum order/packaging/feeder requirements, loss allowance, shipping/receiving control, and custody before shipment. A quote, upload or purchase is not authorized by this candidate.

Before any order, inspect matched BOM/CPL and orientation preview at the actual vendor for every reference, especially CTS X1, SP0504 D5xx/D4, all diodes, SOT transistor/ICs, SOIC/TSSOP and the high-power 0603 resistors. Record each corrected angle/offset with exact PCB hash; regenerate through a new reviewed manifest rather than patching CSV. First article must inspect pad centering, pin1/cathode, solder bridges, package marking, polarity and service-specific reflow limitations. Manual Pico modification, through-hole, microphone and buzzer work remains governed by `01_docs/ASSEMBLY-PLAN.md`; do not assume consigned SMT covers that work. Actual received parts, vendor placement, assembly and inspection have **not** occurred.
