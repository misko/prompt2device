# Backend handoff: JLC connector orientation blockers

Read [the session report](REPORT.md) and the [r10 connector rotation procedure](../MACBOOK-R10-CONNECTOR-ROTATION-HANDOFF.md). This session used the named existing pod draft; the separate newly created drafts were not used. Package integrity passed for all 213 r10 files. The local evidence is diagnostic only and does not establish JLC placement or manufacturing readiness.

Pod J1 is matched to RJHSE-5080 / C599441. JLC's viewer selected it and provided top, oblique and underside images, but showed no saved placement coordinates, absolute angle, coordinate frame or units. Its download control produced an image file, not a placement record. The 90° toolbar field is an increment, not the saved angle. The underside image shows holes and overlapping model geometry without proving the eight terminals and two pegs individually align. The opening direction and registration remain unverified. No connector was rotated and the carrier draft was not inspected in this session. Advancing to the viewer triggered JLC's automatic save, but there were no placement edits. Other placement state and persistence were not independently verified.

Please resolve the evidence gap with a concrete, reproducible method for obtaining the actual saved JLC pose and inspecting the installed model geometry against the native board. Identify a documented read-only export or inspection path and its field names, units, frame and association with the designator, or propose another verifiable method; do not infer a saved angle from the uploaded CPL or conflate bare holes with model pins. Assess the supplied screenshots and native/manufacturer evidence, and explicitly mark any unresolved alignment or opening direction. Preserve the existing native boards and r10 package; do not upload diagnostic CAD.

Prepare a specific next-step procedure for pod J1 first, then carrier J101–J108 and power J1 only after the pod test meets the original handoff's checks. If a quote-only correction is proposed, specify evidence, reversibility, before/after captures and persistence checks. Do not silently change a position or accept placement. Report any need for vendor clarification without sending a message unless separately authorized.

Scope remains 5 fabricated / 2 assembled carriers and 10 fabricated / 8 assembled pods. No purchases, payments, reservations, substitutions, vendor messages, production approvals or manufacturing commitments.
