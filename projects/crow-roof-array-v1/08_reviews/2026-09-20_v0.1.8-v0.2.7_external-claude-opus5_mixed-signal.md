## A. Findings

### CLAUDE-ADV-001 — P0: pod AUDIO− leg is driven from a triple-loaded op-amp output; the "active-balanced" pair is asymmetrically loaded while `U1D` sits idle
**Evidence.** `R12` (100 Ω) is driven from `OUTP_DRV` = `U1.8`; `R13` (100 Ω) is driven from `PRE_OUT` = `U1.7`. Both legs are op-amp outputs, so the defect is unequal loading, not source impedance: `U1B`/`PRE_OUT` simultaneously drives the `R9`/`R8` feedback network, `R10` into the `U1C` summing node, and `R13` into 15 m of cable plus the carrier bias/ESD network, while `U1C`/`OUTP_DRV` drives only `R11` plus `R12` and cable. `U1D` (pins 12/13/14) is parked as a private VREF follower connected to nothing else, as `design_math.md` admits.
**Impact.** The 18/11 differential algebra is correct, but differential gain, phase and CMRR will not track across frequency, temperature, or cable length; the 588 kHz differential corner in `design_math.md` assumes a symmetric 200 Ω source that does not exist, and drive-current asymmetry at 0.95 Vrms into 200 Ω ∥ 1.353 nF is unbounded in the packet.
**Required action.** Re-wire `U1D` as the negative-leg buffer (`pin12 ← PRE_OUT`, `pin13 ← pin14 = OUTD`, `R13` driven from `U1.14`), restoring symmetric loading at no BOM cost. This requires a source/netlist/PCB revision and blocks fabrication of the pod lot as released.

### CLAUDE-ADV-002 — P0: `U_ADC` CONFIG3 (pin 4) hard-shorted to GND with no strap resistor and no published mode decode
**Evidence.** `pin4: N("GND")` where `pinLabels.pin4 = "CONFIG3"`, while every other config pin is strapped through a resistor (`R_CFG1` 4.7 k↓, `R_CFG2` 0 Ω↑, `R_CFG4` 4.7 k↑, `R_CFG5` 100 k↓). `part.yaml` documents pin 4 as `CONFIG3/HGC_SDO` — an output in hardware-gain-control mode — and flags CONFIG/HGC and SPI/I2C multiplexing as strap-dependent.
**Impact.** If any mode reachable from the CFG1/2/4/5 combination enables HGC, pin 4 becomes a push-pull output into a dead short; `R_CFG2` = 0 Ω to 3V3 presents the same hazard with no current limit. The packet contains no mode table binding the strap values to a decoded mode, so the hazard the part.yaml names is not closed.
**Required action.** Strap CONFIG3 through a resistor matching the 4.7 k convention and give `R_CFG2` a finite value, **or** publish the DS1314F1 decode table proving HGC is unreachable and all five config pins are inputs in the selected mode. The hard short as released is not acceptable either way.

### CLAUDE-ADV-003 — P1: realized `C_ADC_CM` paths exceed the declared 5 mm part.yaml ceiling by 4–22× while the audit reports PASS
**Evidence.** `part.yaml` `keep_short` declares `max_span_mm: 5` for all sixteen ADC-input/`C_ADC_CM` pairs; `analog_path_copper.json` reports realized `common_mode` lengths of 20.55–109.84 mm (worst: Ch5 P at 109.84 mm), yet stamps `"status": "PASS"`. The `common_mode` path id alone carries `max_spread_mm: 12.0` versus 1.0 mm elsewhere, so the checker grades P/N *matching* at a loosened tolerance and never grades the absolute 5 mm ceiling.
**Impact.** At ~100 mm with 4–8 vias the 1 nF terminations are not local to the converter pin; loop inductance dominates and the capacitor cannot absorb switched-capacitor charge kickback at 768 kHz-capable sampling — precisely the failure the constraint exists to prevent. Independently, a declared constraint reported PASS while violated is a release-integrity defect regardless of electrical outcome.
**Required action.** Relocate all sixteen `C_ADC_CM` parts inside 5 mm and regenerate the board, or revise the part.yaml ceiling with explicit engineering justification and re-grade. Supervised closure required before the first-article lot is accepted.

### CLAUDE-ADV-004 — P1: shared 3V3_ADC budget is unproven and internally inconsistent
**Evidence.** The test plan quotes 222.374 mA against a 230 mA allocation (3.3 % headroom) with no per-refdes decomposition across 16 OPA2320 amplifiers, CS5308P analog/digital/IO, six logic ICs and the resistive loads; the calculable resistive floor from source is ≈20.0 mA (2 × 1.65 mA VMID dividers, 16.5 mA bleed, 0.120 mA divider). The same document then reserves 5 mA *inside* a 150 mA ADC/reference suballocation, leaving ~80 mA for OPA/logic/resistive, and sets a ≤0.120 A bench-current abort that is arithmetically incompatible with a 222 mA 3V3 load at 12 V input.
**Impact.** If real draw exceeds the LT3041 limit or the 702 mW dissipation ceiling, the rail collapses or folds back mid-startup, taking the ADC reset chain and TDM buffers with it; the conflicting limits mean a passing bench result could equally indicate the estimate is wrong.
**Required action.** Publish the itemized 3V3_ADC budget using datasheet maxima, not typicals, and reconcile it against both the 230 mA allocation and the ≤0.120 A abort rule before first power. Do not raise either limit to obtain a pass.

### CLAUDE-ADV-005 — P1: `R_PRE` 22 Ω precharge gives an unanalyzed 227 mA surge onto a 0.30 A-bounded buck rail
**Evidence.** `R_PRE` 22 Ω sits permanently across `Q_PRE` (AO3401A, source on `5V_LDO_FEED`, drain on `5V_LDO_HOLD`) charging ≈987 µF (2 × 470 µF + 47 µF + 8 × 100 nF): initial current 5/22 = 227 mA, τ ≈ 21.7 ms, ~109 ms to 99 %, peak 1.14 W decaying, ≈12.3 mJ total — the 1206 pulse-rated resistor survives, but the current flows through `D_HOLD` and the buck before `PWR_EN` asserts, against a 0.30 A steady bound on `5V_BUCK`.
**Impact.** At cold start the AP63205 must source 227 mA of precharge concurrently with its own soft-start and output bank; if it current-limits or hiccups, `5V_BUCK` sags, `U_PWR` stays in reset, `PWR_EN` stays low, `Q_PRE` stays off, and the system latches in a slow-charge loop with the LDO never enabled — while the plan's own 0.120 A abort rule fires.
**Required action.** Raise `R_PRE` (e.g. 100 Ω → 50 mA peak, τ ≈ 99 ms) or supply startup simulation/measurement showing soft-start plus precharge plus output bank stays inside 0.30 A and inside the bench abort threshold. Mandatory supervised instrumented startup with a current probe on `5V_LDO_FEED`.

### CLAUDE-ADV-006 — P2: `analog_path_copper.json` stamps PASS while its own sub-statuses are ungraded or owed
**Evidence.** Top-level `"status": "PASS"` / `"verdict": "PASS"` coexist with `physical_dcr_status: "UNVERIFIED"` (twice), `routed_length_status: "NOT_GRADED"`, `realized_copper: "NOT_GRADED"`, `capture_identity_status: "OWED"`, and sixteen entry rows whose `why` records that exact source review and system ESD proof are owed.
**Impact.** A named machine artifact marked PASS will be read downstream as qualification; combined with CLAUDE-ADV-003 this is a systematic grading-scope problem rather than a single omission.
**Required action.** Change the top-level verdict to `PASS-WITH-OWED` or gate `"status": "PASS"` on all sub-statuses being graded. Recorded improvement; does not block the lot.
