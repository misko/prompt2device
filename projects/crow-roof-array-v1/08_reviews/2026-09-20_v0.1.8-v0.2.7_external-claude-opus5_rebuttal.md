# Reassessment — Crow carrier v0.1.8 / mic pod v0.2.7

Reviewed against primary datasheet text (Cirrus DS1314F1 §4.2, AP63205), the `analog_path_copper.json` schema definition, and the exact native pin-center geometry. Four of six original findings do not survive contact with that evidence.

---

### CLAUDE-ADV-001 — DOWNGRADED → **P3 (observation)**
The premise that one leg is a low-impedance op-amp output and the other a resistive divider tap is wrong: `OUTP_DRV` (`U1.8`) and `PRE_OUT` (`U1.7`) are both op-amp outputs behind matched 100 Ω series resistors, and the "200 Ω" in `design_math.md` is the sum of those two source resistors, not a load. The receiver is AC-coupled with 100 kΩ/leg bias; the only asymmetric current is the 10k/18k feedback/summing draw on `PRE_OUT`, which at the modeled 0.481 Vrms maximum leg level is tens of microamps against ~5 mA of available drive — no quantified gain, phase, or CMRR violation, and no bound exceeded anywhere in the packet. The asymmetric small-signal internal loading is real but unquantified-as-harmless-scale; `U1D` remaining a private VREF follower is a spare-amplifier utilization note, not a defect. No source, netlist, or PCB revision is justified. Q4 is withdrawn.

### CLAUDE-ADV-002 — REFUTED
DS1314F1 §4.2 defines every released strap as a valid hardware-mode table entry: CONFIG1 4.7 k↓ = hardware secondary 44.1/48 k; CONFIG2 0 Ω↑ = minimum-slot TDM; CONFIG3 0 Ω↓ = slots 0–7; CONFIG4 4.7 k↑ = default channel order; CONFIG5 100 k↓ = linear-phase fast-rolloff with HPF. All five pins are inputs in this mode, latched at reset release and not operationally changeable. CONFIG3/HGC_SDO becomes an output only in software mode, which requires `HGC_SPI_EN` — a software register defaulting to zero and unreachable from a hardware-mode strapping. The direct-to-GND CONFIG3 tie and the 0 Ω CONFIG2 pullup are therefore datasheet-sanctioned, not an unlimited push-pull short. Strap-resistor style inconsistency is cosmetic. Q1 is answered and withdrawn.

### CLAUDE-ADV-003 — REFUTED
The finding compared two different quantities. `analog_path_copper.json` defines `common_mode` length as the full channel branch from the `U_ISOn` switch output to `C_ADC_CMn`, which is why 20–110 mm is expected and why that path id is graded for P/N *spread*, not against the part.yaml pin-local ceiling. The quantity the 5 mm ceiling governs — ADC pin-center to cap-center span — was independently measured on exact native geometry at 1.804–4.977 mm across all sixteen pairs (worst: ADC8N 4.977 mm), and the source-owned `<=5.0 mm` test passes. The PASS stamp is correct and there is no release-integrity defect here. No relocation or re-spin is warranted.

### CLAUDE-ADV-004 — DOWNGRADED → **P1 (procedural, narrowed)**
The arithmetic-incompatibility claim is withdrawn: a 222 mA 3.3 V rail load and a 120 mA 12 V bench-input ceiling are not contradictory, because the intervening conversion stage changes current (222 mA × 3.3 V ≈ 0.73 W, well under 120 mA × 12 V ≈ 1.44 W). What survives is procedural and unchanged in urgency: the 120 mA 12 V bench abort ceiling has not been reconciled against the modeled local 12 V input current, and the 3V3_ADC budget is still quoted at 3.3 % headroom with no per-refdes decomposition using datasheet maxima. Publish the reconciliation before first power; the abort threshold must not be raised to obtain a pass. Q3 remains open as acceptance support.

### CLAUDE-ADV-005 — REFUTED as a defect; retained as **P3 (duplicate qualification item)**
The 227 mA precharge calculation stands, but no bound is exceeded. The 0.30 A figure is an explicit steady engineering allocation, and 227 mA is inside it before any device margin is invoked; the AP63205 is rated 2 A continuous with ≥2.5 A high-side peak limit and a 4 ms soft-start, and routing evidence separately declares a 2.5 A transient design bound. The latch-in-slow-charge scenario requires the buck to current-limit at ~8–11 % of its guaranteed capability, which the datasheet excludes. No `R_PRE` change is justified. Instrumented startup with a probe on `5V_LDO_FEED` was already planned qualification, so this item duplicates existing coverage rather than adding a finding.

### CLAUDE-ADV-006 — DOWNGRADED → **P3 (documentation/semantics)**
Retained only as a naming observation. The artifact does not conceal its scope: it explicitly publishes `physical_dcr_status: UNVERIFIED`, `routed_length_status: NOT_GRADED`, `realized_copper: NOT_GRADED`, `capture_identity_status: OWED`, and per-row `why` text naming the owed proofs. A reader consuming the sub-statuses is correctly informed; the only exposure is a downstream consumer reading the top-level `PASS` in isolation. Renaming to `PASS-WITH-OWED` remains a cheap clarity improvement. With ADV-003 refuted, there is no "systematic grading-scope problem" — the grading scope is disclosed and the pin-local ceiling is separately enforced and passing.
