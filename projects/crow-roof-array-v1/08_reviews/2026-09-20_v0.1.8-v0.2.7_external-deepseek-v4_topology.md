# Adversarial Electrical/Topology/Numerical Review — Crow Carrier v0.1.8 and Mic Pod v0.2.7

## Scope

Reviewed against the sealed packet only: carrier/pod schematics, netlists, BOM/CPL, rules, release docs, first-article plans, and verification receipts. Machine receipts were treated as measurements to challenge, not authority.

## Summary

A P0 hardware reset-trigger defect on the carrier means the intended CS5308P reset sequence is never generated. There is also a numeric contradiction in the carrier first-article unloaded input-current limit. Overall design verdict is therefore **DEFECTIVE**, and the current order should be **DO-NOT-ORDER** until corrected and re-reviewed.

## Confirmed findings

### CRW-ADV-001 — P0 — Carrier reset monostable never triggers; ADC never receives mandatory reset pulse

- **Board / file:** `crow-audio-carrier-v1`
- **Refdes / net:** `U_RST2`, `Q_RST1`, `ADC_RESET_N`, `POR_N`, `RESET_PULSE_H`
- **Evidence:**  
  `source/crow_audio_carrier_v1.tsx` and `source/crow_audio_carrier_v1.net` connect:

  ```text
  U_RST2.1 (A)       -> GND
  U_RST2.2 (B)       -> 3V3_ADC
  U_RST2.3 (CLR#)    -> POR_N
  ```

  The selected device is `SN74LVC1G123DCTR`, a retriggerable edge-triggered monostable.
- **Triggering condition:** normal cold power-up. `POR_N` is held low during `3V3_ADC` ramp and then released, causing only a rising edge on `CLR#`.
- **Failure mechanism:**  
  SN74LVC1G123 triggers only on:
  - a rising edge on `B` while `A` is low and `CLR#` is high, or
  - a falling edge on `A` while `B` is high and `CLR#` is high.

  Here `A` is tied low and `B` is tied high. Releasing `CLR#` does **not** trigger a pulse. The `Q` output remains low, so `RESET_PULSE_H` never goes high, `Q_RST1` never turns on, and `ADC_RESET_N` never pulses low.
- **Consequence:**  
  Cirrus DS1314F1 requires `RESET` high during initial power, then a high-to-low pulse >=1 ms, then high. The carrier instead holds `ADC_RESET_N` high continuously. The ADC may not initialize or may operate in an undefined configuration/clock state. This is a fundamental nonfunctional first-article condition.
- **Verification:**  
  The net-census gate passed because it checks topology only. Confirm functionally by:
  1. Powering the carrier at 11.4 V/12.0 V with a scope on `3V3_ADC`, `POR_N`, `RESET_PULSE_H`, and `ADC_RESET_N`.
  2. Observe that `POR_N` releases but no low pulse appears on `ADC_RESET_N`.
  Reproduce in logic: hold `B=1`, `A=0`; step `CLR#` L->H; `Q` remains low.

This should stop the current first-article order.

### CRW-ADV-002 — P1 — Carrier first-article unloaded bench-current limit contradicts source power budget

- **Board / file:** `crow-audio-carrier-v1`
- **Refdes/net:** `12V_PROTECTED`, `5V_BUCK`, `3V3_ADC`, `VMID1_EXT`, `VMID2_EXT`
- **Files:**  
  `verification/design_math.md`, `verification/first_article_test_plan.md`, `03_src/rules/first_article.yaml`
- **Triggering condition:** standard first power-up with no spokes connected.
- **Evidence/calculation:**

  `design_math.md` states:

  > bounded steady estimates are 222.374 mA on shared3V3 (230 mA allocation),  
  > 250.414 mA on local5V (300 mA allocation) and 0.985346 A total with eight  
  > 0.10 A spokes.

  The eight spokes account for 0.8 A. The remaining carrier conversion load is therefore about 0.185 A at the 12 V input.

  Using the rail figures:

  ```text
  Buck output power ≈ 5.15 V × 0.250414 A ≈ 1.29 W
  Buck input current at 11.4 V, η = 0.85
      ≈ 1.29 W / (0.85 × 11.4 V)
      ≈ 0.133 A
  ```

  Even at 12.0 V:

  ```text
  1.29 W / (0.85 × 12.0 V) ≈ 0.127 A
  ```

  The plan and card set the post-startup, spokes-unloaded bench supply current ceiling at **0.120 A**.
- **Consequence:**  
  A healthy board operating at its modeled nominal current would trip the first-article abort rule. The procedure is internally contradictory and can block a sound unit.
- **Verification:**  
  Compute from `first_article.yaml` and `design_math.md`, or measure a known-good simulated load. Revise the card and test plan to match the source current budget before ordering.

## Questions raised by insufficient evidence

### Q-CRW-001 — Q_IN orientation/body-diode isolation not fully demonstrated

`Q_IN` is a `PowerDI3333-8` P-channel reverse-polarity FET with fused drain pins. The CPL places it at `270.0`, but the rotation-human gate does **not** list `Q_IN`. The assembly checklist mentions visual inspection, yet no independent footprint/pad-number review is included in the packet.

- **Why this matters:** a reflection/rotation error would reverse source/drain orientation and defeat reverse-hookup protection without any netlist/Drc failure.
- **Requested evidence:** pin-1/cathode/band and fused-drain pad numbering inspection on the final assembly preview; explicit JLC rotation confirmation.

### Q-CRW-002 — Live JLCPCB allocation remains unresolved despite “PUBLIC SOURCING: CLEAR”

Both order receipts (`pcba_order_receipt.json`) show all rows `INCOMPLETE` and no resolved LCSC rows. The release READMEs correctly note final live-uploader checks, but the public-stock readiness evidence does not establish JLC PCBA allocation.

- **Requested evidence:** the actual pre-payment JLC resolved-BOM echo and rotation/polarity preview, matched to the exact sealed BOM/CPL.

### Q-CRW-003 — Pod first-article order profile is “docs-only” but test plan remains archaic

Pod v0.2.7 is described as a docs-only successor, but multiple source docs still carry older no-order language and the test plan says “no purchases.” Confirm which exact files govern the order decision and ensure the immutable archive contains only reconciled documents.

## Final verdicts

- **design_verdict: DEFECTIVE**

  The carrier reset monostable connection is an actual hardware-logic defect that prevents the required ADC reset pulse. This is not a measurement debt; it is a predictable topology failure.

- **order_verdict: DO-NOT-ORDER**

  The current five-carrier and ten-pod first-article purchase should stop until:
  1. `CRW-ADV-001` is corrected and the generated artifacts are re-reviewed.
  2. `CRW-ADV-002` and `CRW-ADV-003` are resolved.
  3. Live JLC allocation, rotations, and polarity checks are produced for the corrected release.

## Five highest-value first-article measurements after correction

1. **ADC reset sequence:** capture `3V3_ADC`, `POR_N`, `RESET_PULSE_H`, and `ADC_RESET_N`; prove initial high, then a low pulse >=1 ms, then stable high.
2. **Unloaded bench current and rails:** measure actual 12 V input current with spokes unloaded and compare directly with the corrected current card for `12V_PROTECTED`, `5V_BUCK`, `3V3_ADC`, `VMID1_EXT`, and `VMID2_EXT`.
3. **Eight-channel slot/polarity impulse test:** inject one calibrated impulse per spoke and verify pods `4,3,2,1,5,6,7,8` appear in TDM slots `0–7` with correct P/N polarity.
4. **Carrier analog headroom/clipping:** sweep 1.2 Vrms differential at the carrier connectors and measure THD+N, common-mode range, clipping margin, and channel-to-channel matching.
5. **Spoke protection/fault matrix:** with a current-bounded fixture, fault one branch at a time and verify the other seven remain powered and recover; record PPTC/PFET drop and hot four-wire voltage at 0.1 A all ports.
