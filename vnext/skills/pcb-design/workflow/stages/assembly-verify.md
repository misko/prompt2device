# JLC-ASSEMBLY-VERIFY — verify the populated artifact

New acceptance requires [registered 3D evidence](../../procedures/mechanical/3d-verification.md)
for the exact staged population: body/model coverage, source identity, registered
contacts and polarity, board/underside clearance and applicable access/mating
checks. Ship reopened populated STEP, six 3D views, same-camera bare views and
generated coverage/registration/clearance reports. Unsupported model dimensions
remain explicit unresolved claims. Historical envelope-only receipts do not
satisfy this upgrade; the version2 owning adapter and independent review bind
these outputs before admission. Tool tests are not a real-board acceptance.

Local pilot through [assembly tooling](../../procedures/fabrication/assembly-tooling.md).
Explicitly adopt its source-bound model/replay and independent admission gate.
Requires the exact
`fabrication_staged` package. This stage produces `assembly_verified`, not
order authority, a release seal or physical qualification.

Reopen staged bytes independently of the exporter. Require nonzero per-board
coverage: every electrical footprint is accounted for exactly once as factory
populated, consigned, manual, DNP, or a justified non-populated mechanical item.
BOM/CPL identities, reference sets and exclusions must agree with that policy.
Manual/consigned modifications need executable instructions and inspection
criteria; they are not performed merely because the package describes them.

Verify placement positions/datum, side, rotation, polarity and mechanical fit
against independent part/manufacturer geometry. Require registered populated
and bare views, mounted-body coverage, and explicit review of model substitutions
and connector access. A render without the part is not fit evidence.

Retain exact stock/quantity observations and clearly separated order-day holds:
final BOM matching/allocation, vendor orientation previews, stack/process
confirmation and manual/THT handling. No catalog stock observation authorizes
ordering. Known design contradictions block, rather than becoming first-article
tests. Every first-article and deployment obligation remains visible.

The pilot provides deterministic population/identity and envelope/contact checks,
exact-subject independent review admission, replay and stale/missing/contradictory
evidence tests. It supports top-side cardinal geometry and single closed native
line/arc outer contours; 2D tessellation is an overview, not solid clearance proof.
Internal Edge.Cuts cutouts, multiple islands and other unsupported geometry require
an explicit extension, not an invented pass. Dimensioned
envelopes may replace unavailable cosmetic 3D models when actual maker/body/land
evidence and stated limitations support the independent fit judgment. Fix a bad model at its source; do not move
valid copper to fit a derivative model. Next: [release review](release-review.md).
