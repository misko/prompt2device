# Generate a schematic candidate

Use after accepted commission, architecture, sourcing and applicable RF-source
prerequisites, or when an accepted schematic's source changed. Read the
[stage contract](../../workflow/stages/schematic.md) and the generate owner
references listed in [existing-tools](../../bindings/existing-tools.md)
completely before acting. The sibling orchestrator bounds the attempt; this
procedure does not change its retry budget.

Reopen exact brief/interface decisions, part identities and cited source
facts. Choose a bounded source change and declared outputs. Author in the
project's `03_tscircuit/src/` for the standard flow; the pinned project-local
TSX toolchain and full conductor own generation. Do not hand-edit a generated
`.kicad_sch`, `circuit.json` or PDF. The existing narrow structured
Python/s-expression fallback is permissible only when a load-bearing
construct cannot be represented by the supported adapter, with the
unsupported construct and failed supported form recorded. It owes the same
diagnostics, native, semantic and review evidence.

The full project conductor is the source-changed path and may stop at the
schematic review checkpoint. Do not bypass a commissioning hold or a critical
part selection refusal. The reuse conductor consumes a pinned unchanged
schematic only; it is not permission to ignore changed TSX. Consult the owning
driver and script help for the exact project's invocation and resume options.

Inspect the actual generated model for embedded producer errors even if the
build exits zero. Require expected component/net denominators, freshness and
source identities; run the conductor's native conversion, ERC, parity,
net-label survival, electrical closure and sourcing/conditional checks.
Review the PDF as a candidate for functional flow and legibility, but do not
adopt your own readability verdict. Record exact source/output identities,
gate receipts, warnings and open findings for an independent
[review](review.md). A failure enters [repair](repair.md), not acceptance.
